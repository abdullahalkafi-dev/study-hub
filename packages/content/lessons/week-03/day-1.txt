@section math | 2 · Math: right-triangle trigonometry and radians | 60 min

### Similar triangles: the idea behind everything
Take a right triangle and shrink or enlarge it. Every side changes, but the **ratios** between its sides do not. A triangle with sides 3, 4, 5 and one with 6, 8, 10 have the same shape, and 3 ÷ 5 = 6 ÷ 10 = 0.6.

So the ratio of two sides depends only on the **angles**. If we give those ratios names, we can measure heights and distances using nothing but an angle and one side.

### Naming the sides
In a right triangle, pick one of the other angles and call it **θ** (theta). The three sides are named relative to θ:

- **Hypotenuse:** the longest side, opposite the right angle.
- **Opposite:** the side across from θ.
- **Adjacent:** the side next to θ (that is not the hypotenuse).

### The three ratios
EQ: sin θ = opposite ÷ hypotenuse     cos θ = adjacent ÷ hypotenuse     tan θ = opposite ÷ adjacent

A memory trick: **SOH-CAH-TOA**. Sine = Opposite ÷ Hypotenuse, Cosine = Adjacent ÷ Hypotenuse, Tangent = Opposite ÷ Adjacent.

BN: SOH-CAH-TOA মনে রাখো: Sin = Opposite ÷ Hypotenuse, Cos = Adjacent ÷ Hypotenuse, Tan = Opposite ÷ Adjacent। Opposite মানে θ কোণের ঠিক বিপরীত বাহু, Adjacent মানে θ এর পাশের বাহু। কোণ বদলালে opposite আর adjacent এর নামও বদলে যায়, তাই আগে θ ঠিক করো।

EX: A tree's height
1. You stand 20 m from a tree and look at its top at an angle of 35° above the horizontal. How tall is the tree (ignoring your height)?
2. The distance 20 m is **adjacent** to the angle. The tree's height is **opposite**. The ratio linking opposite and adjacent is tan.
3. tan 35° = h ÷ 20, so h = 20 × tan 35° = 20 × 0.7002 = **14.0 m**.
/EX

EX: Finding an angle
1. A ramp rises 3 m over a horizontal distance of 4 m. What is its angle?
2. tan θ = 3 ÷ 4 = 0.75.
3. To go from the ratio back to the angle, use the inverse function **tan⁻¹** on your calculator: θ = tan⁻¹(0.75) = **36.9°**.
/EX

EX: Finding sides
1. A ladder 10 m long leans at 40° to the ground. How high does it reach, and how far is its foot from the wall?
2. Height = opposite: 10 × sin 40° = 10 × 0.6428 = **6.43 m**.
3. Distance from the wall = adjacent: 10 × cos 40° = 10 × 0.7660 = **7.66 m**.
4. Check with Pythagoras: 6.43² + 7.66² = 41.3 + 58.7 = 100 ✓.
/EX

TRAP: Make sure your calculator is in **degree mode** when the question gives degrees, and **radian mode** when it gives radians. This is the number one source of wrong trig answers. Test: sin 30° must give 0.5.

### Special angles from two shapes
Some angles are so common that you should know their exact values. They come from two triangles:

**The 45-45-90 triangle.** Take a square of side 1 and cut it along a diagonal. The diagonal is √2 (Pythagoras). So sin 45° = 1 ÷ √2 = **√2/2**, cos 45° = √2/2 and tan 45° = **1**.

**The 30-60-90 triangle.** Take an equilateral triangle with side 2 and cut it in half down the middle. The base becomes 1 and the height is √(4 − 1) = √3. So the 30° angle has opposite = 1, hypotenuse = 2, adjacent = √3, and the 60° angle has the opposite and adjacent swapped.

| Angle | sin | cos | tan |
| 30° | 1/2 | √3/2 | 1/√3 |
| 45° | √2/2 | √2/2 | 1 |
| 60° | √3/2 | 1/2 | √3 |

Notice sin 30° = cos 60° and sin 60° = cos 30°. This is no accident:

KEY: In a right triangle the two non-right angles add to 90°, and the side opposite one is adjacent to the other. So **sin θ = cos(90° − θ)**. The "co" in cosine stands for "complement".

And from Pythagoras (opposite² + adjacent² = hypotenuse²), dividing by hypotenuse² gives one of the most important facts of the whole course:

EQ: sin²θ + cos²θ = 1

BN: ৩০-৬০-৯০ আর ৪৫-৪৫-৯০ ত্রিভুজ থেকে বিশেষ কোণগুলোর মান বের হয়: sin 30° = ½, sin 45° = √2/2, sin 60° = √3/2। এগুলো মুখস্থ করার দরকার নেই, ত্রিভুজ এঁকে বের করা যায়। আর পীথাগোরাস ÷ hypotenuse² থেকে পাই sin²θ + cos²θ = 1, যা এই কোর্সের সবচেয়ে গুরুত্বপূর্ণ সূত্রগুলোর একটা।

### Radians: the natural angle unit
Degrees were invented by ancient Babylonians who liked the number 360. It is an arbitrary choice. Mathematicians use a unit that comes from the circle itself.

**Definition.** One **radian** is the angle at the centre of a circle that subtends an arc **equal in length to the radius**. Since the full circumference is 2πr, the full circle is **2π radians**.

EQ: 2π rad = 360°     so     π rad = 180°     and     1 rad = 180° ÷ π ≈ 57.3°

**Converting:** degrees → radians: multiply by π ÷ 180. Radians → degrees: multiply by 180 ÷ π.

| Degrees | 0° | 30° | 45° | 60° | 90° | 180° | 270° | 360° |
| Radians | 0 | π/6 | π/4 | π/3 | π/2 | π | 3π/2 | 2π |

Why bother? Because radians make formulas clean:

EQ: arc length s = rθ     sector area A = ½ r² θ     (θ in radians)

EX: Arc and sector
1. A circle of radius 5 cm. A sector has angle 1.2 rad. Arc length: s = 5 × 1.2 = **6 cm**. Area: A = ½ × 25 × 1.2 = **15 cm²**.
2. In degrees you would need extra factors of π ÷ 180. With radians there are none.
/EX

EX: Latitude
1. The Earth's radius is 6371 km. One degree of latitude is π ÷ 180 = 0.01745 rad.
2. Arc length = 6371 × 0.01745 = **111 km**. That is why 1° of latitude is about 111 km everywhere.
/EX

BN: এক রেডিয়ান হলো সেই কোণ, যার চাপের দৈর্ঘ্য ব্যাসার্ধের সমান। পুরো বৃত্ত 2π রেডিয়ান, অর্থাৎ π রেডিয়ান = 180°। রেডিয়ানে লিখলে চাপের দৈর্ঘ্য s = rθ আর সেক্টরের ক্ষেত্রফল ½r²θ খুব পরিষ্কার সূত্র হয়ে যায়। ক্যালকুলাসেও আমরা শুধু রেডিয়ান ব্যবহার করব।

P: A right triangle has legs 6 and 8. Find the hypotenuse, sin θ and cos θ for the angle opposite the side of 6. => Hypotenuse = 10. sin θ = 6/10 = **0.6** and cos θ = 8/10 = **0.8**.
P: Convert 72° to radians and 5π/6 to degrees. => 72° = **2π/5**. 5π/6 = **150°**.
P: Write sin 60°, cos 45° and tan 30° exactly. => **√3/2**, **√2/2**, **1/√3** (that is √3/3).
LVL: Level 2 | Solve triangles
P: A 12 m flagpole casts a shadow of 9 m. Find the angle of elevation of the Sun. => tan θ = 12/9, so θ = **53.1°**.
P: A kite string 50 m long makes an angle of 62° with the ground. How high is the kite? => 50 × sin 62° = 50 × 0.8829 = **44.1 m**.
P: A sector of a circle has radius 10 cm and arc length 15 cm. Find its angle in radians and its area. => θ = 15 ÷ 10 = **1.5 rad**. Area = ½ × 100 × 1.5 = **75 cm²**.
P: Show that sin 60° · cos 30° + cos 60° · sin 30° = 1. => (√3/2)(√3/2) + (1/2)(1/2) = 3/4 + 1/4 = **1** ✓.
LVL: Level 3 | Real
P: From the top of a 30 m cliff, the angle of depression of a boat is 25°. How far is the boat from the foot of the cliff? => The angle of depression equals the angle of elevation from the boat. tan 25° = 30 ÷ d, so d = 30 ÷ 0.4663 = **64.3 m**.
P: A satellite dish is aimed at 40° above the horizon. A tree 6 m tall stands 10 m in front of the dish. Does the tree block the beam from a dish whose centre is 1.5 m above the ground? => The beam is at height 1.5 + 10 × tan 40° = 1.5 + 8.39 = 9.9 m above the ground at the tree's position, so the tree (6 m) **does not block it**.
LVL: Challenge | Exact values
P: Use the 30-60-90 triangle to find the exact value of tan 15°. (Hint: extend one side to make an isosceles triangle.) => Take the triangle with the 30° angle at C: AB = 1 (opposite), BC = √3 (adjacent), AC = 2. Extend BC past C to D so that CD = AC = 2. Triangle ACD is isosceles with apex angle 150°, so its base angles are 15°. In the big right triangle ABD the angle at D is 15°, with opposite 1 and adjacent √3 + 2. So tan 15° = 1 ÷ (2 + √3) = **2 − √3 ≈ 0.268**.

@section physics | 3 · Physics: vectors and their components | 30 min

### One arrow, two numbers
Forces and velocities in the real world rarely point exactly along an axis. A kicked football moves up and forward. A rope pulls at an angle. The trick is to **split** each vector into a horizontal part and a vertical part, and then deal with each direction on its own.

For a vector of size V at angle θ above the horizontal:

EQ: Vₓ = V cos θ     V_y = V sin θ

Why? Draw the vector as the hypotenuse of a right triangle. The horizontal side is adjacent to θ (so V cos θ) and the vertical side is opposite (so V sin θ).

To go back from components to the vector:

EQ: V = √(Vₓ² + V_y²)     and     tan θ = V_y ÷ Vₓ

BN: যেকোনো কোণে থাকা ভেক্টরকে দুটো অংশে ভাঙো: অনুভূমিক Vₓ = V cos θ আর উল্লম্ব V_y = V sin θ। এটা SOH-CAH-TOA এর সরাসরি প্রয়োগ। উল্টো দিকে: V = √(Vₓ² + V_y²) আর tan θ = V_y/Vₓ। এভাবে প্রতিটা দিক আলাদাভাবে সামলানো যায়।

EX: A tilted pull
1. A 50 N force acts at 30° above the horizontal.
2. Horizontal: Fₓ = 50 cos 30° = 50 × 0.866 = **43.3 N**.
3. Vertical: F_y = 50 sin 30° = 50 × 0.5 = **25 N**.
4. Check: √(43.3² + 25²) = √(1875 + 625) = √2500 = 50 ✓.
/EX

EX: Adding two vectors by components
1. A hiker walks 100 m at 30° north of east, then 60 m due north. Find the total displacement.
2. First leg: x = 100 cos 30° = 86.6 m. y = 100 sin 30° = 50 m.
3. Second leg: x = 0 and y = 60 m.
4. Total: x = 86.6 and y = 110. Size = √(86.6² + 110²) = √(7500 + 12100) = **140 m**.
5. Direction: tan θ = 110 ÷ 86.6, so θ = **51.8° north of east**.
/EX

KEY: To add vectors: (1) split each into x and y parts, (2) add all the x parts, add all the y parts, (3) rebuild the resultant with Pythagoras and tan⁻¹.

EX: Pulling a suitcase
1. A 20 kg suitcase is pulled along a floor by a strap with 40 N at 25° above the horizontal.
2. Horizontal component: 40 cos 25° = 36.25 N. Vertical component: 40 sin 25° = 16.9 N upward.
3. The vertical part lifts a little of the weight, so the normal force is N = mg − 16.9 = 196 − 16.9 = **179.1 N**, less than the weight. This is why pulling at an angle can reduce friction.
/EX

### Practice

P: A velocity has components 8 m/s east and 6 m/s north. Find its size and direction. => √(64 + 36) = **10 m/s**. tan θ = 6/8, so **36.9° north of east**.

LVL: Challenge | Equilibrium with an angle
P: A 5 kg sign hangs from two cables at 30° above the horizontal on each side (symmetrical). Find the tension in each cable. => Each cable provides T sin 30° upward. Two cables: 2T sin 30° = T = weight = 49 N. So **T = 49 N** in each cable. (The horizontal parts cancel by symmetry.)
