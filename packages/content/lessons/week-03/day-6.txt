@section math | 2 · Part A: Trigonometry (14 marks, about 55 min) | calculator only where stated

LVL: Part A | Questions 1 to 13
P: (1 mark) Convert 150° to radians. => 150 × π ÷ 180 = **5π/6**.
P: (1 mark) Find the exact value of sin 120° × cos 330°. => sin 120° = √3/2 and cos 330° = √3/2. The product is **3/4**.
P: (1 mark) Solve 2 cos x = −1 for 0 ≤ x ≤ 2π. => cos x = −1/2, which has reference angle π/3, in quadrants II and III. **x = 2π/3, 4π/3**.
P: (1 mark) Simplify sin x ÷ (1 + cos x) + (1 + cos x) ÷ sin x. => Common denominator: (sin²x + (1 + cos x)²) ÷ (sin x (1 + cos x)) = (sin²x + 1 + 2 cos x + cos²x) ÷ (…) = (2 + 2 cos x) ÷ (sin x (1 + cos x)) = **2 ÷ sin x**.
P: (1 mark) If cos A = −5/13 and A is in quadrant II, find sin 2A. => sin A = 12/13 (positive in quadrant II). sin 2A = 2 × (12/13) × (−5/13) = **−120/169**.
P: (1 mark) Solve 2 sin²x − sin x − 1 = 0 for 0 ≤ x ≤ 2π. => Factor: (2 sin x + 1)(sin x − 1) = 0. sin x = 1 gives π/2. sin x = −1/2 gives 7π/6 and 11π/6. **x = π/2, 7π/6, 11π/6**.
P: (1 mark) Write 3 sin x + 4 cos x as R sin(x + α) with α acute, and state its maximum value. => R = √(9 + 16) = 5. R cos α = 3, R sin α = 4, so tan α = 4/3 and α = 53.1°. **5 sin(x + 53.1°)**, with maximum value **5**.
P: (1 mark, calculator) Two sides of a triangle are 7 and 9 with an angle of 50° between them. Find the third side. => c² = 49 + 81 − 2 × 7 × 9 × cos 50° = 130 − 126 × 0.6428 = 130 − 80.99 = 49.01. **c = 7.00**.
P: (1 mark, calculator) In a triangle A = 35°, B = 75° and a = 10. Find b. => b = 10 × sin 75° ÷ sin 35° = 10 × 0.9659 ÷ 0.5736 = **16.8**.
P: (1 mark) Find the area of a triangle with sides 8 and 10 and an included angle of 30°. => ½ × 8 × 10 × sin 30° = 40 × 0.5 = **20**.
P: (1 mark) Find sin(cos⁻¹(5/13)). => Triangle with adjacent 5, hypotenuse 13, so opposite 12. **12/13**.
P: (1 mark) State the amplitude and period of y = 3 sin 2x. => Amplitude **3**, period 2π ÷ 2 = **π**.
P: (2 marks) Solve cos 2x = 1/2 for 0 ≤ x ≤ π. => 2x lies in 0 to 2π. cos θ = 1/2 gives θ = π/3 and 5π/3. So 2x = π/3 or 5π/3, giving **x = π/6, 5π/6**.

@section physics | 3 · Part B: Vectors, projectiles and slopes (6 marks, about 35 min) | calculator allowed

LVL: Part B | Questions 14 to 19
P: (1 mark) A 50 N force acts at 37° above the horizontal. Find its horizontal and vertical components. => 50 cos 37° = **39.9 N**, 50 sin 37° = **30.1 N**.
P: (1 mark) A boat steers straight across a river at 4 m/s relative to the water. The current is 3 m/s. Find its speed relative to the ground and the angle its path makes with the straight-across direction. => √(16 + 9) = **5 m/s**. Angle = tan⁻¹(3/4) = **36.9°** downstream.
P: (1 mark) A ball is thrown horizontally at 15 m/s from a cliff 20 m high (g = 9.8). Find the time in the air and the horizontal distance. => t = √(2 × 20 ÷ 9.8) = **2.02 s**. Distance = 15 × 2.02 = **30.3 m**.
P: (1 mark) A projectile is launched from the ground at 30 m/s at 30° above the horizontal (g = 9.8). Find its time of flight and range. => T = 2 × 30 × sin 30° ÷ 9.8 = **3.06 s**. R = 30² × sin 60° ÷ 9.8 = 779.4 ÷ 9.8 = **79.5 m**.
P: (1 mark) Find the acceleration of a block on a frictionless 40° slope. => g sin 40° = 9.8 × 0.6428 = **6.30 m/s²**.
P: (1 mark) A block slides down a 30° slope with μ_k = 0.25. Find its acceleration. => a = g (sin 30° − 0.25 cos 30°) = 9.8 × (0.5 − 0.2165) = **2.78 m/s²**.

### Challenge of the week
LVL: Challenge | Two angles, one range
P: A projectile launched at speed u from the ground has range R = u² sin 2θ ÷ g. Show that angles θ and 90° − θ give the same range. Then, for u = 20 m/s (g = 9.8), find both angles that give a range of 30 m. => For the angle 90° − θ: sin(2(90° − θ)) = sin(180° − 2θ) = sin 2θ, so the range is identical. ∎ For R = 30: sin 2θ = 30 × 9.8 ÷ 400 = 0.735, so 2θ = 47.3° or 132.7°, giving **θ = 23.6° or 66.4°**. The two angles add up to 90°, as the proof says. The low angle gives a flat, fast shot, and the high angle gives a lob that stays in the air longer.
