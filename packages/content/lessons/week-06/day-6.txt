LVL: Part A | Questions 1 to 13
P: (1 mark) Differentiate (x² + 1)(3x − 2). => 2x (3x − 2) + 3 (x² + 1) = **9x² − 4x + 3**.
P: (1 mark) Differentiate x ÷ (x² + 4). => ((x² + 4) − 2x²) ÷ (x² + 4)² = **(4 − x²) ÷ (x² + 4)²**.
P: (1 mark) Differentiate sin(3x²). => **6x cos(3x²)**.
P: (1 mark) Differentiate e^(−2x) cos x. => −2e^(−2x) cos x − e^(−2x) sin x = **−e^(−2x) (2 cos x + sin x)**.
P: (1 mark) Find d/dx ln(x² + 1) at x = 1. => 2x ÷ (x² + 1) = 2 ÷ 2 = **1**.
P: (1 mark) Differentiate arctan(3x). => **3 ÷ (1 + 9x²)**.
P: (1 mark) Find the slope of the circle x² + y² = 25 at (−3, 4). => y′ = −x ÷ y = **3/4**.
P: (1 mark) Find y′ at (2, 1) for the curve xy + y² = 3. => y + x y′ + 2y y′ = 0, so y′ = −y ÷ (x + 2y) = −1 ÷ 4 = **−1/4**. (Check that (2, 1) is on the curve: 2 + 1 = 3 ✓.)
P: (1 mark) Use logarithmic differentiation to differentiate y = x^(2x). => ln y = 2x ln x, so y′ ÷ y = 2 ln x + 2 and **y′ = x^(2x) (2 ln x + 2)**.
P: (1 mark) Find f″ for f(x) = x eˣ. => f′ = eˣ (1 + x), f″ = **eˣ (2 + x)**.
P: (1 mark) The radius of a circle grows at 3 cm/s. Find dA/dt when r = 4 cm. => 2π r dr/dt = 2π × 4 × 3 = **24π cm²/s** (about 75.4).
P: (1 mark) Find the tangent line to y = ln x at x = e. => y′ = 1/x = 1/e, and y(e) = 1. y − 1 = (1/e)(x − e), so **y = x/e**.
P: (2 marks) Find the minimum value of f(x) = x ln x for x > 0, and show it is a minimum. => f′ = ln x + 1 = 0 gives x = 1/e. f(1/e) = (1/e)(−1) = **−1/e**. f″ = 1/x > 0, so it is a minimum ✓.

@section physics | 3 · Part B: Momentum and collisions (6 marks, about 35 min) | calculator allowed

LVL: Part B | Questions 14 to 19
P: (1 mark) A 0.4 kg ball arrives at 10 m/s and leaves in the opposite direction at 6 m/s. The contact time is 0.02 s. Find the average force. => Δp = 0.4 × (−6 − 10) = −6.4 N·s. F = 6.4 ÷ 0.02 = **320 N**.
P: (1 mark) A 3 kg block at 4 m/s sticks to a 1 kg block at rest. Find the common speed and the kinetic energy lost. => v = 12 ÷ 4 = **3 m/s**. KE before 24 J, after 18 J, lost **6 J**.
P: (1 mark) A 3 kg ball at 5 m/s collides head-on elastically with a 2 kg ball at rest. Find both final velocities. => v₁ = ((3 − 2) × 5) ÷ 5 = **1 m/s**. v₂ = (2 × 3 × 5) ÷ 5 = **6 m/s**. (Check momentum: 15 = 3 + 12 ✓.)
P: (1 mark) Find the centre of mass of 2 kg at (0, 0), 3 kg at (5, 0) and 5 kg at (0, 4). => x = 15 ÷ 10 = 1.5. y = 20 ÷ 10 = 2. **(1.5, 2)**.
P: (1 mark) A rocket of mass 10,000 kg expels 80 kg/s of gas at 2500 m/s. Find the thrust and the launch acceleration. => Thrust = **200,000 N**. a = 200,000 ÷ 10,000 − 9.8 = **10.2 m/s²**.
P: (1 mark) A 0.01 kg bullet at 400 m/s embeds in a 1.99 kg block hanging from strings. Find the height the block rises. => V = 4 ÷ 2 = 2 m/s. h = 4 ÷ 19.6 = **0.204 m**.

### Challenges of the week
LVL: Challenge | Which is larger, e to the π or π to the e?
P: Show that f(x) = (ln x) ÷ x has its maximum at x = e, and use this to decide whether e^π or π^e is larger, without a calculator. => f′ = (1 − ln x) ÷ x², which is zero at x = e, positive before and negative after. So f is decreasing for x > e. Since π > e, f(π) < f(e), so ln π ÷ π < 1 ÷ e. Multiply by e π: e ln π < π, which means ln(π^e) < ln(e^π). So **e^π > π^e** (indeed 23.14 vs 22.46).

LVL: Challenge | The stacked-ball bounce
P: A basketball (0.6 kg) with a tennis ball (0.06 kg) resting on top is dropped from 1 m. The basketball hits the floor first and bounces elastically, then hits the falling tennis ball. Treat the collisions as elastic and take up as positive. How high does the tennis ball go? => Both balls reach the floor at √(2 × 9.8 × 1) = 4.43 m/s. The basketball rebounds at +4.43 and meets the tennis ball still moving at −4.43. For the tennis ball: v = ((m₂ − m₁) u₂ + 2 m₁ u₁) ÷ (m₁ + m₂) = ((0.06 − 0.6)(−4.43) + 2 × 0.6 × 4.43) ÷ 0.66 = (2.392 + 5.316) ÷ 0.66 = **11.7 m/s**. Height = 11.7² ÷ 19.6 = **6.96 m**, nearly 7 times the drop height. The small ball gets most of the energy of the big one.
