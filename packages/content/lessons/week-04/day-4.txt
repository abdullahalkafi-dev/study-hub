# The cross product of vectors. Then: Newton's law of gravitation
lead: The cross product takes two vectors in 3D and returns a third vector perpendicular to both, whose length is the area of the parallelogram they span. It gives planes, torque and rotation. In physics you meet gravity as a force that weakens with the square of distance, which explains weight, the Moon and the planets.

BN: ক্রস গুণফল (cross product) ত্রিমাত্রিক দুটি ভেক্টরকে গুণ করে এমন একটি ভেক্টর দেয় যা দুটোরই লম্ব, আর যার দৈর্ঘ্য ওদের তৈরি সামান্তরিকের ক্ষেত্রফল। এটা দিয়ে তল, টর্ক আর ঘূর্ণন বোঝা যায়। পদার্থবিজ্ঞানে মহাকর্ষ: দূরত্বের বর্গের সঙ্গে দুর্বল হওয়া এক বল, যা ওজন, চাঁদ আর গ্রহের গতি ব্যাখ্যা করে।

@section math | 2 · Math: the cross product | 55 min

### Definition
For vectors a = (a₁, a₂, a₃) and b = (b₁, b₂, b₃):

EQ: a × b = (a₂b₃ − a₃b₂ , a₃b₁ − a₁b₃ , a₁b₂ − a₂b₁)

The pattern is a cycle: for each component, cover that column and take "cross-multiplication" of the other two. A helpful memory aid is the 3×3 determinant with rows (i, j, k), a and b.

Its key facts:
- **Direction:** perpendicular to both a and b, given by the **right-hand rule**. Point your fingers along a, curl them toward b, and your thumb points along a × b.
- **Length:** |a × b| = |a||b| sin θ, which is the **area of the parallelogram** spanned by a and b.
- **Anticommutative:** b × a = −(a × b). The order matters.
- **a × a = 0**, and a × b = 0 exactly when a and b are parallel (sin θ = 0).

Basis check: i × j = k, j × k = i, k × i = j. Reversing the order flips the sign.

**Why the length is |a||b| sin θ.** Computing |a × b|² by expanding gives |a|²|b|² − (a · b)². Using a · b = |a||b| cos θ, this equals |a|²|b|²(1 − cos²θ) = |a|²|b|² sin²θ. ∎

BN: a × b ভেক্টরটি a ও b দুটোরই লম্ব (ডান হাতের নিয়ম), দৈর্ঘ্য |a||b| sin θ = সামান্তরিকের ক্ষেত্রফল। ক্রম বদলালে চিহ্ন উল্টে যায়: b × a = −(a × b)। দুই ভেক্টর সমান্তরাল হলে ফল শূন্য। ডট গুণফল দেয় সংখ্যা, ক্রস গুণফল দেয় ভেক্টর।

EX: Compute
1. a = (1, 2, 3) and b = (4, 5, 6).
2. First: 2 × 6 − 3 × 5 = −3. Second: 3 × 4 − 1 × 6 = 6. Third: 1 × 5 − 2 × 4 = −3.
3. a × b = **(−3, 6, −3)**.
4. Check perpendicularity: a · (a × b) = −3 + 12 − 9 = 0 ✓ and b · (a × b) = −12 + 30 − 18 = 0 ✓.
/EX

EX: Area of a triangle in 3D
1. A(1, 0, 0), B(0, 2, 0), C(0, 0, 3). AB = (−1, 2, 0) and AC = (−1, 0, 3).
2. AB × AC = (2 × 3 − 0 × 0 , 0 × (−1) − (−1) × 3 , (−1) × 0 − 2 × (−1)) = **(6, 3, 2)**.
3. Length = √(36 + 9 + 4) = 7. Area of the triangle = ½ × 7 = **3.5**.
/EX

EX: The equation of a plane
1. A plane through the three points above has normal vector n = (6, 3, 2).
2. The plane is n · (r − A) = 0, so 6(x − 1) + 3y + 2z = 0, so **6x + 3y + 2z = 6**.
3. Check B(0, 2, 0): 6 ✓. Check C(0, 0, 3): 6 ✓.
4. The distance from the origin to the plane is 6 ÷ √(36 + 9 + 4) = **6/7**, the same formula as for a line in 2D.
/EX

EX: Line of intersection of two planes
1. Planes x + y + z = 3 and x − y + 2z = 1 have normals (1, 1, 1) and (1, −1, 2).
2. The line lies in both, so its direction is perpendicular to both normals: (1, 1, 1) × (1, −1, 2) = (1 × 2 − 1 × (−1), 1 × 1 − 1 × 2, 1 × (−1) − 1 × 1) = **(3, −1, −2)**.
3. A point on both: set z = 0, then x + y = 3 and x − y = 1, so (2, 1, 0).
4. The line is **(2, 1, 0) + t (3, −1, −2)**.
/EX

### Volume: the triple product
The volume of the box (parallelepiped) spanned by a, b, c is |a · (b × c)|.

EX: Volume
1. a = (1, 0, 0), b = (1, 2, 0), c = (1, 1, 3). b × c = (6, −3, −1). a · (b × c) = **6**, so the volume is 6.
2. Check: the base area spanned by a and b is 2 and the height is 3, so 2 × 3 = 6 ✓.
/EX

### Torque as a cross product
When you push a wrench of length r with force F, the turning effect (**torque**) is

EQ: τ = r × F     |τ| = r F sin θ

Only the component of the force perpendicular to the lever arm turns it.

EX: Torque
1. A 0.25 m wrench, 80 N force at 60° to the wrench: |τ| = 0.25 × 80 × sin 60° = **17.3 N·m**.
2. Pushing at 90° gives the maximum, 20 N·m. Pushing along the wrench (θ = 0°) gives no torque at all.
/EX

TRAP: The cross product only works in 3D (the 2D version is a single number, the signed area a₁b₂ − a₂b₁). And the cross product is **not commutative** and **not associative**: a × (b × c) ≠ (a × b) × c in general.

@section physics | 3 · Physics: Newton's law of gravitation | 35 min

### The law
Every two masses attract each other along the line joining them, with a force

EQ: F = G M m ÷ r²     G = 6.674 × 10⁻¹¹ N·m²/kg²

Key points:
- **Inverse-square law:** double the distance and the force falls to a quarter.
- The forces on the two bodies are equal and opposite (Newton's third law).
- r is the distance between the **centres** (for spheres).
- G is tiny, so gravity is only noticeable when one body is huge.

BN: নিউটনের মহাকর্ষ সূত্র F = GMm/r²। দূরত্ব দ্বিগুণ হলে বল এক-চতুর্থাংশ। দুই বস্তুর ওপর বল সমান ও বিপরীত। G খুব ছোট, তাই শুধু বিশাল বস্তুর (পৃথিবী, সূর্য) ক্ষেত্রেই এটা চোখে পড়ে।

EX: Two people
1. 70 kg and 60 kg, 1 m apart: F = 6.674 × 10⁻¹¹ × 70 × 60 ÷ 1 = **2.8 × 10⁻⁷ N**. Too small to ever notice.
/EX

EX: The Sun and the Earth
1. M_sun = 1.989 × 10³⁰ kg, M_earth = 5.97 × 10²⁴ kg, r = 1.496 × 10¹¹ m.
2. F = 6.674 × 10⁻¹¹ × 1.989 × 10³⁰ × 5.97 × 10²⁴ ÷ (1.496 × 10¹¹)² = **3.54 × 10²² N**.
/EX

### Weight and g
At the surface, a mass m feels a gravitational force m g, so

EQ: g = G M ÷ R²     g at distance r from the centre: g(r) = G M ÷ r²

This is the **gravitational field strength**: the force per kilogram. For Earth (M = 5.97 × 10²⁴ kg and R = 6.371 × 10⁶ m): g = 6.674 × 10⁻¹¹ × 5.97 × 10²⁴ ÷ (6.371 × 10⁶)² = 3.984 × 10¹⁴ ÷ 4.059 × 10¹³ = **9.82 m/s²**.

Notice that g does not depend on the mass m of the falling object. That is why a feather and a hammer fall together in a vacuum (Week 2).

EX: g on other worlds
1. Moon: M = 7.35 × 10²² kg and R = 1.737 × 10⁶ m, so g = 4.905 × 10¹² ÷ 3.017 × 10¹² = **1.63 m/s²**.
2. Mars: M = 6.42 × 10²³ kg and R = 3.39 × 10⁶ m, so g = 4.285 × 10¹³ ÷ 1.149 × 10¹³ = **3.73 m/s²**.
3. A 70 kg person weighs 70 × 1.63 = **114 N** on the Moon and 686 N on Earth, but the mass stays 70 kg.
/EX

EX: g at height
1. At height h above the surface, r = R + h, so g(h) = g₀ (R ÷ (R + h))².
2. At the altitude of the International Space Station (h = 400 km): g = 9.81 × (6371 ÷ 6771)² = 9.81 × 0.8853 = **8.69 m/s²**.
3. So gravity is still 89% of that at the surface up there. Astronauts float because they are in **free fall**, not because gravity is absent.
/EX

EX: The Moon check
1. At the Moon's distance r = 3.84 × 10⁸ m, g = G M ÷ r² = 3.986 × 10¹⁴ ÷ (3.84 × 10⁸)² = **0.0027 m/s²**.
2. On Tuesday we measured the Moon's acceleration from its orbit and got 0.00272 m/s². The two agree. This was Newton's great test: the same gravity that pulls an apple also holds the Moon in orbit.
/EX

### Superposition
The total gravitational force from several bodies is the **vector sum** of the individual forces. This lets you find points where forces cancel.

EX: A neutral point
1. At what point between the Earth and the Moon do the two pulls cancel? Let x be the distance from the Earth and d = 3.84 × 10⁸ m the separation.
2. G M_E ÷ x² = G M_M ÷ (d − x)², so x ÷ (d − x) = √(M_E ÷ M_M) = √81.2 = 9.01.
3. x = 9.01 d ÷ 10.01 = 0.900 d = **3.46 × 10⁸ m** from the Earth, 90% of the way to the Moon.
/EX

TRAP: Do not mix G and g. **G** is a universal constant (6.67 × 10⁻¹¹). **g** is a local field strength that depends on the planet and the height. Also, r is measured from the **centre**, not from the surface.

NOTES:

### Recap
- Cross product: perpendicular to both vectors, |a × b| = |a||b| sin θ = area of the parallelogram, anticommutative. Uses: plane normal, triangle area, torque τ = r × F, volume via the triple product.
- Gravitation: F = GMm/r², g = GM/R², inverse-square, g(h) = g₀ (R/(R + h))². Weight changes with place, mass does not.

BN: আজকের সারকথা: ক্রস গুণফল দুই ভেক্টরের লম্ব, দৈর্ঘ্য = সামান্তরিকের ক্ষেত্রফল, ক্রম বদলালে চিহ্ন উল্টে যায়। তলের অভিলম্ব, ত্রিভুজের ক্ষেত্রফল, টর্ক τ = r × F। মহাকর্ষ F = GMm/r², g = GM/R²; ওজন স্থানভেদে বদলায়, ভর বদলায় না।
