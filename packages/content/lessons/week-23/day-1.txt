@section math | 2 · Math review: complex numbers and matrices | 45 min

### The map of Weeks 12 to 15
| Idea | Key results |
| Complex numbers | z = a + bi = r e^(iθ). Multiply: r's multiply, angles add. De Moivre: zⁿ = rⁿ e^(inθ). Roots: n of them, spaced 360° ÷ n |
| Matrices | Product row × column. det and inverse. Rank. Gaussian elimination |
| Eigen | A v = λ v. det (A − λ I) = 0. Diagonalisation A = P D P⁻¹, so Aⁿ = P Dⁿ P⁻¹ |
| Applications | Impedance Z = R + i (ωL − 1 ÷ ωC), networks, Fibonacci, vibrations, principal axes |

BN: জটিল সংখ্যা z = r e^(iθ): গুণে r গুণ, কোণ যোগ। ডি-মোভার zⁿ = rⁿ e^(inθ), n-তম মূল n টি। ম্যাট্রিক্স: গুণফল, নির্ণায়ক, বিপরীত, গাউসীয় অপসারণ। আইগেনমান: det(A − λI) = 0, A = PDP⁻¹, Aⁿ = PDⁿP⁻¹।

EX: Complex numbers
1. (3 + 4i)(1 − 2i) = 3 − 6i + 4i − 8i² = **11 − 2i**. And (3 + 4i) ÷ (1 − 2i) = (3 + 4i)(1 + 2i) ÷ 5 = (−5 + 10i) ÷ 5 = **−1 + 2i**.
2. (1 + i)⁸: r = √2, θ = 45°, so r⁸ = 16 and the angle is 360°. The answer is **16**.
3. Roots of z³ = 8: **2** and 2 e^(±2πi/3) = **−1 ± i √3**, spaced 120° on a circle of radius 2.
4. **Impedance:** a series circuit with R = 20 Ω, L = 0.1 H and C = 100 μF at 50 Hz: X_L = 31.4 Ω, X_C = 31.8 Ω, so Z = 20 − 0.42 i Ω and |Z| = **20.004 Ω**. The circuit is almost at resonance (f₀ = 50.3 Hz), so only the resistor limits the current.
/EX

EX: Matrices
1. A = [[4, 1], [2, 3]]: det A = **10**, A⁻¹ = (1 ÷ 10) [[3, −1], [−2, 4]].
2. Eigenvalues: λ² − 7λ + 10 = 0, so **λ = 5 and λ = 2**. For λ = 5: (4 − 5) x + y = 0, so v = **(1, 1)**. For λ = 2: 2x + y = 0, so v = **(1, −2)**. Checks: trace 7 = 5 + 2 ✓, det 10 = 5 × 2 ✓.
3. A⁵ = P D⁵ P⁻¹ = [[2094, 1031], [2062, 1063]]. The big eigenvalue wins: for large n, Aⁿ ≈ 5ⁿ × (a rank-1 matrix).
4. **A linear system:** x + y + z = 6, 2x − y + z = 3, x + 2y − z = 2. Eliminate: add the first and third equations, 2x + 3y = 8; add the second and third, 3x + y = 5. Then x = 1, y = 2, z = 3. The determinant is 7, not zero, so the solution is unique.
5. **Fibonacci:** [[1, 1], [1, 0]]¹⁰ = [[89, 55], [55, 34]], so F₁₀ = **55** and F₁₁ = 89. The eigenvalues (1 ± √5) ÷ 2 give the golden ratio 1.618 as the growth rate.
/EX

TRAP: In complex division multiply by the conjugate of the **denominator**. For roots, give all n of them. Matrix multiplication is not commutative. An eigenvector is never the zero vector, and the eigenvalues of Aⁿ are λⁿ.

@section physics | 3 · Physics review: fluids, heat and thermodynamics | 45 min

### The map of Weeks 12 to 15
| Idea | Key results |
| Fluids at rest | P = P₀ + ρ g h. Buoyancy F = ρ_fluid V g. A floating body displaces its weight |
| Fluids in motion | A v = constant. Bernoulli. Viscosity and Reynolds number |
| Heat | Q = m c ΔT, Q = m L. Conduction Q/t = k A ΔT ÷ L |
| Gases | P V = n R T. Kinetic theory. First law ΔU = Q − W |
| Entropy | ΔS = Q ÷ T. Carnot e = 1 − T_c ÷ T_h. No engine beats Carnot |

BN: স্থির তরল P = P₀ + ρgh, প্লবতা ρVg। গতিশীল তরলে Av ধ্রুব ও বার্নুলি। তাপ Q = mcΔT, Q = mL, পরিবহন kAΔT/L। গ্যাস PV = nRT, প্রথম সূত্র ΔU = Q − W। এনট্রপি ΔS = Q/T, কার্নো e = 1 − T_c/T_h।

EX: Fluids and heat
1. **Flotation:** a block of density 920 kg/m³ floats in water. The submerged fraction is 920 ÷ 1000 = **92%**: this is why an iceberg shows only 8% above the water.
2. **Venturi:** a pipe narrows from 20 cm² to 5 cm². At 1.5 m/s in the wide part, v₂ = 1.5 × 4 = **6 m/s** and the pressure drops by ½ ρ (v₂² − v₁²) = 0.5 × 1000 × (36 − 2.25) = **16,875 Pa**.
3. **Torricelli:** water leaving a hole 1.8 m below the surface has v = √(2 g h) = **5.94 m/s**.
4. **Calorimetry:** 200 g of tea at 90 °C and 50 g of cold milk at 20 °C reach (200 × 90 + 50 × 20) ÷ 250 = **76 °C**.
5. **Latent heat:** 0.1 kg of ice at 0 °C is dropped into 0.5 kg of water at 30 °C. The water can give up 0.5 × 4186 × 30 = 62,790 J to reach 0 °C; melting the ice needs 0.1 × 334,000 = 33,400 J. The rest, 29,390 J, warms all 0.6 kg: ΔT = 29,390 ÷ (0.6 × 4186) = 11.7 K, so the final temperature is **11.7 °C**.
6. **Ideal gas:** one mole at 300 K and 1 atm occupies V = n R T ÷ P = **24.6 L**. Two moles expanding isothermally from 10 L to 20 L at 300 K do W = n R T ln 2 = 2 × 8.314 × 300 × 0.693 = **3458 J** and absorb the same heat (ΔU = 0).
7. **Engine:** between 500 K and 300 K the Carnot limit is **40%**. For 1000 J taken from the hot source the cold sink gets at least 600 J. Entropy: the source loses 1000 ÷ 500 = 2 J/K and the sink gains 600 ÷ 300 = 2 J/K. The total is **0**: reversible. Any real engine dumps more heat and creates entropy.
/EX
