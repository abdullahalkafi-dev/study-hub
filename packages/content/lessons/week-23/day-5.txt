@section math | 2 · Checking by units and size | 20 min

Before the paper, remember how to catch your own errors in 20 seconds:

BN: পেপারের আগে ২০ সেকেন্ডে নিজের ভুল ধরার উপায় মনে করো।

1. **Units.** Does the answer have the right unit? A pressure in N, or a time in V, is wrong immediately.
2. **Size.** A 100 W amplifier gives a few amperes, not 3000 A. A 1 nF capacitor holds nanocoulombs. A power station line current is hundreds of amperes, not millions.
3. **Limits.** What happens as one quantity goes to 0 or ∞? The efficiency must stay between 0 and Carnot; a fraction submerged must be between 0 and 1.
4. **Second route.** Find the same number a different way (energy or momentum, series or parallel).

EX: Three quick sanity checks
1. A proton radius in a 0.4 T field comes out as 5 km. Wrong: the cyclotron radius at 2 × 10⁶ m/s is of order centimetres.
2. A 12 V battery gives 2000 A through a 4 Ω resistor? I = V ÷ R = 3 A. A slip in the power of ten.
3. A Carnot engine between 300 K and 600 K claims 70% efficiency. The limit is 50%.

LVL: Paper 2 | Questions 1 to 10
P: **Q1 (3 marks).** Water flows at 2 m/s in a pipe of diameter 10 cm, which narrows to a diameter of 5 cm. Find the speed in the narrow section, the pressure drop and the flow rate in litres per second (ρ = 1000 kg/m³). => The area falls by 4, so v₂ = **8 m/s** (1 mark). ΔP = ½ ρ (v₂² − v₁²) = 0.5 × 1000 × (64 − 4) = **30,000 Pa** (1 mark). Q = A₁ v₁ = π (0.05)² × 2 = 0.01571 m³/s = **15.7 L/s** (1 mark).
P: **Q2 (3 marks).** A 0.30 kg aluminium block (c = 900 J/(kg K)) at 200 °C is dropped into 1.00 kg of water at 20 °C (c = 4186 J/(kg K)). Find the final temperature. => Heat lost = heat gained: 0.3 × 900 × (200 − T) = 1 × 4186 × (T − 20) (1 mark). 54,000 − 270 T = 4186 T − 83,720 (1 mark). T = 137,720 ÷ 4456 = **30.9 °C** (1 mark).
P: **Q3 (3 marks).** 0.50 mol of an ideal gas at 300 K expands isothermally from 5.0 L to 15.0 L. Find the work done by the gas, the heat absorbed and the entropy change of the gas. => W = n R T ln (V₂ ÷ V₁) = 0.5 × 8.314 × 300 × ln 3 = **1370 J** (1 mark). ΔU = 0, so Q = **1370 J** (1 mark). ΔS = Q ÷ T = 1370 ÷ 300 = **4.57 J/K** (1 mark).
P: **Q4 (3 marks).** A heat engine operates between 600 K and 300 K. It takes 2000 J from the hot source in each cycle and has an efficiency of 30%. Find the work, the Carnot limit and the entropy created per cycle. => W = 0.30 × 2000 = **600 J** (1 mark). Carnot limit = 1 − 300 ÷ 600 = **50%** (1 mark). Heat rejected = 1400 J, so ΔS_total = 1400 ÷ 300 − 2000 ÷ 600 = 4.667 − 3.333 = **+1.33 J/K** (1 mark): positive, as the second law demands for a real engine.
P: **Q5 (3 marks).** A charge of +4 μC is at x = 0 and a charge of −1 μC is at x = 0.6 m. Find the point on the x-axis where the electric field is zero. => Between the charges both fields point the same way, so the zero lies outside, beyond the smaller charge (x > 0.6) (1 mark). Let the distance beyond the −1 μC charge be d: 4 ÷ (0.6 + d)² = 1 ÷ d² (1 mark). Then 2d = 0.6 + d, so d = 0.6 m: **x = 1.2 m** (1 mark).
P: **Q6 (3 marks).** Parallel plates of area 0.020 m² are 0.50 mm apart with a dielectric of κ = 3 and are charged to 200 V. Find the capacitance, the charge and the stored energy. => C = κ ε₀ A ÷ d = 3 × 8.854 × 10⁻¹² × 0.02 ÷ 0.0005 = **1.06 nF** (1 mark). Q = C V = **212 nC** (1 mark). U = ½ C V² = **21.2 μJ** (1 mark).
P: **Q7 (3 marks).** A series RLC circuit has R = 10 Ω, L = 50 mH and C = 20 μF. Find the resonant angular frequency, the resonant frequency and the quality factor. => ω₀ = 1 ÷ √(L C) = 1 ÷ √(10⁻⁶) = **1000 rad/s** (1 mark). f₀ = 1000 ÷ 2π = **159 Hz** (1 mark). Q = ω₀ L ÷ R = 1000 × 0.05 ÷ 10 = **5** (1 mark).
P: **Q8 (3 marks).** An electron moves at 3.0 × 10⁶ m/s perpendicular to a magnetic field of 2.0 mT. Find the radius of its path and its period. => r = m v ÷ (q B) = 9.109 × 10⁻³¹ × 3 × 10⁶ ÷ (1.602 × 10⁻¹⁹ × 2 × 10⁻³) = **8.53 mm** (2 marks). T = 2π m ÷ (q B) = **17.9 ns** (1 mark). (The period does not depend on the speed.)
P: **Q9 (3 marks).** A generator coil has 500 turns of area 0.040 m² in a field of 0.50 T and turns at 3000 rpm. Find the peak EMF, the RMS EMF and the frequency (2 poles). => ω = 3000 × 2π ÷ 60 = 314.2 rad/s. ε_peak = N B A ω = 500 × 0.5 × 0.04 × 314.2 = **3142 V** (1 mark). RMS = 3142 ÷ √2 = **2221 V** (1 mark). f = **50 Hz** (1 mark).
P: **Q10 (3 marks).** A wind farm sends 6.0 MW through a line of total resistance 5.0 Ω at 33 kV. Find the line current and the power lost. Then find the loss if the line voltage were only 11 kV, and the ratio of the two losses. => I = 6 × 10⁶ ÷ 33,000 = **182 A** and the loss is I² R = 182² × 5 = **165 kW** (2.75%) (1 mark for the current, 1 mark for the loss). At 11 kV: I = 545 A and the loss is **1.49 MW** (24.8%), which is **9 times** larger, since (33 ÷ 11)² = 9 (1 mark).

### The wind turbine, from wind to socket
A turbine has blades of length R = 40 m and sits in a wind of 12 m/s.

1. **Wind power (Week 12):** the air of density 1.225 kg/m³ passing through the swept area A = π R² = 5027 m² carries P = ½ ρ A v³ = 0.5 × 1.225 × 5027 × 12³ = **5.32 MW**. Because of v³, a wind 10% faster gives 33% more power; half the speed gives **one eighth**.
2. **Betz limit:** no turbine can take more than 59.3% of this (the air must keep moving), so at most **3.15 MW**. A real turbine, with C_p = 0.45, delivers **2.39 MW** to the shaft.
3. **Rotor (Phase 2):** at 15 rpm the angular speed is 1.571 rad/s and the blade tip moves at ω R = **62.8 m/s**, a tip-speed ratio of 62.8 ÷ 12 = **5.2**.
4. **Gearbox and generator (Week 22):** a 2-pole generator needs 3000 rpm for 50 Hz: gear ratio 3000 ÷ 15 = **200 : 1**. Direct-drive turbines avoid the gearbox by using a generator with many poles, f = p n ÷ 120: at 15 rpm, 50 Hz needs p = 400 poles.
5. **Output:** the generator gives 690 V three-phase. Current for 2.39 MW at power factor 0.95: I = 2.39 × 10⁶ ÷ (√3 × 690 × 0.95) = **2100 A**, which is why a transformer sits at the tower base.
6. **Step-up:** 690 V → 33 kV, turns ratio 33,000 ÷ 690 = **47.8**. The current falls to 2.39 × 10⁶ ÷ (√3 × 33,000 × 0.95) = **44 A**.
7. **To the grid:** 60 turbines in a farm give 143 MW. Then 33 kV → 400 kV at the substation (Day 3 of Week 22) so that the long line loses only a small percentage.
8. **Capacity factor:** the wind is not always 12 m/s. Averaged over a year, a good site gives about 35% of the rated power, so 143 MW of turbines supply on average about 50 MW, enough for roughly 50,000 homes at 1 kW each.

BN: বায়ু টারবাইন: ধাপ ১ বাতাসের ক্ষমতা P = ½ρAv³ = 5.32 MW (v³ এর কারণে গতি অর্ধেক হলে ক্ষমতা আট ভাগের এক ভাগ); ধাপ ২ বেৎস সীমা 59.3%; ধাপ ৩ প্রান্তের গতি ωR; ধাপ ৪ গিয়ারবক্স 200:1 অথবা বহু-মেরু জেনারেটর; ধাপ ৫-৬ 690 V → 33 kV, কারেন্ট 2100 A থেকে 44 A; ধাপ ৭-৮ ট্রান্সমিশন ও ক্যাপাসিটি ফ্যাক্টর।

### Recap
- Fluids: A v = const, ΔP = ½ ρ (v₂² − v₁²). Heat: Q = m c ΔT, W = n R T ln (V₂ ÷ V₁), ΔS = Q ÷ T, e ≤ Carnot.
- E field and capacitors: zero-field points, C = κ ε₀ A ÷ d, U = ½ C V². RLC: ω₀ = 1 ÷ √(LC), Q = ω₀ L ÷ R. Magnetism: r = m v ÷ (q B). Generators: N B A ω. Grid: I² R falls as 1 ÷ V². Wind: P = ½ ρ A v³.

BN: আজকের সারকথা: তরল Av ধ্রুব ও ΔP; তাপ Q = mcΔT, W = nRT ln(V₂/V₁), ΔS = Q/T; ধারক ও RLC; চুম্বকীয় বৃত্ত r = mv/(qB); জেনারেটর NBAω; লাইনের ক্ষতি I²R ∝ 1/V²; বায়ু ক্ষমতা ½ρAv³।
