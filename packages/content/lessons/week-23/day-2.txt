# Review 2: multivariable and vector calculus, and electrostatics
lead: Today you review the calculus of several variables (partial derivatives, the gradient, optimisation, multiple integrals, line integrals, divergence and curl) and the physics that uses it all: Coulomb's law, the electric field, Gauss's law, potential, capacitors and why lightning rods work. The connection is deep: the field is the gradient of the potential, and Gauss's law is the divergence theorem in disguise.

BN: আজ বহুচলক ক্যালকুলাস (আংশিক অন্তরক, গ্র্যাডিয়েন্ট, সর্বোচ্চ-সর্বনিম্ন, বহু-সমাকলন, রেখা-সমাকলন, ডাইভারজেন্স ও কার্ল) এবং তার ব্যবহার: কুলম্বের সূত্র, তড়িৎ ক্ষেত্র, গাউসের সূত্র, বিভব, ধারক ও বজ্রনিরোধক দণ্ড। সংযোগটা গভীর: ক্ষেত্র হলো বিভবের গ্র্যাডিয়েন্ট, আর গাউসের সূত্র হলো ডাইভারজেন্স উপপাদ্যের ছদ্মবেশ।

BN: আংশিক অন্তরক, চেইন নিয়ম, স্পর্শতল। গ্র্যাডিয়েন্ট ∇f, দিকীয় অন্তরক ∇f · u। সর্বোচ্চ-সর্বনিম্ন: ∇f = 0 ও হেসিয়ান; লাগ্রাঞ্জ ∇f = λ∇g। দ্বিমাত্রিক ও ত্রিমাত্রিক সমাকলন (পোলার, নলাকার, গোলীয়)। রেখা-সমাকলন, ডাইভারজেন্স, কার্ল, গ্রিন-গাউস-স্টোকস।

EX: Calculus of several variables
1. **Gradient:** f = x² y + y³, so f_x = 2xy and f_y = x² + 3y². At (1, 2): ∇f = **(4, 13)**. The steepest rate is |∇f| = **13.6**. The rate in the direction (3, 4) ÷ 5 is (12 + 52) ÷ 5 = **12.8**.
2. **Classify:** f = x³ − 3x + y². ∇f = (3x² − 3, 2y) = 0 at (1, 0) and (−1, 0). The Hessian determinant is f_xx f_yy = 6x × 2 = 12x. At (1, 0): 12 > 0 and f_xx = 6 > 0: **minimum**. At (−1, 0): −12 < 0: **saddle**.
3. **Lagrange:** maximise x y with x + 2y = 10. ∇(xy) = (y, x) = λ (1, 2): y = λ and x = 2λ. Then 4λ = 10, λ = 2.5, so **x = 5, y = 2.5** and the maximum is **12.5**.
4. **Double integral:** ∫₀² ∫₀³ x y² dy dx = (∫₀² x dx)(∫₀³ y² dy) = 2 × 9 = **18**.
5. **Polar:** the integral of x² + y² over a disc of radius 2 is ∫₀^{2π} ∫₀² r² · r dr dθ = 2π × 4 = **8π = 25.1**.
6. **Spherical:** the volume of a ball of radius 3 is ∫ r² sin φ dr dφ dθ = (27 ÷ 3) × 2 × 2π = **36π = 113.1**.
7. **Line integral:** F = (y, x) = ∇(xy) is conservative, so from (0, 0) to (2, 3) the integral is 2 × 3 − 0 = **6** on any path.
8. **Div and curl:** F = (x², y², z²) has div F = 2 (x + y + z) = **12** at (1, 2, 3). F = (−y, x, 0) has curl = **(0, 0, 2)**. For the unit circle, ∮ F · dl = 2π = ∬ curl · dA = 2 × π ✓ (Green).
9. **Divergence theorem:** F = (x, y, z) has div = 3; the flux out of a ball of radius 2 is 3 × (4/3) π 2³ = **100.5**.
/EX

TRAP: A critical point is only a candidate: check the Hessian. The determinant test is silent when it is zero. For Lagrange, remember to check the boundary or the second derivative to know whether you found the max or the min. In curvilinear coordinates the **Jacobian** (r, r² sin φ) must be included. A field with curl ≠ 0 is not conservative.

@section physics | 3 · Physics review: electrostatics and capacitors | 45 min

### The map of Weeks 16 to 18
| Idea | Key results |
| Charge and force | Coulomb. Superposition. F = q E |
| Field | Point charge E = k Q ÷ r². Plane σ ÷ 2ε₀. Conductor σ ÷ ε₀. Inside a conductor E = 0 |
| Gauss | ∮ E · dA = Q ÷ ε₀: spheres, lines, planes |
| Potential | V = k Q ÷ r, E = −dV/dx, U = q V |
| Capacitors | C = Q ÷ V, C = ε₀ κ A ÷ d, U = ½ C V², series 1 ÷ C, parallel C |

BN: কুলম্ব F = kq₁q₂/r², ক্ষেত্র E = kQ/r², সমতল σ/2ε₀, পরিবাহীর পৃষ্ঠে σ/ε₀, ভেতরে E = 0। গাউস ∮E·dA = Q/ε₀। বিভব V = kQ/r, E = −dV/dx। ধারক C = Q/V = ε₀κA/d, শক্তি ½CV²।

EX: Electrostatics
1. **Coulomb:** charges of 2 μC and 3 μC, 0.3 m apart: F = 8.99 × 10⁹ × 6 × 10⁻¹² ÷ 0.09 = **0.599 N**.
2. **Plane of charge:** σ = 1 μC/m²: E = σ ÷ 2ε₀ = **56.5 kV/m**.
3. **Potential:** a 1 nC charge at 10 cm gives V = 8.99 × 10⁹ × 10⁻⁹ ÷ 0.1 = **89.9 V**.
4. **Parallel plates:** A = 0.01 m², d = 1 mm: C = ε₀ A ÷ d = **88.5 pF**. At 100 V: Q = 8.85 nC and U = ½ C V² = **0.44 μJ**. A dielectric with κ = 4 makes C four times larger at the same voltage.
5. **Combinations:** 2 μF and 3 μF in series give 1 ÷ (1/2 + 1/3) = **1.2 μF**; in parallel **5 μF**.
6. **Camera flash:** 120 μF at 300 V stores ½ × 120 × 10⁻⁶ × 300² = **5.4 J**; released in 1 ms that is **5.4 kW**.
7. **Lightning rod:** two conducting spheres of radius 10 cm and 1 cm joined by a wire have the same V. Since E at the surface is V ÷ r, the small one has a field **10 times** larger. The sharp tip ionises the air first and leaks charge away quietly.

TRAP: A field is a **vector**: add components, not magnitudes. In series capacitors carry the same **charge**, in parallel the same **voltage**. The field inside a conductor is zero, but the field just outside is σ ÷ ε₀ (twice that of an isolated plane). Energy stored is ½ C V², not C V². Potential is a scalar and is zero only by choice of reference.
