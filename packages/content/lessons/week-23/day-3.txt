# Review 3: differential equations, Fourier, circuits and induction, and the loudspeaker project
lead: Today closes the review with the biggest block: differential equations (first and second order, Laplace, numerical methods), Fourier series and transforms, then the physics of circuits, magnetism and induction. The project at the end ties them together: a loudspeaker is a damped, driven oscillator pushed by a magnetic force, fed through an RLC-style crossover, and analysed by Fourier. If you can follow that project from start to finish, Phase 3 is yours.

BN: আজ সবচেয়ে বড় ব্লক: অবকল সমীকরণ (প্রথম ও দ্বিতীয় ক্রম, ল্যাপলাস, সংখ্যাগত পদ্ধতি), ফুরিয়ে ধারা ও রূপান্তর, তারপর বর্তনী, চুম্বকত্ব ও আবেশ। শেষের প্রজেক্টে সব জোড়া লাগে: লাউডস্পিকার একটি স্যাঁতসেঁতে চালিত দোলক যাকে চৌম্বক বল ঠেলে, যে বর্তনীতে ক্রসওভার ফিল্টার আছে, আর ফুরিয়ে দিয়ে বিশ্লেষণ করা যায়। এই প্রজেক্ট বুঝলে Phase 3 তোমার।

@section math | 2 · Math review: differential equations and Fourier | 45 min

### The map of Weeks 18 to 22
| Idea | Key results |
| First order | Separable. Linear y′ + P y = Q with integrating factor. Logistic growth. Equilibria |
| Second order | y″ + b y′ + c y = 0: real roots (over), repeated (critical), complex (under). Forced: resonance |
| Laplace | y′ → s Y − y(0). Solve algebraically, then invert by partial fractions. H (s) = Y ÷ X |
| Numerical | Euler y + h f, Heun, RK4: error h, h², h⁴ |
| Fourier | Series for periodic signals, bₙ = 4 ÷ nπ for the square wave. Transform for pulses. Parseval |
| Sampling | f_s > 2 f_max, alias f_s − f. DFT bin f_s ÷ N. FFT N log₂ N |

BN: প্রথম ক্রম: পৃথকযোগ্য, রৈখিক (সমাকলন গুণক), লজিস্টিক। দ্বিতীয় ক্রম: মূল বাস্তব (অতি-স্যাঁতসেঁতে), সমান (ক্রান্তিক), জটিল (দোলনযুক্ত); চালিত হলে অনুনাদ। ল্যাপলাস: y′ → sY − y(0), বীজগণিতে সমাধান, আংশিক ভগ্নাংশে ফেরত। সংখ্যাগত: অয়লার, হিউন, RK4। ফুরিয়ে: ধারা, রূপান্তর, পার্সেভাল; স্যাম্পলিং ও FFT।

EX: Differential equations
1. **Linear first order:** y′ + 2y = 6, y (0) = 0. The steady state is 6 ÷ 2 = 3 and the transient e^(−2t): **y = 3 (1 − e^(−2t))**.
2. **Logistic:** carrying capacity K = 1000, rate r = 0.5, P (0) = 100: P (t) = K ÷ (1 + 9 e^(−rt)), so P (4) = 1000 ÷ (1 + 9 e^(−2)) = **451**.
3. **Second order:** y″ + 4y′ + 3y = 0 has roots −1 and −3: overdamped, y = A e^(−t) + B e^(−3t). y″ + 2y′ + 5y = 0 has roots −1 ± 2i: underdamped, y = e^(−t) (A cos 2t + B sin 2t), with damped angular frequency 2.
4. **Laplace:** y′ + 3y = 6, y (0) = 1. Then (s + 3) Y = 1 + 6 ÷ s, so Y = 1 ÷ (s + 3) + 6 ÷ (s (s + 3)) = 2 ÷ s − 1 ÷ (s + 3) and **y = 2 − e^(−3t)**. Checks: y (0) = 1 ✓, y (∞) = 2 = 6 ÷ 3 ✓.
5. **Euler's method:** y′ = −2y, y (0) = 1, h = 0.1. Each step multiplies by 0.8. At t = 1: 0.8¹⁰ = 0.1074 against the true e⁻² = 0.1353, an error of **−20.7%**. Halving h roughly halves the error.
6. **Fourier:** the square wave of height 1 has bₙ = 4 ÷ (nπ), so b₁ = 1.273; the first harmonic holds 8 ÷ π² = 81% of the energy. A pulse of duration τ has its first spectral zero at 1 ÷ τ.
/EX

TRAP: For a linear first-order equation the integrating factor is e^(∫ P dt). Do not forget the initial condition when you invert a Laplace transform. In second-order equations the sign of b² − 4c, not of b, decides between over-, critical and under-damping. Euler's method with a step that is too large can be unstable (here it explodes for h > 1).

### The map of Weeks 19 to 22
| Idea | Key results |
| DC circuits | V = I R, Kirchhoff (junction and loop), P = V I, internal resistance |
| RC, RL | τ = R C, τ = L ÷ R. Charge: 1 − e^(−t/τ) |
| RLC and AC | ω₀ = 1 ÷ √(LC), Q = ω₀ L ÷ R, Z = R + i (ωL − 1 ÷ ωC), V_rms = V₀ ÷ √2 |
| Magnetism | F = q v B, F = B I L, B = μ₀ I ÷ 2π r, solenoid μ₀ n I, torque, motor |
| Induction | ε = −dΦ/dt, ε = B ℓ v, ε = N B A ω sin ωt, transformers, Maxwell, c = f λ |

BN: DC বর্তনী: ওহম, কিরশফ, P = VI। RC: τ = RC, RL: τ = L/R। RLC ও AC: ω₀ = 1/√(LC), Q = ω₀L/R, Z = R + i(ωL − 1/ωC)। চুম্বকত্ব: F = qvB, F = BIL, তারের ক্ষেত্র μ₀I/2πr, সলিনয়েড μ₀nI। আবেশ: ε = −dΦ/dt, Bℓv, জেনারেটর NBAω, ট্রান্সফর্মার, ম্যাক্সওয়েল।

EX: Circuits, magnetism, induction
1. **RC charging:** R = 10 kΩ, C = 100 μF, τ = **1 s**. After 1 s the capacitor has 63.2%, after 3 s 95%.
2. **RL:** L = 0.1 H, R = 20 Ω: τ = **5 ms**.
3. **RLC:** L = 0.1 H, C = 100 μF, R = 20 Ω: ω₀ = 1 ÷ √(LC) = **316 rad/s** (f₀ = 50.3 Hz), Q = ω₀ L ÷ R = **1.58**.
4. **Proton in a field:** v = 2 × 10⁶ m/s, B = 0.4 T: r = m v ÷ (q B) = **5.22 cm**.
5. **Solenoid:** n = 2000 per metre and I = 2.5 A: B = μ₀ n I = **6.28 mT**.
6. **Transformer:** 230 V to 12 V with 1150 turns on the primary: N_s = **60**.
7. **EM wave:** 98 MHz FM: λ = c ÷ f = **3.06 m**.
/EX

TRAP: Time constants: τ = R C but τ = L ÷ R (the inductor opposes the current change). The current in an inductor does not jump. RMS is the peak ÷ √2 only for a sine wave. The force on a charge in a magnetic field does no work. An EMF needs a **changing** flux: a steady field gives no voltage.

@section notes | 4 · Project: the loudspeaker from start to finish | 25 min

NOTES:

### The system
A loudspeaker has a cone of mass m = 20 g on a suspension of stiffness k = 3200 N/m and damping b = 11.3 N s/m, driven by a voice coil of wire length ℓ = 6 m in a magnet field B = 1 T, with resistance R = 8 Ω and inductance L = 0.5 mH.

BN: লাউডস্পিকারের কোন ভর m = 20 g, স্প্রিং k = 3200 N/m, স্যাঁতসেঁতে b = 11.3 N s/m, ভয়েস কয়েলে তারের দৈর্ঘ্য ℓ = 6 m, ক্ষেত্র B = 1 T, R = 8 Ω, L = 0.5 mH।

### Step by step
1. **Mechanics (Week 19):** m x″ + b x′ + k x = F (t). Natural frequency ω₀ = √(k ÷ m) = **400 rad/s** (f₀ = 63.7 Hz). Damping ratio ζ = b ÷ (2√(k m)) = 0.706, so Q = 1 ÷ 2ζ = **0.71** (maximally flat, the classic choice).
2. **Magnetic force (Week 21):** F = B ℓ I = 6 N per ampere of current.
3. **Amplitude (Week 19):** x = F₀ ÷ √((k − m ω²)² + (b ω)²). For F₀ = 6 N (1 A): static 1.875 mm; at ω = 200 rad/s 1.82 mm; at resonance 1.33 mm; at 600 rad/s 0.76 mm; at 800 rad/s 0.46 mm. The cone moves freely up to the resonance, then the response falls: a **low-pass** behaviour.
4. **Electric side (Weeks 20 and 22):** the coil is an RL load; its corner frequency is R ÷ (2π L) = 2546 Hz, above which the current falls. The cone also generates a back EMF B ℓ v = 6 v, which acts as extra damping. A 100 W amplifier into 8 Ω gives V_rms = √(P R) = **28.3 V** and I_rms = **3.54 A**.
5. **Crossover (Week 20):** a woofer–tweeter split at 2 kHz with R = 8 Ω needs L = R ÷ (2π f_c) = **0.64 mH** and C = 1 ÷ (2π f_c R) = **9.95 μF**. A first-order low-pass has the gain 1 ÷ √(1 + (f ÷ f_c)²): 0.97 at 500 Hz, 0.80 at 1.5 kHz, 0.62 at 2.5 kHz.
6. **Fourier (Weeks 21 and 22):** a square wave of 500 Hz has harmonics at 1.5 kHz (1/3), 2.5 kHz (1/5), and so on. The woofer passes the fundamental and the tweeter takes the rest: this is exactly what the filter does to the spectrum.
7. **Sound level (Week 10):** if 1 W of acoustic power spreads over a hemisphere at 1 m, I = 1 ÷ (2π) = 0.159 W/m², so the level is 10 log₁₀ (0.159 ÷ 10⁻¹²) = **112 dB**. (A 1% efficient speaker needs 100 W for that.)

### Recap
- ODE tools: separable, integrating factor, characteristic roots, Laplace, Euler. Fourier: series, transform, Parseval, sampling.
- Circuit times: τ = R C, L ÷ R. Resonance ω₀ = 1 ÷ √(LC), Q = ω₀ L ÷ R. Forces: q v B, B I L. Induction: −dΦ/dt. A speaker joins all of them.
