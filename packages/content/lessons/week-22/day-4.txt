# The fast Fourier transform and windows. Then: Maxwell's equations and the displacement current
lead: The DFT of Day 3 takes N × N multiplications, too slow for real signals. The fast Fourier transform (FFT) computes exactly the same numbers with about N log₂ N operations, which for a million points is a speed-up of fifty thousand times. It is one of the most important algorithms ever found. But a real recording has a start and an end, and cutting it abruptly smears every tone: the cure is a window. In physics, we finally write all of electricity and magnetism in four equations, and discover that Ampère's law from Week 21 was incomplete: a changing electric field acts like a current.

BN: Day 3 এর DFT তে N × N গুণ লাগে, বাস্তব সংকেতের জন্য ধীর। FFT একই উত্তর পায় প্রায় N log₂ N ধাপে: দশ লাখ বিন্দুতে পঞ্চাশ হাজার গুণ দ্রুত। কিন্তু বাস্তব রেকর্ডিং হঠাৎ শুরু আর শেষ হয়, তাই প্রতিটি সুর ছড়িয়ে যায়: সমাধান window। পদার্থবিজ্ঞানে বিদ্যুৎ-চুম্বকত্ব চারটি সমীকরণে লিখব, আর দেখব Week 21 এর Ampère সূত্র অসম্পূর্ণ ছিল: পরিবর্তনশীল তড়িৎ ক্ষেত্র কারেন্টের মতো কাজ করে।

@section math | 2 · Math: the FFT and windows | 55 min

### Why the DFT can be made fast
The DFT sum X_k = Σ x_n e^(−2π i k n ÷ N) has N terms for each of N values of k: **N² operations**. The FFT notices that a DFT of size N can be built from **two DFTs of size N ÷ 2** (the even-numbered samples and the odd-numbered ones) plus N ÷ 2 "butterfly" combinations:

EQ: X_k = E_k + e^(−2π i k ÷ N) O_k,   X_(k+N/2) = E_k − e^(−2π i k ÷ N) O_k

Repeat the splitting log₂ N times (N a power of two): the cost is **(N ÷ 2) log₂ N** complex multiplications, in total about N log₂ N operations. The result is the same, only faster.

BN: DFT তে প্রতিটি k এর জন্য N পদ: মোট N² ধাপ। FFT লক্ষ করে N আকারের DFT কে ভাঙা যায় দুটি N/2 আকারের DFT তে (জোড় নমুনা E, বিজোড় O) এবং বাটারফ্লাই যোগে: X_k = E_k + e^(−2πik/N) O_k, X_(k+N/2) = E_k − e^(−2πik/N) O_k। log₂N বার ভাঙলে খরচ প্রায় N log₂ N।

EX: How much faster?
1. **N = 1024:** N² = 1,048,576 against N log₂ N = 10,240: the FFT is **102 times** faster.
2. **N = 2²⁰ ≈ 10⁶** (a 24 s recording at 44.1 kHz): N² = 1.1 × 10¹² operations, which at 10⁹ operations per second takes **1100 s ≈ 18 minutes**. The FFT needs 2.1 × 10⁷ operations: **21 ms**. The speed-up is N ÷ log₂ N = **52,400 times**. Without the FFT, no phone could analyse a voice in real time.
3. **A tiny example (N = 4):** x = (1, 2, 3, 4). Even samples (1, 3) have the DFT (4, −2), odd samples (2, 4) have (6, −2). Then X₀ = 4 + 6 = **10**, X₂ = 4 − 6 = **−2**. For k = 1: the factor e^(−iπ/2) = −i, so X₁ = −2 + (−i)(−2) = **−2 + 2i**, X₃ = −2 − 2i. Check: X₀ is the sum of the samples, 10 ✓.

### Windows: why a tone smears
A real record is cut out of a longer signal, which is the same as multiplying it by a rectangular window. By the convolution theorem (Day 3), the spectrum of the cut piece is the true spectrum **convolved** with the window's spectrum. The rectangular window has a sinc spectrum: a narrow main lobe, but side lobes only **13 dB** below the peak, so a strong tone hides a weak neighbour. A smooth window such as Hann, w_n = 0.5 (1 − cos 2π n ÷ N), trades a wider main lobe for much lower side lobes:

| Window | Main lobe half-width | Highest side lobe |
| Rectangular | 1 bin | −13 dB |
| Hann | 2 bins | −31 dB |
| Hamming | 2 bins | −43 dB |

A smooth window loses amplitude (for Hann the average gain is 0.5, so we divide to correct), but we gain the ability to see weak tones. **Zero-padding** (adding zeros) makes the plot smoother but does **not** improve the resolution. Only a longer record does.

BN: রেকর্ড কাটা মানে আয়তাকার উইন্ডো দিয়ে গুণ; কনভোলিউশন উপপাদ্য অনুযায়ী বর্ণালী উইন্ডোর sinc বর্ণালীর সাথে মেশে। আয়তাকারের পাশের শিখর মাত্র −13 dB, তাই জোরালো সুর দুর্বল প্রতিবেশীকে ঢেকে দেয়। Hann (−31 dB) আর Hamming (−43 dB) প্রধান শিখর চওড়া করে কিন্তু পাশের শিখর অনেক কমায়। শূন্য যোগ করলে ছবি মসৃণ হয়, রেজোলিউশন বাড়ে না; তার জন্য লম্বা রেকর্ড দরকার।

EX: Resolving two tones
1. Two tones at 1000 Hz and 1100 Hz, f_s = 44.1 kHz. With a rectangular window the bin spacing must be below the 100 Hz separation, so N ≥ 44100 ÷ 100 = **441** samples (10 ms). The Hann window has a main lobe half-width of 2 bins, so for the same separation we want a bin spacing of 50 Hz: N ≥ **882** samples (20 ms).
2. A 0 dB tone and one 40 dB weaker (amplitude 1%) 5 bins away: the rectangular window leaves a side lobe about −25 dB at 5 bins, which **hides** the weak tone. Hann there is below −50 dB, so the weak tone is visible.
3. Amplitude correction: a tone of true amplitude 1 under a Hann window gives a peak 0.5 × N ÷ 2 instead of N ÷ 2. Divide by 0.5 to recover the amplitude.

TRAP: The FFT gives exactly the DFT. It is faster, not more accurate and not finer in resolution. Windows trade **resolution for dynamic range**: you cannot have both. Zero-padding is interpolation, not new information. The split in the FFT needs N to be a power of two (or other factorable numbers).

@section physics | 3 · Physics: Maxwell's equations part 1 | 35 min

### Four equations
Everything in classical electricity, magnetism and light follows from four laws, here in the integral form.

1. **Gauss (electric):** ∮ E · dA = Q ÷ ε₀. Electric field lines start on charges. For a point charge, E = Q ÷ (4π ε₀ r²). For 1 nC at 3 cm: E = 8.99 × 10⁹ × 10⁻⁹ ÷ (0.03)² = **9990 V/m**.
2. **Gauss (magnetic):** ∮ B · dA = 0. There are no magnetic charges: field lines never start or end.
3. **Faraday:** ∮ E · dl = −dΦ_B/dt. A changing magnetic flux makes an electric field (Weeks 21 and 22).
4. **Ampère-Maxwell:** ∮ B · dl = μ₀ (I + ε₀ dΦ_E/dt).

BN: চারটি সূত্র: (১) গাউস (তড়িৎ) ∮E·dA = Q/ε₀, (২) গাউস (চুম্বক) ∮B·dA = 0, (৩) ফ্যারাডে ∮E·dl = −dΦ_B/dt, (৪) অ্যাম্পিয়ার-ম্যাক্সওয়েল ∮B·dl = μ₀(I + ε₀ dΦ_E/dt)। শেষ পদটি ম্যাক্সওয়েলের আবিষ্কার: পরিবর্তনশীল তড়িৎ ক্ষেত্র কারেন্টের মতো চৌম্বক ক্ষেত্র তৈরি করে।

### The displacement current
Ampère's law μ₀ I has a flaw. Charge a capacitor with a current I. Take a loop around the wire: the surface that is a flat disc cut by the wire gives μ₀ I. Take the same loop but a surface shaped like a bag that passes **between the plates**: no wire passes through it, so I = 0. The same loop gives two answers. Maxwell repaired it: between the plates the electric flux changes, and

EQ: I_d = ε₀ dΦ_E/dt

For a plate area A, E = Q ÷ (ε₀ A), so ε₀ A dE/dt = dQ/dt = I. The displacement current is **equal to the conduction current**, and both answers agree. And now the surprise: a changing E makes a B, a changing B makes an E (Faraday), so the two fields can sustain each other in empty space: a **wave**, with speed

EQ: c = 1 ÷ √(μ₀ ε₀) = 3.00 × 10⁸ m/s

Maxwell computed this number, saw it equal the measured speed of light, and concluded that light is an electromagnetic wave. Tomorrow we finish the story.

BN: অ্যাম্পিয়ারের সূত্রে একটি ত্রুটি: ক্যাপাসিটর চার্জ করার সময় একই লুপের জন্য তারের মধ্য দিয়ে তল নিলে μ₀I, প্লেটের মাঝ দিয়ে তল নিলে 0। ম্যাক্সওয়েলের সংশোধন: I_d = ε₀ dΦ_E/dt, যা ঠিক I এর সমান। তারপর চমক: পরিবর্তনশীল E থেকে B, পরিবর্তনশীল B থেকে E, ফলে শূন্যস্থানে তরঙ্গ, গতি c = 1/√(μ₀ε₀) = 3 × 10⁸ m/s। আলো একটি তড়িৎ-চৌম্বক তরঙ্গ।

EX: Charging a capacitor
1. Circular plates of radius R = 5 cm (A = 7.85 × 10⁻³ m²) charged by I = 2 A. The field changes at dE/dt = I ÷ (ε₀ A) = 2 ÷ (8.854 × 10⁻¹² × 7.85 × 10⁻³) = **2.88 × 10¹³ V/(m s)**. A huge rate.
2. **Magnetic field between the plates.** For a loop of radius r < R, the displacement current is I (r² ÷ R²), so B = μ₀ I r ÷ (2π R²). At r = 3 cm: B = 2 × 10⁻⁷ × 2 × 0.03 ÷ 0.0025 = **4.8 μT**. At the edge, r = R: **8.0 μT**.
3. **Outside** (r > R): the same as for a wire, B = μ₀ I ÷ (2π r). At r = 10 cm: **4.0 μT**. The two formulas agree at r = R (8.0 μT).
4. **The speed of light:** with μ₀ = 4π × 10⁻⁷ and ε₀ = 8.854 × 10⁻¹², c = 1 ÷ √(1.2566 × 10⁻⁶ × 8.854 × 10⁻¹²) = **2.998 × 10⁸ m/s** ✓.

TRAP: The displacement current is **not** a flow of charge: it is a changing electric flux. It makes a magnetic field like a current, but it does not heat a wire. Faraday and Ampère-Maxwell look alike but the sign differs: Faraday has −, Ampère-Maxwell has +. The total magnetic flux through any closed surface is always 0.
