# Convolution, sampling and the discrete Fourier transform. Then: transformers and the power grid
lead: Two ideas turn the Fourier transform into the engine of modern technology. The first is the convolution theorem: what a system does to a signal in time (a messy integral) becomes a plain multiplication in frequency. The second is sampling: a computer only sees a signal at separate instants, so we need to know when no information is lost (Nyquist) and what goes wrong when we are careless (aliasing). The physics half is the transformer, the device that lets a power station send 100 MW across a country with small losses.

BN: দুটি ধারণা ফুরিয়ে রূপান্তরকে আধুনিক প্রযুক্তির ইঞ্জিন বানিয়েছে। প্রথমটি কনভোলিউশন উপপাদ্য: সময়ে জটিল ইন্টিগ্রাল, কম্পাঙ্কে সাধারণ গুণ। দ্বিতীয়টি স্যাম্পলিং: কম্পিউটার সংকেত দেখে আলাদা আলাদা মুহূর্তে, তাই জানতে হবে কখন কিছু হারায় না (Nyquist) আর অসাবধান হলে কী ভুল হয় (aliasing)। পদার্থবিজ্ঞানে ট্রান্সফর্মার: যে যন্ত্রের কারণে ১০০ MW বিদ্যুৎ কম ক্ষতিতে সারা দেশে পাঠানো যায়।

@section math | 2 · Math: convolution and sampling | 55 min

### Convolution
For a linear, time-invariant system the output is the **convolution** of the input with the impulse response h:

EQ: y (t) = (h * x)(t) = ∫ h (τ) x (t − τ) dτ

Read it as: every instant of the input sends out its own copy of h, delayed and scaled, and the output is the sum of all those copies. It is the same idea as the Laplace "impulse response" of Week 20.

**Convolution theorem:** the Fourier transform of h * x is the product **H (ω) X (ω)**. Hard integral in time, one multiplication in frequency. This is why filters are designed in the frequency picture.

BN: রৈখিক সিস্টেমে আউটপুট y = h * x = ∫ h(τ) x(t − τ) dτ: ইনপুটের প্রতিটি মুহূর্ত h এর একটি দেরি করা, স্কেল করা কপি পাঠায়, সব কপি যোগ হয়। উপপাদ্য: (h * x) এর ফুরিয়ে রূপান্তর = H(ω) X(ω)। সময়ে কঠিন ইন্টিগ্রাল, কম্পাঙ্কে একটি গুণ।

EX: Convolution in action
1. **Two equal pulses.** Convolving a rectangular pulse of width 1 ms with itself gives a **triangle** of base 2 ms and peak height 1 ms (the area of the overlap is greatest when they coincide). Its spectrum is the square of the pulse spectrum: the first zero stays at **1 kHz** but the side lobes fall much faster (sinc²). Smoothing in time means the high frequencies are cut.
2. **An RC filter.** h (t) = (1 ÷ RC) e^(−t/RC). For a step input, y (t) = 1 − e^(−t/RC). With RC = 1 ms, after 1 ms y = **0.632**, after 3 ms y = **0.950**.
3. **An echo.** The signal plus a copy delayed by T = 1 ms at 50% strength is y = x (t) + 0.5 x (t − T). Its transfer function is H = 1 + 0.5 e^(−iωT), with the gain |H| = √(1.25 + cos 2π f T). The gain is **1.5** (+3.5 dB) at f = 0, 1, 2 … kHz and **0.5** (−6.0 dB) at 0.5, 1.5, 2.5 … kHz: a **comb** with spacing 1 ÷ T = 1 kHz. This is the colour that a hard reflecting wall adds to a voice.

### Sampling
A computer measures x (t) at the instants t = n ÷ f_s, where f_s is the **sampling frequency**. In frequency terms, sampling makes **copies of the spectrum at every multiple of f_s**. If the signal contains frequencies up to f_max, the copies do not overlap provided

EQ: f_s > 2 f_max   (the Nyquist condition)

then the original can be rebuilt exactly. If not, the copies overlap and a tone at f appears at a wrong, lower frequency, the **alias**. For f between f_s ÷ 2 and f_s the alias is **f_s − f**.

BN: নমুনা নেওয়ার মানে কম্পাঙ্ক বর্ণালী f_s এর প্রতিটি গুণিতকে কপি হয়। কপি না মিললে f_s > 2 f_max (Nyquist) এবং মূল সংকেত পুরো ফিরে পাওয়া যায়। না হলে f কম্পাঙ্ক ভুল নিচু কম্পাঙ্কে দেখায়: alias = f_s − f (f_s/2 < f < f_s হলে)।

EX: Sampling and aliasing
1. **CD audio:** hearing reaches 20 kHz, so f_s > 40 kHz. The CD uses **44.1 kHz** (a small margin so the anti-alias filter is not impossibly sharp).
2. **A tone of 30 kHz** sampled at 44.1 kHz appears at 44.1 − 30 = **14.1 kHz**, a perfectly audible tone that was never in the room. That is why a recorder always puts a low-pass filter (anti-alias) before the sampler. Ultrasound at 25 kHz would likewise appear at **19.1 kHz**.
3. **7 kHz sampled at 10 kHz:** since 7 > 5, the alias is 10 − 7 = **3 kHz**.
4. **The wagon-wheel effect:** a film runs at 24 frames per second. A wheel with 8 spokes (spacing 45°) turns at 3 rev/s, so each frame it turns 45° exactly: the spokes look **frozen**. At 3.1 rev/s the wheel turns 46.5° per frame, which looks like only **1.5° forward** per frame, 36° per second: it seems to creep forward at 0.1 rev/s. At 2.9 rev/s it seems to creep **backwards**.

### The discrete Fourier transform (DFT)
For N samples x₀ … x_(N−1), the DFT is

EQ: X_k = Σ x_n e^(−2π i k n ÷ N),  k = 0 … N − 1

Bin k corresponds to the frequency **k f_s ÷ N**, so the spacing between bins is f_s ÷ N and a window of N samples lasts N ÷ f_s. To see finer detail in frequency you need to record **longer**. Examples:
- A single pulse (x₀ = 1, the rest 0) has X_k = 1 for all k: a flat spectrum.
- N = 8, x_n = cos (2π n ÷ 8): the energy is in bins 1 and 7, with **X₁ = X₇ = N ÷ 2 = 4**, all other bins 0.
- N = 1024 at f_s = 44.1 kHz: bin spacing **43.07 Hz**, window **23.2 ms**.

If the tone is not exactly on a bin, it leaks into the neighbours: the leakage curve is |sin (π δ) ÷ (π δ)| where δ is the distance in bins. The cure is a smooth window (tomorrow).

BN: DFT: X_k = Σ x_n e^(−2πikn/N)। বিন k এর কম্পাঙ্ক k f_s / N, বিনের দূরত্ব f_s/N, উইন্ডো N/f_s সময়ের। সূক্ষ্ম বিস্তারিত দেখতে হলে বেশি সময় রেকর্ড করতে হবে। সুর যদি বিনে না পড়ে তাহলে পাশের বিনে ছড়িয়ে যায় (leakage)।

TRAP: Nyquist needs f_s **greater than** twice the highest frequency (not equal). Aliasing cannot be repaired after sampling: filter **before**. Bin spacing depends on f_s ÷ N: raising f_s alone makes the resolution worse. The convolution theorem is for the **whole** product: |H X| = |H| |X| but phases add.

@section physics | 3 · Physics: transformers and the grid | 35 min

### The ideal transformer
Two coils on one iron core. A changing current in the primary makes a changing flux Φ in the core, and the same flux links the secondary. Per turn the EMF is dΦ/dt in both, so

EQ: V_s ÷ V_p = N_s ÷ N_p   and, for no loss,   V_p I_p = V_s I_s

Raise the voltage and the current falls by the same factor. Only alternating current works, since only a changing flux gives an EMF. For two coils not tightly coupled, the **mutual inductance** M describes it: ε_s = −M dI_p/dt, with M = k √(L₁ L₂) and 0 ≤ k ≤ 1 (k is the coupling).

**Eddy currents:** the changing flux also drives circles of current inside the iron itself, which heat it. The core is built of thin insulated **laminations**; the loss is proportional to the thickness squared, so halving the thickness cuts it to **1/4**; 0.5 mm instead of 2 mm gives **1/16**.

BN: আদর্শ ট্রান্সফর্মার: V_s/V_p = N_s/N_p আর ক্ষতিহীন হলে V_p I_p = V_s I_s। ভোল্টেজ বাড়ালে কারেন্ট একই অনুপাতে কমে। শুধু AC চলে। পারস্পরিক ইন্ডাকট্যান্স M = k√(L₁L₂)। লোহার মধ্যে এডি কারেন্ট তাপ তৈরি করে, তাই কোর পাতলা পাত (lamination) দিয়ে তৈরি: ক্ষতি পুরুত্বের বর্গের সমানুপাতিক।

EX: Transformers and the grid
1. **A phone-charger style step-down:** 230 V to 12 V with 1150 turns on the primary: N_s = 1150 × 12 ÷ 230 = **60 turns**.
2. **Currents:** a 24 W load draws I_s = 24 ÷ 12 = **2 A**, so the ideal primary current is 24 ÷ 230 = **0.104 A**. With 95% efficiency the input is 25.26 W and I_p = **0.110 A**.
3. **Mutual inductance:** L₁ = 0.2 H, L₂ = 0.05 H, k = 0.9: M = 0.9 √0.01 = **0.09 H**. If the primary current changes at 100 A/s, the secondary EMF is **9 V**.
4. **The reason for high-voltage transmission.** The power station sends P = 100 MW through a line of total resistance R = 10 Ω. The loss is I² R with I = P ÷ V:
   - at **11 kV**: I = 9091 A, loss **826 MW**, more than we send. Impossible.
   - at **132 kV**: I = 758 A, loss **5.74 MW** (5.7%).
   - at **400 kV**: I = 250 A, loss **0.625 MW** (0.6%).
   Raising V by a factor 36 (11 to 400 kV) cuts the loss by 36² = **1300 times**. A step-up transformer with N_s ÷ N_p = 36.4 does this at the power station, and step-down transformers bring it back to 230 V at the home.

TRAP: A transformer does not make energy: raising voltage lowers current. The ratio applies to voltage and turns, but currents go the **opposite** way. A battery (DC) does not work in a transformer. The loss is I² R, not V² ÷ R, and the V in P = V I is the line voltage, not the small voltage drop along the wire.
