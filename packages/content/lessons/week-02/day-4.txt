@section math | 2 · Math: logarithms | 60 min

### The problem we cannot solve yet
Suppose a bacteria colony doubles every hour. After x hours there are 2ˣ times as many bacteria as you started with. If x = 3, the colony is 2³ = 8 times bigger.

Now ask the question in reverse. **How many hours until the colony is 8 times bigger?** You can answer it in your head: 3. You solved 2ˣ = 8.

Now try 2ˣ = 10. Since 2³ = 8 and 2⁴ = 16, the answer lies between 3 and 4. But what *is* it? Guessing is slow, and none of the tools you have (adding, multiplying, powers, roots) can pull an exponent out of an equation.

We have been here before. Squaring has an undo button, the square root. Multiplying by 5 has dividing by 5. Mathematicians gave the same help to exponents and named it the **logarithm**.

BN: ধরো ব্যাকটেরিয়া প্রতি ঘণ্টায় ২ গুণ হয়। ৩ ঘণ্টা পর হবে 2³ = 8 গুণ, এটা তুমি আগেই পারো। কিন্তু প্রশ্নটা উল্টে দিলে? "কত ঘণ্টায় ১০ গুণ হবে?" তখন সমীকরণ 2ˣ = 10। এই x বের করার কোনো উপায় আমাদের হাতে এখনো নেই। বর্গ করার উল্টো কাজ যেমন বর্গমূল, তেমনি ঘাত বের করার উল্টো টুলের নাম লগারিদম।

KEY: A logarithm is an exponent. `log_b(y)` means: "What power do I raise b to, to get y?"

### The definition, said slowly
EQ: log_b(y) = x   means exactly the same as   b^x = y

Read the left side aloud as a question: "log base 2 of 8" is "to what power must I raise 2 to get 8?" The answer is 3, so log₂(8) = 3. The two statements below say the same thing, once in exponent form and once in log form.

EQ: 2³ = 8  ⟺  log₂(8) = 3

The three parts keep their jobs. **b** is the base, the number being multiplied. **y** is the result. **x** is the exponent, and it is what the log gives back.

BN: log₂(8) মানে প্রশ্ন: "2 কে কত ঘাত দিলে 8 হয়?" উত্তর 3। তাই log₂(8) = 3। মনে রাখার সূত্র: লগের **উত্তরটা** সবসময় একটা **ঘাত (exponent)**। base আর ফলাফল থাকে লগের ভেতরে, ঘাতটা থাকে উত্তরে।

TRAP: If you ever get stuck on a log, rewrite it as an exponent. Every time. Do not try to "do" the log directly. Turn log_b(y) = x into b^x = y, and you are back in territory you already know.

BN: আটকে গেলে লগকে ঘাতের আকারে লিখে ফেলো। log_b(y) = x মানেই b^x = y। এটা করলেই অর্ধেক সমস্যা শেষ।

### Worked examples, one move at a time
EX: log₅(125)
1. Say it as a question: to what power must 5 be raised to give 125?
2. Count: 5¹ = 5, 5² = 25, 5³ = 125.
3. So log₅(125) = **3**.
/EX

EX: log₂(1/8), with a negative answer
1. Ask: 2 to what power gives 1/8?
2. From the warm-up, 2⁻³ = 1/8.
3. So log₂(1/8) = **−3**. Logs can be negative. The log of a number between 0 and 1 is always negative, because you need a negative exponent to shrink something.
/EX

EX: log₉(3), with a fractional answer
1. Ask: 9 to what power gives 3?
2. A power that turns 9 into its square root. The exponent 1/2 means the square root, and √9 = 3.
3. So log₉(3) = **1/2**.
/EX

### Four facts that follow, and why each is true
| Fact | Why (read it as a question) |
| log_b(1) = 0 | b to what power gives 1? Any number to the power 0 is 1. |
| log_b(b) = 1 | b to what power gives b? The power 1. |
| log_b(bⁿ) = n | b to what power gives bⁿ? Obviously n. The log undoes the exponent. |
| b^(log_b(y)) = y | The log hands you an exponent, and raising b to it gives y back. The exponent undoes the log. |

The last two rows are the proof that log and exponent are inverses, exactly like the function f and f⁻¹ from yesterday.

### What a log is allowed to take
Take base 2. Raise it to any power you like: 2³ = 8, 2⁰ = 1, 2⁻⁵ = 1/32. The result is **always positive**. A log asks "which power gives y?", so y has to be something a power can produce.

- y must be **greater than 0**. log₂(−4) and log₂(0) do not exist.
- The base b must be **positive and not 1**. The base 1 would give 1ˣ = 1 for every x, so it could never produce 8 or anything else.
- The *answer* x can be any real number, including negative.

BN: লগের ভেতরে শুধু ধনাত্মক (positive) সংখ্যা যাবে। কারণ 2 কে যত ঘাতই দাও, ফল কখনো শূন্য বা ঋণাত্মক হয় না। আর base হতে হবে ধনাত্মক এবং 1 ছাড়া। উত্তরটা (ঘাত) কিন্তু ঋণাত্মক বা ভগ্নাংশ হতেই পারে, যেমন log₂(1/8) = −3।

### The graph: a mirror image
Plot the points of y = 2ˣ: (0, 1), (1, 2), (2, 4), (3, 8). Now swap each pair. You get (1, 0), (2, 1), (4, 2), (8, 3). Those are the points of y = log₂(x). Inverse functions swap inputs and outputs, so their graphs mirror across the line y = x.

GRAPH: y = 2ˣ and y = log₂(x) | x | y | -1,0.5 0,1 1,2 2,4 3,8 ;; 0.5,-1 1,0 2,1 4,2 8,3 | Teal: y = 2ˣ. Orange: y = log₂(x). The orange curve never touches the vertical axis, since log₂(0) does not exist. It also rises slowly: to get to height 3 it needs x = 8.

BN: 2ˣ এর গ্রাফ আর log₂x এর গ্রাফ আসলে y = x রেখার দুই পাশে আয়নার প্রতিবিম্ব। কারণ ইনভার্স ফাংশনে input আর output জায়গা বদলায়: (3, 8) হয়ে যায় (8, 3)। লগের গ্রাফ খুব ধীরে ওঠে, আর কখনো y-অক্ষ ছুঁয়ে ফেলে না।

### The two logs on your calculator
Your calculator has two log buttons.

- `log` is base 10. log(1000) = 3 because 10³ = 1000.
- `ln` is the *natural log*, base e ≈ 2.718 (you met e yesterday).

What about log₂(10)? There is no base 2 button, but there is a trick, and it is short enough to prove right now.

KEY: Change of base: `log_b(a) = log(a) ÷ log(b)`, or with ln, the same formula.

**Why it works.** Let x = log_b(a). Rewrite as an exponent: bˣ = a. Take log (base 10) of both sides: x · log(b) = log(a). (Bringing x down is a log rule you will prove tomorrow. For today, accept it.) Divide: x = log(a) ÷ log(b). ∎

Now we can finally answer the colony question. 2ˣ = 10 means x = log₂(10) = log(10) ÷ log(2) = 1 ÷ 0.30103 ≈ **3.32** hours. It sits between 3 and 4, as we predicted. Check it: 2^3.32 ≈ 9.99.

EX: Solve 5ˣ = 40
1. x is the exponent of 5 that gives 40, so x = log₅(40).
2. Change base: x = ln(40) ÷ ln(5) = 3.6889 ÷ 1.6094.
3. x ≈ **2.29**. Sanity check: 5² = 25 and 5³ = 125, and 40 is closer to 25. ✓
/EX

BN: ক্যালকুলেটরে শুধু log (base 10) আর ln (base e) বোতাম থাকে। তাই log₂(10) বের করতে চাইলে করো: log(10) ÷ log(2) = 1 ÷ 0.30103 ≈ 3.32। নিয়মটা মনে রাখো: নতুন base দিয়ে ভাগ, উপরে যেটার লগ চাই সেটা, নিচে পুরনো base।

### Why scientists love logs: quantities that span huge ranges
The faintest sound you can hear and a jet engine differ in intensity by a factor of about 10¹², a trillion. On a normal number line you cannot show both. A log scale counts **powers of ten** instead: each step up the scale means "× 10".

- **Decibels:** loudness in dB = 10 · log₁₀(I ÷ I₀), where I₀ is the faintest audible intensity. A whisper is about 30 dB, normal talk 60 dB, a jet at close range 120 dB.
- **Richter-type earthquake scales:** each whole step means the shaking amplitude is 10 times larger.
- **pH in chemistry:** pH 3 is ten times more acidic than pH 4.

EX: Is 80 dB twice as loud as 40 dB?
1. The difference is 40 dB. Every 10 dB is a factor 10 in intensity, since 10 · log₁₀(10) = 10.
2. 40 dB means four steps of 10 dB, so the intensity grows by 10 · 10 · 10 · 10 = 10,000.
3. So 80 dB has **10,000 times** the intensity of 40 dB. "Twice as many dB" is nowhere near "twice as loud". This is why a log scale is both powerful and easy to misread.
/EX

BN: ডেসিবেল (dB) স্কেলে প্রতি 10 dB বাড়লে শব্দের তীব্রতা 10 গুণ বাড়ে। তাই 40 dB থেকে 80 dB হলে তীব্রতা 10,000 গুণ, দ্বিগুণ নয়! লগ স্কেল বিশাল পরিসরের সংখ্যাকে ছোট জায়গায় দেখাতে পারে, কিন্তু "দ্বিগুণ" মনে করলে ভুল হবে। একই ধরন দেখা যায় ভূমিকম্পের রিখটার স্কেলে আর রসায়নের pH স্কেলে।

### A neat trick: counting digits
10² = 100 has 3 digits. 10³ = 1000 has 4 digits. So log(100) = 2 is one less than the digit count. In general, a positive whole number N has **⌊log(N)⌋ + 1 digits**, where ⌊ ⌋ means "round down". You will use this in the challenge.

### Today's formula sheet
| What | Formula | বাংলায় |
| Definition | log_b(y) = x ⟺ bˣ = y | লগ মানে ঘাত বের করা |
| Zero and one | log_b(1) = 0, log_b(b) = 1 | যেকোনো base এর জন্য |
| Undo each other | log_b(bⁿ) = n, b^(log_b y) = y | লগ আর ঘাত একে অপরের উল্টো |
| Allowed values | y > 0, b > 0, b ≠ 1 | ভেতরে ধনাত্মক সংখ্যা |
| Change of base | log_b(a) = log(a) ÷ log(b) | ক্যালকুলেটরের জন্য |
| Decibels | dB = 10 · log₁₀(I ÷ I₀) | 10 dB = ১০ গুণ তীব্রতা |
| Digits of N | ⌊log(N)⌋ + 1 | সংখ্যায় কয়টা অঙ্ক |

BN: লগ শুধু আজকের টপিক নয়। ক্যালকুলাসে ln x এর ঢাল বের করবে, সার্কিটে ক্যাপাসিটর চার্জ হওয়ার সময় বের করবে, আর পরিসংখ্যানে বড় গুণফলকে যোগে বদলাবে। আজ ভিত পাকা করলে সামনের ৩০ সপ্তাহ সহজ হবে।

### Mistakes to avoid
TRAP: Five common ones. (1) Thinking log₂(8) = 8 ÷ 2 = 4. Log is not division, it is a question about exponents. (2) Forgetting the base: log₂ and log₁₀ are different questions. (3) Taking the log of zero or a negative number. (4) Writing log(a) ÷ log(b) as log(a ÷ b). They are not the same: with a = 100 and b = 10, the first gives 2 ÷ 1 = 2, the second gives log(10) = 1. (5) Mixing up which number is the answer. In log_b(y) = x, the answer x is the exponent.

QUIZ:

Q: What is log₁₀(1)?; 0; 1; 10; undefined; 1; 10⁰ = 1. Any base to the power 0 gives 1.

@section physics | 3 · Physics: Newton's second law | 35 min

### Why this law exists
Push a shopping trolley that is empty and then full, using the same effort. The empty one speeds up quickly. The full one barely moves. Now push the empty one harder. It speeds up even faster. Two observations from daily life, both about acceleration (how fast velocity changes, from Week 1):

- More force gives **more** acceleration. Double the force and you double the acceleration.
- More mass gives **less** acceleration. Double the mass and you halve the acceleration.

Measured carefully in the lab, both relationships turn out to be exact, and they combine into one equation.

KEY: Newton's second law: `F_net = m · a`, or a = F_net ÷ m. The *net* force on an object, which is the sum of all forces with directions included, equals its mass times its acceleration. The acceleration points the same way as the net force.

BN: বল (force) বেশি হলে ত্বরণ (acceleration) বেশি হয়। ভর (mass) বেশি হলে ত্বরণ কমে যায়। দুটো কথা একসাথে লিখলে হয় F = m × a। খেয়াল রাখো, এখানে F মানে **নিট বল (net force)**, অর্থাৎ সব বলের ভেক্টর যোগফল, দিকসহ। ত্বরণ নিট বলের দিকেই হয়।

### The unit comes for free
Mass is in kg and acceleration in m/s², so force is kg · m/s². We call this one **newton (N)**. So 1 N is the force that gives a 1 kg mass an acceleration of 1 m/s². An apple weighs about 1 N.

**Weight is a force.** It is gravity's pull: W = m · g, with g ≈ 9.8 m/s² near Earth's surface. Your mass stays the same on the Moon. Your weight drops to about one sixth.

BN: 1 নিউটন (N) হলো সেই বল, যা 1 kg ভরকে 1 m/s² ত্বরণ দেয়। ওজন (weight) একটা বল: W = mg। ভর সব জায়গায় একই থাকে, কিন্তু ওজন বদলায়। চাঁদে তোমার ভর একই, ওজন প্রায় ছয় ভাগের এক ভাগ। লিফট উপরে উঠতে শুরু করলে নিজেকে ভারী লাগে কারণ স্কেল তোমাকে বেশি বল দিয়ে ঠেলে (791 N)। নিচে নামতে শুরু করলে হালকা লাগে (581 N)।

### Worked examples
EX: A car
1. A 1200 kg car has a net forward force of 3000 N. Find its acceleration, and its speed after 4 s from rest.
2. a = F ÷ m = 3000 ÷ 1200 = **2.5 m/s²**.
3. From Week 1, v = u + at = 0 + 2.5 × 4 = **10 m/s** (36 km/h).
/EX

EX: Net force, the block
1. You pull a 10 kg block with 50 N. Friction pulls back with 20 N. What is its acceleration?
2. Choose forward as positive. Net force = 50 − 20 = 30 N. Friction is a force too, and it counts.
3. a = 30 ÷ 10 = **3 m/s²**.
/EX

EX: Why you feel heavy in a lift
1. A 70 kg person stands on a scale in a lift that accelerates *upward* at 1.5 m/s². What does the scale read?
2. Two forces act: the scale pushes up with N, gravity pulls down with mg = 70 × 9.8 = 686 N.
3. Take up as positive. Net force = N − mg, and Newton's law says it equals ma. So N − 686 = 70 × 1.5 = 105.
4. N = **791 N**. The scale reads more than your weight of 686 N, which is exactly the "heavier" feeling when a lift starts upward.
/EX

TRAP: Three places where students lose marks. (1) **Mass vs weight.** Mass is in kg and never changes. Weight is a force in N. (2) **Using one force instead of the net force.** Add all forces with signs first. (3) **"Moving means a force is acting."** No. Constant velocity means net force zero. A force is needed to *change* velocity, not to keep it.
