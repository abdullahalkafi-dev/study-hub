@section math | 2 · Part A: Applications of derivatives (14 marks, about 55 min) | no calculator

LVL: Part A | Questions 1 to 13
P: (1 mark) Find and classify the critical points of f(x) = x³ − 12x. => f′ = 3x² − 12 = 0 gives x = ±2. f″ = 6x. At x = 2, f″ = 12 > 0: **local minimum, f = −16**. At x = −2, f″ = −12 < 0: **local maximum, f = 16**.
P: (1 mark) A farmer has 100 m of fence and uses a straight river as the fourth side of a rectangular field. Find the largest area. => With width x, length 100 − 2x, A = x(100 − 2x). A′ = 100 − 4x = 0 gives x = 25, so **A = 25 × 50 = 1250 m²**.
P: (1 mark) Estimate √16.4 by linear approximation at 16. => f′(16) = 1/(2 × 4) = 1/8. √16.4 ≈ 4 + 0.4 ÷ 8 = **4.05**.
P: (1 mark) Do one step of Newton's method for f(x) = x² − 7 starting at x₀ = 3. => x₁ = 3 − (9 − 7) ÷ 6 = 3 − 1/3 = **8/3 ≈ 2.667**.
P: (1 mark) A 5 m ladder slides down a wall. Its foot moves away at 0.5 m/s. How fast is the top moving down when the foot is 3 m from the wall? => x² + y² = 25, so x x′ + y y′ = 0. At x = 3, y = 4, so y′ = −(3 ÷ 4)(0.5) = **−0.375 m/s** (down at 0.375 m/s).
P: (1 mark) Find lim (x → 0) (e^(3x) − 1) ÷ x. => L'Hôpital: 3e^(3x) ÷ 1 → **3**.
P: (1 mark) Find lim (x → 0) (1 − cos 2x) ÷ x². => 0 ÷ 0: 2 sin 2x ÷ 2x → 0 ÷ 0: 4 cos 2x ÷ 2 → **2**.
P: (1 mark) Find lim (x → ∞) x² e^(−x). => x² ÷ eˣ → 2x ÷ eˣ → 2 ÷ eˣ → **0**.
P: (1 mark) Find lim (x → 0) (tan x − x) ÷ x³. => 0 ÷ 0: (sec² x − 1) ÷ 3x² = tan² x ÷ 3x² = (1/3)(tan x ÷ x)² → **1/3**.
P: (1 mark) Find the value c given by the mean value theorem for f(x) = x³ − x on [0, 2]. => Chord slope = (6 − 0) ÷ 2 = 3. 3c² − 1 = 3, so c² = 4/3 and **c = 2/√3 ≈ 1.155**.
P: (1 mark) Find the inflection point of f(x) = x³ − 6x² + 9x. => f″ = 6x − 12 = 0 at x = 2, and f″ changes sign there. f(2) = 8 − 24 + 18 = 2. The point is **(2, 2)**.
P: (1 mark) Find all asymptotes of f(x) = (2x + 1) ÷ (x − 3). => The denominator vanishes at x = 3 (numerator 7 ≠ 0): vertical asymptote **x = 3**. As x → ±∞, f → 2: horizontal asymptote **y = 2**.
P: (2 marks) An open-top box with a square base must hold 32 m³. Find the dimensions that use the least material, and the least surface area. => Base side x, height h = 32 ÷ x². A = x² + 4xh = x² + 128 ÷ x. A′ = 2x − 128 ÷ x² = 0 gives x³ = 64, **x = 4 m, h = 2 m**. **A = 16 + 32 = 48 m²**. (A″ = 2 + 256 ÷ x³ > 0, so it is a minimum.)

@section physics | 3 · Part B: Torque, equilibrium and materials (6 marks, about 35 min) | calculator allowed

LVL: Part B | Questions 14 to 19
P: (1 mark) A 30 kg child sits 2 m from the pivot of a seesaw. Where must a 20 kg child sit on the other side to balance? => 30 × 2 = 20 × d, so **d = 3 m**.
P: (1 mark) A uniform 6 m beam of mass 40 kg rests on supports at its two ends. A 60 kg person stands 2 m from the left end. Find both support forces. => Torques about the left end: R × 6 = 40 × 9.8 × 3 + 60 × 9.8 × 2 = 1176 + 1176 = 2352, so **right support = 392 N**. Total weight is 980 N, so **left support = 588 N**.
P: (1 mark) A wheel at rest accelerates at 3 rad/s². Find ω and the angle turned after 4 s. => ω = 3 × 4 = **12 rad/s**. θ = ½ × 3 × 16 = **24 rad**.
P: (1 mark) A force of 50 N acts tangentially at the rim of a disc of radius 0.4 m and moment of inertia 0.5 kg·m². Find the angular acceleration. => τ = 50 × 0.4 = 20 N·m. α = τ ÷ I = 20 ÷ 0.5 = **40 rad/s²**.
P: (1 mark) A steel wire (E = 200 GPa) of length 2 m and cross-section 2 × 10⁻⁶ m² carries a load of 400 N. Find the stress and the extension. => σ = 400 ÷ 2 × 10⁻⁶ = **200 MPa**. ΔL = σ L ÷ E = 200 × 10⁶ × 2 ÷ 200 × 10⁹ = **2 mm**.
P: (1 mark) A uniform box 0.4 m wide and 1.0 m tall, mass 30 kg, stands on a rough floor (no slipping). A horizontal push is applied at the very top. What push just tips it? => Tipping about the lower edge: F × 1.0 = m g × 0.2. F = 30 × 9.8 × 0.2 = **58.8 N**.

### Challenges of the week
LVL: Challenge | The shortest ladder over a fence
P: A fence 2 m high stands 1 m in front of a tall wall. Find the shortest ladder that reaches from the ground, over the fence, to the wall. => With θ the angle of the ladder with the ground, L(θ) = 2 ÷ sin θ + 1 ÷ cos θ. L′ = −2 cos θ ÷ sin² θ + sin θ ÷ cos² θ = 0 gives tan³ θ = 2, so tan θ = 2^(1/3) = 1.260. Then sin θ = 0.783 and cos θ = 0.622, and L = 2 ÷ 0.783 + 1 ÷ 0.622 = **4.16 m**. (Closed form: L = (2^(2/3) + 1)^(3/2).)

LVL: Challenge | A limit with an exponent
P: Find lim (x → 0) (sin x ÷ x)^(1/x²). => Let y be the expression. ln y = ln(sin x ÷ x) ÷ x². For small x, sin x ÷ x ≈ 1 − x²/6, and ln(1 − x²/6) ≈ −x²/6, so ln y → **−1/6** and y → **e^(−1/6) ≈ 0.846**. (L'Hôpital twice on ln(sin x ÷ x) ÷ x² gives the same result.)
