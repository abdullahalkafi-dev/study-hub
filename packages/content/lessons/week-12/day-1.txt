@section math | 2 · Math: complex numbers | 55 min

### Why a new number?
The equation x² = −1 has no real solution, since a real number squared is never negative. Mathematicians invented a number i with

EQ: i² = −1

A **complex number** is z = a + b i, where a and b are real. We call a the **real part** (Re z) and b the **imaginary part** (Im z). Every real number is complex with b = 0.

Complex numbers are not "imaginary" in any unreal sense: they are as consistent as the reals, and engineers use them daily. The cost of the invention is zero: every rule of ordinary algebra still works, with the extra fact i² = −1.

BN: x² = −1 এর কোনো বাস্তব সমাধান নেই, তাই নতুন সংখ্যা i ধরা হয়: i² = −1। জটিল সংখ্যা z = a + bi, a বাস্তব অংশ, b কাল্পনিক অংশ। এটা মোটেও "অবাস্তব" নয়: বাস্তব সংখ্যার মতোই সুসংগত এবং প্রকৌশলীরা প্রতিদিন ব্যবহার করে। বীজগণিতের সব নিয়ম একই থাকে, শুধু একটা নতুন তথ্য: i² = −1।

### Arithmetic
EX: The four operations
1. **Add:** (3 + 2i) + (1 − 4i) = **4 − 2i** (add real and imaginary parts separately).
2. **Multiply:** (3 + 2i)(1 − 4i) = 3 − 12i + 2i − 8i² = 3 − 10i + 8 = **11 − 10i**.
3. **Powers of i:** i, −1, −i, 1, then it repeats every 4. So i¹⁵ = i³ = **−i**, and i¹⁰⁰ = **1**.
4. **Conjugate:** the conjugate of z = a + bi is z̄ = a − bi (flip the sign of the imaginary part). Then **z z̄ = a² + b²**, a real number.
5. **Divide:** multiply top and bottom by the conjugate of the bottom. (3 + 2i) ÷ (1 − 4i) = (3 + 2i)(1 + 4i) ÷ (1 + 16) = (3 + 12i + 2i − 8) ÷ 17 = **(−5 + 14i) ÷ 17**.
/EX

PREDICT: What do you get when you multiply a complex number by its own conjugate, for example (3 + 4i)(3 − 4i)? Real, imaginary or complex? Why does that make division possible? => You get 9 + 16 = **25**, a purely real number, because the imaginary terms cancel. That is why we multiply the denominator by its conjugate: it turns the denominator into a real number, and then division is ordinary.

### The Argand plane and the modulus
Plot z = a + bi as the point (a, b). The distance from the origin is the **modulus**:

EQ: abs(z) = √(a² + b²)     and     abs(z)² = z z̄

Addition is vector addition. Conjugation reflects in the real axis. Multiplication by i rotates a point by **90°**: i (a + bi) = −b + ai.

EX: Modulus and rotation
1. abs(3 + 4i) = √(9 + 16) = **5**. abs(5 − 12i) = **13**.
2. z = 2 + 3i: i z = −3 + 2i, which is z turned a quarter-turn anticlockwise.
3. abs(z₁ z₂) = abs(z₁) abs(z₂): (1 + i)(2 − i) = 2 − i + 2i − i² = 3 + i, with modulus √10 = √2 × √5 ✓.
/EX

### Quadratics with no real roots
The quadratic formula still works when the discriminant is negative: √(−d) = i √d.

EX: Complex roots
1. z² + 2z + 5 = 0: z = (−2 ± √(4 − 20)) ÷ 2 = (−2 ± 4i) ÷ 2 = **−1 ± 2i**. Check: (−1 + 2i)² = 1 − 4i − 4 = −3 − 4i, and −3 − 4i + 2(−1 + 2i) + 5 = 0 ✓.
2. z² − 4z + 13 = 0: z = 2 ± 3i.
3. For polynomials with **real** coefficients, complex roots come in conjugate pairs.
/EX

EX: Equations in z
1. Find z if z (2 − i) = 5 + 5i: z = (5 + 5i)(2 + i) ÷ 5 = (10 + 5i + 10i − 5) ÷ 5 = **1 + 3i**.
2. Find z if z + 2 z̄ = 6 + 3i. With z = a + bi: 3a − bi = 6 + 3i, so a = 2, b = −3, **z = 2 − 3i**.
/EX

BN: ঋণাত্মক ডিসক্রিমিন্যান্টেও দ্বিঘাত সূত্র চলে, √(−d) = i√d। z² + 2z + 5 = 0 এর মূল −1 ± 2i। বাস্তব সহগের বহুপদীতে জটিল মূল সবসময় অনুবন্ধী (conjugate) জোড়ায় আসে। মডুলাস |z| = √(a² + b²), z·z̄ = |z|², আর |z₁z₂| = |z₁||z₂|। i দিয়ে গুণ মানে 90° ঘোরানো।

TRAP: Do not write √(−4) × √(−9) = √36: use i first: (2i)(3i) = 6i² = −6. When dividing, multiply **both** numerator and denominator by the conjugate. And abs(z)² is a² + b², **not** (a + bi)².

@section physics | 3 · Physics: pressure in fluids at rest | 35 min

### Density and pressure
A **fluid** is a substance that flows: a liquid or a gas. Two basic quantities:

EQ: density ρ = m ÷ V (kg/m³)     pressure P = F ÷ A (pascal, 1 Pa = 1 N/m²)

| Substance | Density (kg/m³) |
| Air (sea level) | 1.2 |
| Oil | 800 |
| Water | 1000 |
| Seawater | 1025 |
| Mercury | 13,600 |
| Aluminium | 2700 |

Atmospheric pressure at sea level is **101,325 Pa = 1 atm** (about 10⁵ Pa). Pressure acts in **all directions**, perpendicular to any surface.

BN: ঘনত্ব ρ = m/V, চাপ P = F/A (পাস্কাল = N/m²)। সমুদ্রপৃষ্ঠে বায়ুচাপ 101,325 Pa = 1 atm। তরলে চাপ সব দিকে সমান এবং যেকোনো তলের লম্ব।

EX: Pressure
1. A 60 kg person on a 4 cm² stiletto heel (all weight on one heel): P = 588 ÷ (4 × 10⁻⁴) = **1.47 MPa** (14.5 atm), much more than an elephant's footprint.
2. A 2.7 kg block of volume 1000 cm³ has ρ = 2.7 ÷ 10⁻³ = **2700 kg/m³**: aluminium.
/EX

### Pressure at depth
Take a column of fluid of cross-section A. The pressure at depth h must hold up the weight of the fluid above it. For a small change in depth dy the pressure changes by dP = ρ g dy, so:

EQ: P(h) = P₀ + ∫₀ʰ ρ g dy = P₀ + ρ g h     (constant density)

The **gauge pressure** is P − P₀ = ρ g h. It depends only on the depth, not on the shape of the container.

EX: Depths
1. At 10 m in water: gauge = 1000 × 9.8 × 10 = 98,000 Pa. Absolute P = 101,325 + 98,000 = **199,325 Pa ≈ 1.97 atm**. Every 10 m of water adds about 1 atm.
2. At 30 m in the sea: gauge = 1025 × 9.8 × 30 = 301 kPa, absolute = **403 kPa ≈ 4.0 atm**.
3. **Barometer:** the atmosphere supports a column of mercury: h = 101,325 ÷ (13,600 × 9.8) = **0.760 m**, the origin of "760 mmHg". With water, the column would be **10.3 m** tall.
4. **Blood pressure** of 120 mmHg = 120 × 133.3 = **16.0 kPa** (above atmospheric).
/EX

### Pascal's principle and hydraulics
A pressure change applied to a confined fluid is transmitted undiminished everywhere. So in a hydraulic jack: P = F₁ ÷ A₁ = F₂ ÷ A₂.

EX: A hydraulic jack
1. Small piston radius 1 cm, large piston radius 10 cm: the area ratio is 100. A 100 N push gives **F₂ = 10,000 N** (enough to lift a 1000 kg car).
2. Energy is conserved: the small piston must move 100 times farther. To raise the car by 1 cm you push the small piston down by **1 m**.
/EX

### A pressure that changes with height: the atmosphere
For a gas the density depends on the pressure (ρ = ρ₀ P ÷ P₀ for a gas at constant temperature). Going up by dh decreases pressure by dP = −ρ g dh:

dP ÷ dh = −(ρ₀ g ÷ P₀) P     so     **P(h) = P₀ e^(−h ÷ H)**     with     H = P₀ ÷ (ρ₀ g) = 101,325 ÷ (1.225 × 9.8) = **8440 m**

(This is a separable differential equation: the solution is the exponential decay you already know.)

EX: Pressure on a mountain
1. At 3000 m: P = 101.3 × e^(−3000 ÷ 8440) = 101.3 × 0.701 = **71.0 kPa** (70% of sea level).
2. At the top of Everest (8848 m): P = 101.3 × e^(−1.048) = **35.5 kPa** (the measured value is about 34 kPa, a third of sea level, which is why climbers need oxygen).
/EX

### Force on a dam
The pressure on a vertical wall increases with depth, so the total force is an integral.

EX: A dam
1. A dam of width w = 100 m holds water of depth H = 20 m. At depth y the pressure (gauge) is ρ g y, and a strip of height dy has area w dy.
2. F = ∫₀ᴴ ρ g y w dy = ½ ρ g w H² = ½ × 1000 × 9.8 × 100 × 400 = **1.96 × 10⁸ N** (about 20,000 tonnes of weight).
3. Doubling the depth **quadruples** the force. That is why dams are thick at the base.
/EX

TRAP: Use **gauge** pressure (ρ g h) for forces on the wall of a container open to the atmosphere (the atmosphere pushes on both sides), and **absolute** pressure for gas laws. The depth h is the **vertical** depth, not the distance along a slanted pipe. And a hydraulic jack multiplies force, not energy.
