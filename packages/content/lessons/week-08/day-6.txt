@section math | 2 · Part A: Integration (14 marks, about 55 min) | no calculator

LVL: Part A | Questions 1 to 13
P: (1 mark) Find ∫ (4x³ − 6x + 2) dx. => **x⁴ − 3x² + 2x + C**.
P: (1 mark) Find ∫ (e^(2x) + 1 ÷ x) dx for x > 0. => **e^(2x) ÷ 2 + ln x + C**.
P: (1 mark) Find f if f′(x) = 6x − 4 and f(1) = 3. => f = 3x² − 4x + C. 3 − 4 + C = 3, so C = 4 and **f(x) = 3x² − 4x + 4**.
P: (1 mark) Evaluate ∫₀³ (x² + 2x) dx. => [x³ ÷ 3 + x²] = 9 + 9 = **18**.
P: (1 mark) Evaluate ∫₁⁴ 1 ÷ √x dx. => [2√x] = 4 − 2 = **2**.
P: (1 mark) Evaluate ∫₀^π (1 + sin x) dx. => [x − cos x] = (π + 1) − (0 − 1) = **π + 2**.
P: (1 mark) Find ∫ 2x (x² + 5)³ dx. => u = x² + 5: **(x² + 5)⁴ ÷ 4 + C**.
P: (1 mark) Evaluate ∫₀¹ x e^(x²) dx. => u = x² from 0 to 1: ½ (e − 1) = **0.859**.
P: (1 mark) Find the area enclosed between y = x² and y = x + 2. => They meet where x² − x − 2 = 0, so x = −1 and 2. The line is on top. ∫₋₁² (x + 2 − x²) dx = [x²/2 + 2x − x³/3] = (2 + 4 − 8/3) − (1/2 − 2 + 1/3) = 10/3 + 7/6 = **9/2 = 4.5**.
P: (1 mark) Find the average value of f(x) = x³ on [0, 2]. => (1/2) [x⁴ ÷ 4] = (1/2)(4) = **2**.
P: (1 mark) Find d/dx ∫₀^(x²) eᵗ dt. => eˣ² × 2x = **2x e^(x²)**.
P: (1 mark) Estimate ∫₀² x² dx with four right-endpoint rectangles. => Δx = 0.5, heights 0.25, 1, 2.25, 4. Sum = 7.5 × 0.5 = **3.75** (exact 8/3).
P: (2 marks) A particle has velocity v(t) = 2t − 6 m/s for 0 ≤ t ≤ 5. Find its displacement and the total distance it travels. => Displacement = [t² − 6t] = 25 − 30 = **−5 m**. The velocity is zero at t = 3. ∫₀³ v dt = 9 − 18 = −9, and ∫₃⁵ v dt = (−5) − (−9) = +4. Distance = 9 + 4 = **13 m**.

@section physics | 3 · Part B: Rotation (6 marks, about 35 min) | calculator allowed

LVL: Part B | Questions 14 to 19
P: (1 mark) Find I for a 3 kg mass at 0.4 m and a 1 kg mass at 1.2 m from the same axis. => 3 × 0.16 + 1 × 1.44 = 0.48 + 1.44 = **1.92 kg·m²**.
P: (1 mark) A uniform rod (1.5 kg, 2 m) spins about one end at 3 rad/s. Find I and the kinetic energy. => I = (1/3)(1.5)(4) = **2 kg·m²**. KE = ½ × 2 × 9 = **9 J**.
P: (1 mark) A 20 N force acts tangentially on the rim of a solid disc (M = 10 kg, R = 0.5 m) free to turn about its centre. Find α. => I = ½ × 10 × 0.25 = 1.25. τ = 20 × 0.5 = 10 N·m. α = **8 rad/s²**.
P: (1 mark) A skater spins at 4 rad/s with I = 3 kg·m² and pulls in to I = 1 kg·m². Find the new ω and the work she does. => ω₂ = 12 ÷ 1 = **12 rad/s**. KE₁ = ½ × 3 × 16 = 24 J, KE₂ = ½ × 1 × 144 = 72 J. Work = **48 J**.
P: (1 mark) Masses of 1 kg and 3 kg hang over a solid disc pulley of mass 2 kg. Find the acceleration and the tension on the heavier side. => I ÷ R² = M ÷ 2 = 1 kg. a = (3 − 1) × 9.8 ÷ (1 + 3 + 1) = **3.92 m/s²**. T₂ = 3 (9.8 − 3.92) = **17.6 N**.
P: (1 mark) A solid sphere rolls without slipping from rest down a ramp, dropping 0.7 m. Find its speed at the bottom. => v = √(2 × 9.8 × 0.7 ÷ 1.4) = √9.8 = **3.13 m/s**.

### Challenges of the week
LVL: Challenge | A bowl by revolution
P: The region under y = x² between x = 0 and x = 2 is spun about the **y-axis**, which makes a bowl of depth 4 (use slices at height y with radius x = √y). Find the volume the bowl holds. => A slice at height y is a disc of radius √y, so area π y. V = π ∫₀⁴ y dy = π × 16 ÷ 2 = **8π ≈ 25.1**. (Check: the cylinder of radius 2 and height 4 has volume 16π, and the bowl holds exactly half.)

LVL: Challenge | A rod that gets heavier
P: A rod of length L has a mass per unit length λ(x) = λ₀ (1 + x ÷ L), where x is measured from one end. Find its total mass M, its centre of mass, and its moment of inertia about the x = 0 end in terms of M and L. Compare with the uniform rod. => M = ∫₀ᴸ λ₀ (1 + x ÷ L) dx = λ₀ (L + L ÷ 2) = **1.5 λ₀ L**. Centre of mass: (1 ÷ M) ∫ x λ dx = λ₀ (L² ÷ 2 + L² ÷ 3) ÷ (1.5 λ₀ L) = (5/6) L ÷ 1.5 = **5L ÷ 9** (a bit beyond the middle, as expected). I = ∫ x² λ dx = λ₀ (L³ ÷ 3 + L³ ÷ 4) = (7/12) λ₀ L³ = (7/12)(M ÷ 1.5) L² = **7 M L² ÷ 18 ≈ 0.389 M L²**, compared with 0.333 M L² for the uniform rod: more mass far from the axis means a larger I.
