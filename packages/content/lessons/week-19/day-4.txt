# Forced oscillation and resonance. Then: internal resistance and power transfer
lead: A force that keeps pushing a spring changes the equation to m x″ + b x′ + k x = F₀ cos ωt, a non-homogeneous equation. You solve it in two steps: the homogeneous part you know, plus one particular solution found by educated guessing (undetermined coefficients). The result is resonance: push at the right frequency and the amplitude grows enormously. In physics, a real battery is not ideal. It has an internal resistance, so the voltage at its terminals falls when it supplies current, and there is a famous rule for getting the most power out of it.

BN: বাইরে থেকে বল দিলে সমীকরণ হয় m x″ + b x′ + k x = F₀ cos ωt। সমাধান দুই ধাপে: জানা সমসত্ত্ব অংশ + একটা বিশেষ সমাধান (অনুমান করে, undetermined coefficients)। ফল: অনুনাদ (resonance), ঠিক ফ্রিকোয়েন্সিতে ঠেললে বিস্তার বিশাল হয়ে যায়। পদার্থবিজ্ঞানে বাস্তব ব্যাটারির অভ্যন্তরীণ রোধ আছে, তাই কারেন্ট দিলে টার্মিনাল ভোল্টেজ কমে; আর সর্বোচ্চ ক্ষমতা পাওয়ার একটা বিখ্যাত নিয়ম আছে।

@section math | 2 · Math: non-homogeneous equations and resonance | 55 min

### The method
**General solution = complementary solution (the homogeneous one, with constants) + one particular solution y_p.**
Guess y_p with the same form as the right-hand side with unknown coefficients: exponential e^(ax) → A e^(ax); polynomial → polynomial of the same degree; cos or sin → A cos + B sin. **If the guess already solves the homogeneous equation (resonance), multiply it by x.**

BN: সাধারণ সমাধান = সমসত্ত্ব সমাধান (ধ্রুবকসহ) + একটা বিশেষ সমাধান y_p। ডান পাশের মতো আকারে অনুমান করো: e^(ax) → A e^(ax); বহুপদী → একই ঘাতের বহুপদী; cos/sin → A cos + B sin। অনুমান যদি সমসত্ত্ব সমীকরণের সমাধান হয় (অনুনাদ), তাহলে x দিয়ে গুণ করো।

EX: Three guesses
1. **y″ − 3y′ + 2y = 4e^(3x).** The roots are 1 and 2. Try y_p = A e^(3x): (9 − 9 + 2) A = 4 gives A = 2. **y = C₁ e^x + C₂ e^(2x) + 2 e^(3x)**.
2. **y″ + y′ − 2y = 6x.** Try y_p = A x + B: A − 2 (A x + B) = 6x. The x terms: −2A = 6, A = −3. The constants: A − 2B = 0, B = −1.5. **y_p = −3x − 1.5**.
3. **Resonance: y″ + y = cos x.** The guess A cos x + B sin x already solves the homogeneous equation. Try y_p = (x ÷ 2) sin x: y_p′ = ½ sin x + (x ÷ 2) cos x, y_p″ = cos x − (x ÷ 2) sin x, and y_p″ + y_p = **cos x** ✓. The amplitude grows linearly with x without bound.
4. **Undamped forced:** x″ + 4x = 3 cos 3t. Try A cos 3t: (−9 + 4) A = 3, A = **−0.6**. The response is out of phase with the push when ω is above ω₀.
/EX

### The forced, damped oscillator
For m x″ + b x′ + k x = F₀ cos ωt, after the transients (e^(−ζω₀t)) have died out, the **steady state** is x = A cos (ωt − φ) with
**A (ω) = F₀ ÷ √[(k − m ω²)² + (b ω)²]**, tan φ = b ω ÷ (k − m ω²).
The amplitude peaks near ω_r = ω₀ √(1 − 2ζ²) and at ω = ω₀ the phase is exactly 90° and A = F₀ ÷ (b ω₀) = (F₀ ÷ k) × Q, where Q = 1 ÷ 2ζ.

BN: স্থির অবস্থায় x = A cos(ωt − φ), A(ω) = F₀/√[(k − mω²)² + (bω)²]। ω = ω₀ তে কাল-দশা ঠিক 90° আর A = F₀/(bω₀) = (F₀/k)·Q, Q = 1/(2ζ)। অবমন্দন কম হলে (Q বড়) শিখর খাড়া ও উঁচু।

EX: The car on a bumpy road
The same car: m = 300 kg, k = 20,000 N/m, b = 3000 N s/m (ζ = 0.612, ω₀ = 8.165 rad/s), pushed by an amplitude F₀ = 1000 N.
1. **ω = 0 (a constant push):** A = F₀ ÷ k = **0.050 m**.
2. **ω = 4 rad/s:** (k − mω²) = 15,200 and bω = 12,000: A = 1000 ÷ 19,368 = **0.0516 m**, phase 38°.
3. **ω = ω₀ = 8.165:** A = 1000 ÷ (3000 × 8.165) = **0.0408 m**, phase exactly 90°.
4. **ω = 16 rad/s:** A = **0.0134 m**, phase 140°: a fast shaking is mostly absorbed.
5. **A lighter damper:** b = 1500 (ζ = 0.306): at ω = ω₀, A = 1000 ÷ (1500 × 8.165) = **0.0816 m**, which is Q = 1.63 times F₀ ÷ k. The amplitude doubles when the damping is halved at resonance.
6. **The peak for ζ = 0.612:** ω_r = 8.165 × √(1 − 0.75) = **4.08 rad/s**, A = **0.0516 m**, only slightly above the static value 0.05: a strongly damped system hardly resonates.
7. **Rule of thumb:** a resonance is dangerous when ζ < 0.2 or so (Q above 2.5): buildings, bridges, wine glasses, a washing machine at one spin speed.
/EX

TRAP: The resonance peak is at ω_r = ω₀ √(1 − 2ζ²), a bit below ω₀, and exists only for ζ < 0.707. The **steady state** only lasts after the transient has gone. When the guess duplicates a homogeneous solution you must multiply by x: forgetting this makes the coefficient equation 0 = 1.

@section physics | 3 · Physics: internal resistance and maximum power | 35 min

### A real battery
A real battery is an ideal EMF ε in series with an **internal resistance r**. When it supplies a current I, the **terminal voltage** is **V = ε − I r**. With a load R: I = ε ÷ (R + r), V = ε R ÷ (R + r).
The power delivered to the load is **P = ε² R ÷ (R + r)²**, which is a maximum when **R = r**, with P_max = ε² ÷ 4r. At that point only **50%** of the energy reaches the load. The efficiency is R ÷ (R + r).

BN: বাস্তব ব্যাটারি = আদর্শ EMF ε এর সাথে শ্রেণিতে অভ্যন্তরীণ রোধ r। কারেন্ট I দিলে টার্মিনাল ভোল্টেজ V = ε − Ir। লোডে ক্ষমতা P = ε²R/(R + r)², সর্বোচ্চ হয় R = r এ, P_max = ε²/4r; তখন দক্ষতা মাত্র 50%।

EX: A battery with ε = 12 V and r = 0.5 Ω
| Load R (Ω) | Current I (A) | Terminal V (V) | Power in load (W) | Efficiency |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.5 | 12.0 | 6.0 | **72** | 50% |
| 1 | 8.0 | 8.0 | 64 | 67% |
| 5.5 | 2.0 | 11.0 | 22 | 92% |
| 10 | 1.14 | 11.43 | 13.1 | 95% |

1. **Maximum power** at R = r = 0.5 Ω: P = 144 ÷ 2 = **72 W** (ε² ÷ 4r = 144 ÷ 2 = 72 ✓).
2. **Short circuit:** a car battery (12 V, r = 0.02 Ω): I = 12 ÷ 0.02 = **600 A** and the internal power is I² r = 7200 W: a spanner across the terminals welds.
3. **Why the voltage sags:** a car battery at 12.6 V drops to ≈ 10 V while cranking a 120 A starter: 120 × 0.02 = 2.4 V lost inside.
4. **A phone battery:** ε = 3.7 V, r = 0.15 Ω: a 1.85 Ω load draws 3.7 ÷ 2 = **1.85 A**, V = 3.7 − 0.28 = **3.42 V**.
5. **Two uses of the rule:** an audio amplifier is matched to the speaker (R = r) to get the maximum power, but a power station is built for r ≪ R to get a high efficiency.
6. **Bridge circuit:** equal arms of 100 Ω with one arm 105 Ω and 10 V across: the bridge output is 10 × (100 ÷ 200 − 100 ÷ 205) = **0.122 V**. A strain gauge amplifies a 5% change this way.
/EX

TRAP: Maximum **power** is **not** maximum **efficiency**: matching (R = r) gives only 50%. The terminal voltage equals ε only when no current flows. Do not use ε alone in P = V²/R: use the terminal voltage across the load.
