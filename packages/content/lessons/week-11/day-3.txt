@section math | 2 · Math review: series | 45 min

### The map of Week 10
| Idea | Key results |
| Geometric series | Σ a rⁿ = a ÷ (1 − r), abs(r) < 1 |
| Tests | divergence, p-series, comparison, ratio, integral, alternating (error ≤ first omitted term) |
| Power series | radius from the ratio test, check endpoints, differentiate and integrate term by term |
| Taylor | f(x) = Σ f⁽ⁿ⁾(a)(x − a)ⁿ ÷ n!, remainder ≤ M abs(x − a)ⁿ⁺¹ ÷ (n + 1)! |
| Uses | approximations, limits, exact sums, solving y″ = −y |

BN: গুণোত্তর ধারা a/(1 − r); পরীক্ষা: ডাইভার্জেন্স, p, তুলনা, অনুপাত, ইন্টিগ্রাল, পর্যায়ক্রমিক; ঘাত ধারায় ব্যাসার্ধ ও প্রান্ত; টেলর: f⁽ⁿ⁾(a)(x − a)ⁿ/n! যোগ; ব্যবহার: আসন্ন মান, লিমিট, সঠিক যোগফল।

EX: Mixed series problems
1. Classify: Σ (2/3)ⁿ converges to 3 (from n = 0). Σ 1 ÷ √n diverges. Σ n ÷ (n² + 4) diverges (compare with 1 ÷ n). Σ (−1)ⁿ ÷ n converges conditionally. Σ 3ⁿ ÷ n! converges (ratio 3 ÷ (n + 1) → 0). Σ n² ÷ 2ⁿ converges (ratio → 1/2).
2. Interval: Σ xⁿ ÷ (n 3ⁿ) has R = 3, diverges at x = 3, converges at x = −3: **[−3, 3)**.
3. cos 0.3 ≈ 1 − 0.045 + 0.0003375 = **0.95534** (true 0.955336).
4. e^(−0.1) ≈ 1 − 0.1 + 0.005 − 0.000167 + 0.000004 = **0.904837**.
5. tan x = x + x³/3 + 2x⁵/15 + …, so lim (x → 0) (x − tan x) ÷ x³ = **−1/3**.
6. Σ from n = 1 of n ÷ 3ⁿ = x ÷ (1 − x)² at x = 1/3 = (1/3) ÷ (4/9) = **3/4**.
/EX

TRAP: A series test comes **before** a calculation: never sum a divergent series, and never apply a convergent-series formula outside its radius. "The terms go to 0" is necessary but not sufficient. For the ratio test, remember that L = 1 tells you nothing, and endpoints need separate tests.

### The map of Week 10
| Idea | Key results |
| Wave | y = A sin(kx − ωt), k = 2π ÷ λ, ω = 2π f, v = f λ |
| String | v = √(T ÷ μ), P = ½ μ ω² A² v, standing: fₙ = n v ÷ (2L) |
| Pipes | open fₙ = n v ÷ (2L), closed fₙ = n v ÷ (4L) with n odd |
| Interference | constructive d = m λ, destructive d = (m + ½) λ, beats f₁ − f₂ |
| Sound | I = P ÷ (4π r²), β = 10 log₁₀(I ÷ I₀), −6 dB per doubling of distance |
| Doppler | f′ = f (v + vₒ) ÷ (v − vₛ), Mach: sin θ = v ÷ vₛ |

BN: তরঙ্গ y = A sin(kx − ωt), v = fλ; তার v = √(T/μ), fₙ = nv/(2L); পাইপ খোলা nv/(2L), এক মুখ বন্ধ nv/(4L) (n বিজোড়); ব্যতিচার d = mλ বা (m + ½)λ; শব্দ I = P/(4πr²), β = 10 log(I/I₀); ডপলার f′ = f(v + vₒ)/(v − vₛ)।

EX: Mixed wave problems

2. A string with μ = 0.004 kg/m, T = 100 N, L = 1.0 m: v = √25,000 = **158 m/s**, f₁ = **79.1 Hz**.
3. An open pipe 0.4 m long: f₁ = 343 ÷ 0.8 = **429 Hz**. Closed at one end: f₁ = 343 ÷ 1.6 = **214 Hz**, with the next resonance at 643 Hz.
4. The open pipe (429 Hz) and a 430 Hz fork beat at **1 Hz**.
5. Two 85 dB machines together: 85 + 3 = **88 dB**. To lower an 85 dB source to 65 dB, move 20 dB = a factor of 10 in distance.
6. A train horn of 500 Hz approaches at 30 m/s: 500 × 343 ÷ 313 = **548 Hz**.

@section notes | 4 · Project: the bouncing ball from start to finish | 25 min

### Project brief
A tennis ball (mass 0.057 kg) is dropped from 2.0 m onto a hard floor. Each impact returns a fraction **e = 0.75** of the impact speed. You will describe its motion, its total time, and the fading sound of the bounces. Work through the steps on paper before reading the answers.

NOTES:

### Step by step
LVL: Project | Steps 1 to 7
P: **Step 1 (derivatives).** Write h(t) for the first fall, find the velocity v(t) and the impact speed, and the fall time t₀. => h = 2.0 − 4.9 t², v = −9.8 t. Landing at h = 0: t₀ = √(2 ÷ 4.9) = **0.639 s**, and the impact speed is 9.8 × 0.639 = **6.26 m/s** (equal to √(2 g h) ✓).
P: **Step 2 (geometric sequences).** Find the rebound speeds, heights and flight times for the first three bounces. => Speeds: 4.70, 3.52, 2.64 m/s (multiply by 0.75). Heights: v² ÷ 2g gives **1.125 m, 0.633 m, 0.356 m** (multiply by 0.5625 = e²). Flight times: 2v ÷ g = **0.959 s, 0.719 s, 0.539 s** (multiply by 0.75).
P: **Step 3 (series).** Find the total distance travelled and the total time. => Distance = 2.0 × (1 + e²) ÷ (1 − e²) = 2.0 × 1.5625 ÷ 0.4375 = **7.14 m**. Time = t₀ (1 + e) ÷ (1 − e) = 0.639 × 1.75 ÷ 0.25 = **4.47 s**. (Infinitely many bounces in a finite time.)
P: **Step 4 (momentum and impulse).** Find the impulse on the ball at the first impact, and the average force if the contact lasts 5 ms. => Δp = m (v_in + v_out) = 0.057 × (6.26 + 4.70) = **0.625 N·s**. F = 0.625 ÷ 0.005 = **125 N**, about 220 times the ball's weight (0.56 N).
P: **Step 5 (energy).** Find the energy lost at the first impact and the percentage lost at every impact. => KE before = ½ × 0.057 × 6.26² = 1.117 J. After: 0.628 J. Lost: **0.489 J, 43.75%** each time (1 − e²).
P: **Step 6 (sound level).** Assume the loudness of the impact scales with the impact energy, and the first impact is 70 dB. By how much does the level change per impact, and when does the sound drop below a 30 dB background? => The impact energy falls by e² = 0.5625 per bounce: 10 log(0.5625) = **−2.5 dB per impact**. We need 40 dB of drop: 40 ÷ 2.5 = 16 more impacts, so the **17th impact** is the last one above the background. It occurs at t = t₀ + 2 t₀ e (1 − e¹⁶) ÷ (1 − e) = 0.639 + 0.958 × 0.990 ÷ 0.25 = **4.43 s**, nearly the whole 4.47 s of bouncing.
P: **Step 7 (check with an approximation).** For a nearly elastic ball with e = 1 − δ, show that the energy fraction kept per bounce is about 1 − 2δ, and compare with the exact value for e = 0.95. => e² = 1 − 2δ + δ², so the first-order estimate is 1 − 2δ. For δ = 0.05: estimate 0.900, exact 0.9025: the error is **0.28%**, as expected from the neglected δ² term.
