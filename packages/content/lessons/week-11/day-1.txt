### Mixed worked problems
EX: Limits
1. lim (x → 2) (x² − 4) ÷ (x² − 3x + 2) = (x − 2)(x + 2) ÷ ((x − 1)(x − 2)) = (x + 2) ÷ (x − 1) → **4**.
2. lim (x → ∞) (3x² + 2x) ÷ (5x² − 1): divide by x² to get **3/5**.
3. lim (x → 0) (eˣ − cos x) ÷ sin x is 0 ÷ 0. L'Hôpital: (eˣ + sin x) ÷ cos x → **1**.
4. lim (x → 0) (1 − cos 2x) ÷ x²: use 1 − cos u ≈ u²/2 with u = 2x, giving 2x² ÷ x² = **2**.
/EX

EX: Derivatives
1. From the definition: f(x) = x² + 3x at x = 1: [(1 + h)² + 3(1 + h) − 4] ÷ h = (5h + h²) ÷ h → **5**.
2. Implicit: x² + x y + y² = 7 at (2, 1). Differentiate: 2x + y + x y′ + 2y y′ = 0. At (2, 1): 5 + 4 y′ = 0, so **y′ = −5/4**. (Check the point: 4 + 2 + 1 = 7 ✓.)
3. Logarithmic: y = x^(sin x). ln y = sin x ln x, so y′ = x^(sin x) (cos x ln x + sin x ÷ x).
/EX

EX: Applications
1. **Related rates:** a spherical balloon is inflated at 100 cm³/s. How fast does the radius grow when r = 5 cm? V = (4/3)π r³ gives dV/dt = 4π r² dr/dt, so dr/dt = 100 ÷ (4π × 25) = **0.318 cm/s**.
2. **Optimisation:** an open cylindrical can holds 500 cm³. Minimise the metal. A = π r² + 2π r h with h = 500 ÷ (π r²), so A = π r² + 1000 ÷ r. A′ = 2π r − 1000 ÷ r² = 0 gives r³ = 500 ÷ π = 159.2, **r = 5.42 cm and h = 5.42 cm** (height equals radius). A = 92.3 + 184.5 = **276.8 cm²**. A″ > 0, so it is a minimum.
3. **Newton's method** for x³ − 2x − 5 = 0, from x₀ = 2: f = −1, f′ = 10, so x₁ = 2.1. Then f(2.1) = 0.061, f′ = 11.23, so x₂ = 2.1 − 0.00543 = **2.0946**.
4. **Linearisation:** √26 ≈ 5 + 1 ÷ 10 = **5.1** (true 5.0990).
/EX

TRAP: In related rates, **differentiate first, then substitute** the numbers. In optimisation, always check that the critical point is a max or min (second derivative or sign change) and that the answer lies in the allowed range. With L'Hôpital, check the 0 ÷ 0 form each time.

LVL: Challenge | Combine tools
P: Show that x³ − 2x − 5 = 0 has exactly one real root, then use the answer from Newton's method above to estimate the angle (in radians) whose tangent equals that root. => f′ = 3x² − 2 = 0 at x = ±0.816. The local maximum is f(−0.816) = −0.544 + 1.633 − 5 = −3.91 < 0, so f is negative for every x up to the local minimum at x = 0.816 (where f = −6.09). The arctan series gives 0.4774 − 0.0363 + 0.0050 − 0.0008 + 0.0001 = 0.4454. So the angle is **1.125 rad (64.5°)**.

@section physics | 3 · Physics review: mechanics, work and statics | 45 min

### The map of Weeks 5 to 7
| Idea | Key results |
| Energy | W = ∫ F dx, W = ΔKE, KE = ½ m v², PE = m g h = ½ k x², P = F v |
| Momentum | p = m v, J = ∫ F dt = Δp, conserved with no external force |
| Collisions | Elastic: KE conserved, v₁ − v₂ reverses. Perfectly inelastic: stick together |
| Centre of mass | x_cm = Σ m x ÷ Σ m |
| Statics | ΣF = 0, Στ = 0, τ = F r sin θ |
| Materials | σ = F ÷ A, ε = ΔL ÷ L, σ = E ε |

BN: শক্তি: W = ∫F dx, W = ΔKE, P = Fv। ভরবেগ: p = mv, ঘাত J = Δp, বাইরের বল না থাকলে সংরক্ষিত। সংঘর্ষ: স্থিতিস্থাপকে KE সংরক্ষিত; সম্পূর্ণ অস্থিতিস্থাপকে লেগে যায়। স্থিতিবিদ্যা: ΣF = 0, Στ = 0। উপাদান: σ = F/A, ε = ΔL/L, σ = Eε।

EX: Mixed mechanics
1. A 2 kg block slides from rest down a frictionless ramp from a height of 1.5 m: ½ v² = g h, so v = √(2 × 9.8 × 1.5) = **5.42 m/s**.
2. The same ramp is 30° with μ = 0.2 and 3 m long: a = g (sin θ − μ cos θ) = 9.8 × (0.5 − 0.173) = 3.20 m/s². v = √(2 × 3.20 × 3) = **4.38 m/s**. Without friction the speed would be √(2 × 9.8 × 1.5) = 5.42 m/s (the 3 m ramp drops 1.5 m), so friction lowers it to 4.38 m/s.
3. A 1200 kg car goes from 0 to 27 m/s in 9 s. The average power is KE ÷ t = ½ × 1200 × 729 ÷ 9 = **48.6 kW** (65 horsepower).
4. A 1000 kg car at 20 m/s hits a stationary 1500 kg car and they lock: v = 20,000 ÷ 2500 = **8 m/s**. The energy lost is 200,000 − 80,000 = **120 kJ**.
5. A 0.15 kg ball arrives at 40 m/s and leaves at 30 m/s the opposite way over 5 ms. Δp = 0.15 × 70 = 10.5 N·s, so F = 10.5 ÷ 0.005 = **2100 N**.
6. A uniform 4 m beam of 20 kg is hinged at a wall and held horizontal by a cable attached to its far end at 30° above the beam. Torque about the hinge: T sin 30° × 4 = 20 × 9.8 × 2, so **T = 196 N**.
7. A steel wire (E = 200 GPa, 2 m long, 2 mm diameter) holds a 50 kg load. A = 3.14 × 10⁻⁶ m², σ = 490 ÷ 3.14 × 10⁻⁶ = **156 MPa**, and ΔL = σ L ÷ E = **1.56 mm**.
/EX

P: A 1.5 kg mass moving at 6 m/s collides elastically with a stationary 1.5 kg mass. Find the final velocities. => Equal masses exchange velocities: the first stops and the second moves at **6 m/s**.
LVL: Level 3 | Real

LVL: Challenge | Where does the energy go?

The starting energy is 0.1 × 9.8 × 10 = 9.8 J. After the 5th bounce: 9.8 × 0.64⁵ = 9.8 × 0.107 = **1.05 J**.
