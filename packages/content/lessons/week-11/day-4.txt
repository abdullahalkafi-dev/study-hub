@section math | 2 · Paper 1 (30 marks, 75 minutes) | no calculator

### Section A: limits and derivatives (8 marks)
LVL: Section A | Questions 1 to 5
P: (1 mark) Find lim (x → 1) (x³ − 1) ÷ (x² − 1). => Factor: (x − 1)(x² + x + 1) ÷ ((x − 1)(x + 1)) = (x² + x + 1) ÷ (x + 1) → **3/2**.
P: (1 mark) Find lim (x → 0) (e^(2x) − 1 − 2x) ÷ x². => By series, e^(2x) − 1 − 2x = 2x² + (4/3)x³ + …, so the limit is **2**. (L'Hôpital twice gives 4e^(2x) ÷ 2 → 2.)
P: (2 marks) Differentiate f(x) = (x² + 1) ÷ eˣ and show that f is decreasing for every x. => f = (x² + 1) e^(−x). f′ = 2x e^(−x) − (x² + 1) e^(−x) = −(x² − 2x + 1) e^(−x) = **−(x − 1)² e^(−x)**. This is ≤ 0 for all x (zero only at x = 1), so f is decreasing.
P: (2 marks) Find dy/dx at (1, 2) on the curve y³ + x y = 10. => First check: 8 + 2 = 10 ✓. Differentiate: 3y² y′ + y + x y′ = 0. At (1, 2): 12 y′ + 2 + y′ = 0, so **y′ = −2/13**.
P: (2 marks) A box with no lid is made from a 12 cm by 12 cm square of card by cutting a square of side x from each corner and folding. Find x for the maximum volume, and that volume. => V = x (12 − 2x)². V′ = (12 − 2x)² − 4x (12 − 2x) = (12 − 2x)(12 − 6x) = 0 gives x = 2 (x = 6 gives zero volume). V(2) = 2 × 64 = **128 cm³**. The maximum is at **x = 2 cm**.

### Section B: integration (12 marks)
LVL: Section B | Questions 6 to 12
P: (1 mark) Evaluate ∫₀¹ (6x² − 4x + 3) dx. => [2x³ − 2x² + 3x] = 2 − 2 + 3 = **3**.
P: (2 marks) Evaluate ∫₀^(π/2) x cos x dx. => By parts (u = x): [x sin x] − ∫ sin x dx = π/2 − [−cos x] = π/2 − 1. Answer **π/2 − 1 ≈ 0.571**.
P: (2 marks) Evaluate ∫₀² x ÷ √(x² + 4) dx. => u = x² + 4, du = 2x dx, limits 4 to 8: ½ ∫ u^(−1/2) du = [√u] = √8 − 2 = **2√2 − 2 ≈ 0.828**.
P: (2 marks) Find ∫ (3x + 1) ÷ (x² − 1) dx. => (3x + 1) ÷ ((x − 1)(x + 1)) = A ÷ (x − 1) + B ÷ (x + 1) with A = 4 ÷ 2 = 2 and B = (−2) ÷ (−2) = 1. So **2 ln abs(x − 1) + ln abs(x + 1) + C**.
P: (2 marks) Evaluate ∫₁^∞ (ln x) ÷ x² dx. => By parts: [−(ln x) ÷ x − 1 ÷ x] from 1 to ∞ = 0 − (0 − 1) = **1**. (The limit of (ln t) ÷ t is 0.)
P: (1 mark) Find the area enclosed between y = x and y = x³ for 0 ≤ x ≤ 1. => ∫₀¹ (x − x³) dx = 1/2 − 1/4 = **1/4**.
P: (2 marks) The region under y = e^(−x) from x = 0 to 1 is spun about the x-axis. Find the volume. => V = π ∫₀¹ e^(−2x) dx = π [−e^(−2x) ÷ 2] = **π (1 − e^(−2)) ÷ 2 ≈ 1.358**.

### Section C: series (10 marks)
LVL: Section C | Questions 13 to 17
P: (2 marks) Decide whether Σ (n + 1) ÷ (n³ + 1) converges, with a reason. => For large n the terms behave like 1 ÷ n². The limit comparison with Σ 1 ÷ n² gives a ratio tending to 1, a positive finite number, and Σ 1 ÷ n² converges (p = 2). So it **converges**.
P: (2 marks) Find the radius and the interval of convergence of Σ (x + 2)ⁿ ÷ (n 4ⁿ). => The ratio gives abs(x + 2) ÷ 4, so **R = 4**, centre −2. At x = 2: Σ 1 ÷ n diverges. At x = −6: Σ (−1)ⁿ ÷ n converges. Interval **[−6, 2)**.
P: (2 marks) Write the Maclaurin series of x² e^(−x) up to the term in x⁵. => e^(−x) = 1 − x + x²/2 − x³/6 + …, so **x² − x³ + x⁴/2 − x⁵/6**.
P: (2 marks) Use the first two nonzero terms of the series for cos(x²) to estimate ∫₀^0.5 cos(x²) dx. => cos(x²) = 1 − x⁴/2 + x⁸/24 − …. Integrating the first two terms: [x − x⁵ ÷ 10] = 0.5 − 0.03125 ÷ 10 = **0.496875**. (The next term adds only 0.000009.)
P: (2 marks) Find Σ from n = 1 of (n + 1) ÷ 3ⁿ. => Split: Σ n ÷ 3ⁿ + Σ 1 ÷ 3ⁿ. The first is x ÷ (1 − x)² at x = 1/3: 3/4. The second is (1/3) ÷ (2/3) = 1/2. Total **5/4**.

@section physics | 3 · Marking and exam technique | 25 min

### Mark yourself
- Count your marks out of 30. Use a different colour pen. Be strict: no marks for correct answers with no working when working was asked for.
- Place your score on this scale: **26 to 30** excellent; **21 to 25** good, review the dropped marks; **15 to 20** gaps to close in the next two days; **under 15** you should tell me, so that I can point you to the right earlier days.
- For every lost mark classify it: **K** (did not know the idea), **M** (knew it but made an arithmetic or algebra slip), **T** (ran out of time), **R** (misread the question). Count each type. The most frequent letter tells you what to train.

BN: ৩০ এর মধ্যে নম্বর গোনো, কঠোরভাবে। প্রতিটা হারানো নম্বরকে চার ভাগে ফেলো: K (ধারণা জানা ছিল না), M (জানতাম কিন্তু হিসাবে ভুল), T (সময় শেষ), R (প্রশ্ন ভুল পড়েছি)। যে অক্ষর সবচেয়ে বেশি, সেটাই কাল থেকে অনুশীলন করার জায়গা।

### Technique that gains marks
- **Read the question twice** and underline the command word (find, show, estimate, explain).
- **Show a check** when cheap: a point lies on the curve, a derivative matches, a result is positive.
- **Units and signs** at the end: a negative length is a warning.
- A "show that" question is about the **argument**; the answer is already given.
- If you have 5 minutes left, go back to a question that has a visible method but no final answer.
