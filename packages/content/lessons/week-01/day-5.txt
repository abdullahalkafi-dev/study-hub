@section math | 2 · Math: fractions, proportion, scientific notation | 55 min

### Algebraic fractions: same rules as ordinary fractions
A fraction in x behaves exactly like 6/8 = 3/4. You simplify by **cancelling common factors**, not common terms. To do that you must factor first.

EX: Simplify (x² − 9) ÷ (x² + 3x)
1. Factor the top: x² − 9 = (x − 3)(x + 3).
2. Factor the bottom: x² + 3x = x(x + 3).
3. Cancel the common factor (x + 3): **(x − 3) ÷ x**.
4. This is valid when x ≠ 0 and x ≠ −3, because those values would make the original bottom zero.
/EX

TRAP: You cannot cancel the x's in (x + 3) ÷ x. The x in the top is a **term** (added to 3), not a factor. Test with x = 1: (1 + 3) ÷ 1 = 4, but cancelling would give 3.

**Multiply** fractions top by top and bottom by bottom. **Divide** by flipping the second fraction and multiplying.

EX: Simplify (x + 2)/(x − 1) × (x² − 1)/(x + 2)
1. Factor: x² − 1 = (x − 1)(x + 1).
2. Cancel (x + 2) and (x − 1).
3. Left with **x + 1**.
/EX

**Add or subtract** by finding a common denominator, just like 1/2 + 1/3 = 3/6 + 2/6.

EX: Simplify 1/x + 1/(x + 1)
1. Common denominator: x(x + 1).
2. 1/x = (x + 1) ÷ [x(x + 1)] and 1/(x + 1) = x ÷ [x(x + 1)].
3. Add the tops: **(2x + 1) ÷ [x(x + 1)]**.
/EX

EX: Solve 3/(x − 1) = 2/x
1. Cross-multiply (valid when x ≠ 1 and x ≠ 0): 3x = 2(x − 1).
2. 3x = 2x − 2, so **x = −2**.
3. Check: 3/(−3) = −1 and 2/(−2) = −1 ✓.
/EX

BN: ভগ্নাংশ সরল করতে আগে ফ্যাক্টর করো, তারপর সাধারণ উৎপাদক (factor) কাটো, পদ (term) নয়। (x + 3)/x তে x কাটা যায় না কারণ উপরে x হলো যোগের একটা পদ। যোগ-বিয়োগে ছোট বা সাধারণ হর (common denominator) বানাতে হবে। আর হর শূন্য হলে অর্থহীন, তাই x এর কিছু মান বাদ যায়।

### Ratio and sharing
A **ratio** compares quantities. 3 : 5 means "for every 3 of one, 5 of the other". To share 640 taka in the ratio 3 : 5, add the parts (3 + 5 = 8), find one part (640 ÷ 8 = 80), and multiply: **240 and 400**.

### Variation: how one quantity depends on another
- **Direct variation:** y = kx. Double x and y doubles. Example: distance at constant speed.
- **Inverse variation:** y = k/x. Double x and y halves. Example: time to do a job versus the number of workers.
- **Square variation:** y = kx². Double x and y quadruples. Example: braking distance versus speed, which you met yesterday.

The constant k is found from one data point, then used for the rest.

EX: Inverse variation
1. Six workers finish a job in 10 days. How long for 15 workers (same pace)?
2. y = k/x with y = days and x = workers: 10 = k/6, so k = 60 worker-days.
3. With 15 workers: 60 ÷ 15 = **4 days**.
/EX

EX: Square variation
1. y varies as the square of x. y = 18 when x = 3. Find y when x = 5.
2. y = kx²: 18 = 9k, so k = 2.
3. y = 2 × 25 = **50**.
/EX

BN: দুটো রাশির সম্পর্ক তিন রকম: সরাসরি (y = kx, একটা দ্বিগুণ হলে অন্যটাও দ্বিগুণ), বিপরীত (y = k/x, একটা দ্বিগুণ হলে অন্যটা অর্ধেক), আর বর্গ (y = kx², একটা দ্বিগুণ হলে অন্যটা চার গুণ)। পদার্থবিজ্ঞানে এই তিনটা বারবার আসবে। প্রথমে একটা তথ্য দিয়ে k বের করো।

### Scientific notation and orders of magnitude
Engineers handle numbers from the size of an atom (about 10⁻¹⁰ m) to the size of the Sun's distance (1.5 × 10¹¹ m). We write them as **a × 10ⁿ** with 1 ≤ a < 10.

- 4,500,000 = 4.5 × 10⁶
- 0.00032 = 3.2 × 10⁻⁴

Multiplying: multiply the a's and **add** the powers (exponent rule 1 from Monday).

EX: Multiply (3 × 10⁸)(5 × 10²)
1. 3 × 5 = 15 and 10⁸ × 10² = 10¹⁰.
2. That is 15 × 10¹⁰, which is not in standard form. Rewrite 15 = 1.5 × 10¹.
3. **1.5 × 10¹¹**.
/EX

EX: Divide (6 × 10⁵) ÷ (2 × 10⁻³)
1. 6 ÷ 2 = 3 and 10⁵ ÷ 10⁻³ = 10⁵⁻⁽⁻³⁾ = 10⁸.
2. **3 × 10⁸**.
/EX

**Estimation.** A quick estimate catches big mistakes. Example: how many seconds are there in 80 years? One year is about 365 × 24 × 3600 ≈ 3.15 × 10⁷ s. So 80 years ≈ 80 × 3.15 × 10⁷ = **2.5 × 10⁹ s**, about 2.5 billion seconds.

KEY: Before trusting any calculation, ask "roughly how big should the answer be?" The order of magnitude (the power of ten) is often all you need to know a number is wrong.

### Multi-stage motion
Real motion happens in stages, such as speeding up, cruising, braking. Treat each stage separately. The **final velocity of one stage is the starting velocity of the next**.

EX: A train between two stations
1. Stage 1: from rest, a = 1 m/s² until it reaches 20 m/s. Time 20 s, distance ½ × 1 × 400 = 200 m.
2. Stage 2: cruises at 20 m/s for 60 s, covering 1200 m.
3. Stage 3: brakes at −2 m/s² from 20 m/s to rest. Time 10 s, distance 400 ÷ 4 = 100 m.
4. Total distance **1500 m**, total time **90 s**, average speed 1500 ÷ 90 = **16.7 m/s**, which is less than the cruising speed.
/EX

### One object chasing another
When two objects meet, they are at the **same place at the same time**. Write each one's position as a function of time, then set them equal.

EX: Catching up
1. Car B drives at constant 20 m/s. Car A starts from rest at the same place at the same moment with a = 2 m/s². When does A catch B?
2. Position of A: x_A = ½ × 2 × t² = t². Position of B: x_B = 20t.
3. Set equal: t² = 20t, so t² − 20t = 0 and t(t − 20) = 0. **t = 0 (the start) or t = 20 s.**
4. Distance: x = 20 × 20 = **400 m**. At that moment A's velocity is 2 × 20 = 40 m/s, twice B's, so A overtakes.
/EX

GRAPH: A chases B | time (s) | x (m) | 0,0 5,25 10,100 15,225 20,400 25,625 ;; 0,0 5,100 10,200 15,300 20,400 25,500 | The straight line is B at constant velocity. The curve is A, accelerating from rest. They meet at 20 s at 400 m. Before that, B is ahead. After that, A is ahead.

BN: দুটো বস্তু যখন মিলিত হয়, তখন একই সময়ে একই জায়গায় থাকে। প্রত্যেকের অবস্থান (x) সময়ের সাপেক্ষে লিখে সমান করো। এখানে সোমবার থেকে শেখা সব কাজে লাগল: সূত্র, একটা দ্বিঘাত সমীকরণ, ফ্যাক্টর করা। t = 0 হলো শুরুর মুহূর্ত, t = 20 হলো ধরে ফেলার মুহূর্ত।

KEY: Strategy for any kinematics problem: draw it → list knowns with signs → decide stages → pick the equation that fits the unknown → solve → check with a second route or an estimate.

### Practice
LVL: Level 1 | Warm-up
P: A cyclist travels 600 m at 6 m/s and then 400 m at 4 m/s. Find the total time. => 100 s + 100 s = **200 s**.
P: A car goes from rest at 2 m/s² for 8 s, then at constant speed for 5 s. Find the total distance. => Stage 1: ½ × 2 × 64 = 64 m, v = 16 m/s. Stage 2: 16 × 5 = 80 m. Total **144 m**.
LVL: Level 2 | Meeting problems
P: A goods train 200 m long passes a pole in 20 s at constant speed. What is its speed in km/h? => 200 ÷ 20 = 10 m/s = **36 km/h**.
P: A is at 0 m moving at 3 m/s. B starts at 100 m moving towards A at 2 m/s. When and where do they meet? => 3t = 100 − 2t, so t = **20 s**. Position = 60 m from A's start.
P: A truck at 15 m/s passes a stationary police car, which then accelerates at 3 m/s². When does the police car catch the truck? => 15t = ½ × 3t², so 1.5t² − 15t = 0, giving t = **10 s**. They are 150 m away.
LVL: Challenge | Take-off and landing
P: A plane accelerates from rest at 4 m/s² along a runway. It takes off at 80 m/s. (a) How long and how far? A pilot who aborts brakes at −5 m/s² from 80 m/s. (b) What stopping distance is needed? (c) What minimum runway does the plane need if it must be able to abort at the last moment? => (a) t = 80 ÷ 4 = **20 s**, s = 6400 ÷ 8 = **800 m**. (b) s = 6400 ÷ 10 = **640 m**. (c) Accelerate 800 m, then stop 640 m: **1440 m**. Real runways add large safety margins on top.
