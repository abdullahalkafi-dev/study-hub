@section math | 2 · Math: expanding, factoring and solving equations | 55 min

### The distributive law: where everything starts
a(b + c) = ab + ac. The reason is an area. Draw a rectangle with height a and width (b + c). Split it into two rectangles, one with width b and one with width c. The total area is the sum of the two parts: ab + ac.

EQ: a(b + c) = ab + ac

EX: Expand 3x(2x − 5)
1. Multiply 3x by each term inside the bracket.
2. 3x · 2x = 6x² and 3x · (−5) = −15x.
3. Result: **6x² − 15x**.
/EX

### Two brackets
To expand (x + 3)(x + 4), multiply every term in the first bracket by every term in the second. That is four products: x·x, x·4, 3·x, 3·4.

EX: Expand (x + 3)(x + 4)
1. x · x = x².
2. x · 4 = 4x.
3. 3 · x = 3x.
4. 3 · 4 = 12. Add: x² + 4x + 3x + 12 = **x² + 7x + 12**.
/EX

BN: দুটো বন্ধনী গুণ করার সময় প্রথম বন্ধনীর প্রতিটা পদকে দ্বিতীয় বন্ধনীর প্রতিটা পদ দিয়ে গুণ করো। এখানে মোট ৪টা গুণ হয়। তারপর একই ধরনের পদ (like terms) যোগ করো।

### Three identities to know by heart
EQ: (a + b)² = a² + 2ab + b²
EQ: (a − b)² = a² − 2ab + b²
EQ: (a + b)(a − b) = a² − b²

**Why the first one is true.** Draw a square with side (a + b). Its area is (a + b)². Cut it into four pieces: an a×a square, a b×b square, and two a×b rectangles. The total area is a² + b² + 2ab.

**Why the third one is true.** Expand it: a·a − a·b + b·a − b·b. The two middle terms cancel, leaving a² − b².

TRAP: (a + b)² is NOT a² + b². The middle term 2ab is the one students forget. Check with a = 3, b = 4: (3 + 4)² = 49, while 3² + 4² = 25. The gap is 2 · 3 · 4 = 24.

### Factoring: running expansion backwards
**Factoring** means writing an expression as a product. Always try the methods in this order:

1. **Common factor.** 6x² − 15x = 3x(2x − 5).
2. **Difference of squares.** x² − 49 = (x − 7)(x + 7).
3. **Perfect squares.** x² + 6x + 9 = (x + 3)².
4. **Trinomial x² + bx + c.** Find two numbers that multiply to c and add to b.

EX: Factor x² + 7x + 12
1. We need two numbers that multiply to 12 and add to 7.
2. Pairs of 12: (1, 12) adds to 13, (2, 6) adds to 8, (3, 4) adds to 7 ✓.
3. So x² + 7x + 12 = **(x + 3)(x + 4)**. You already expanded this above, so you can check it.
/EX

EX: Factor x² − 2x − 15
1. Multiply to −15, add to −2. One number is positive and one negative, and the bigger one is negative.
2. Try (3, −5): 3 · (−5) = −15 and 3 + (−5) = −2 ✓.
3. Answer: **(x + 3)(x − 5)**.
/EX

EX: Factor 2x² + 7x + 3
1. When the front number is not 1, multiply it by the last number: 2 · 3 = 6. Find two numbers that multiply to 6 and add to 7: 1 and 6.
2. Split the middle term: 2x² + x + 6x + 3.
3. Group: x(2x + 1) + 3(2x + 1).
4. Take out the common bracket: **(2x + 1)(x + 3)**. Expand it to check.
/EX

BN: ফ্যাক্টর করা মানে গুণফলের রূপে লেখা। কোনটা আগে করবে তার ক্রম: (১) সাধারণ উৎপাদক, (২) a²−b² ধরন, (৩) পূর্ণ বর্গ, (৪) x² + bx + c এর জন্য এমন দুটো সংখ্যা খোঁজো যাদের গুণফল c আর যোগফল b। উত্তর ঠিক কি না তা বের করার সহজ উপায়: আবার গুণ করে (expand) দেখো।

### Solving linear equations: the balance idea
An equation is a balanced scale. Whatever you do to one side you must do to the other. The goal is to get x alone.

EX: Solve 3(2x − 1) = 4x + 9
1. Expand the left side: 6x − 3 = 4x + 9.
2. Move the x terms left: 6x − 4x − 3 = 9, so 2x − 3 = 9.
3. Add 3 to both sides: 2x = 12.
4. Divide by 2: **x = 6**. Check: left = 3(11) = 33, right = 24 + 9 = 33 ✓.
/EX

### Equations with fractions
Multiply every term by the lowest common denominator (LCD) to clear the fractions.

EX: Solve x/2 + x/3 = 10
1. The LCD of 2 and 3 is 6. Multiply every term by 6: 3x + 2x = 60.
2. 5x = 60, so **x = 12**. Check: 6 + 4 = 10 ✓.
/EX

### A preview: the zero-product rule
If a product is zero, at least one factor is zero. So if (x − 2)(x − 3) = 0, then x − 2 = 0 or x − 3 = 0, giving x = 2 or x = 3. This is why factoring solves quadratic equations. We will use it heavily in Week 2.

KEY: Factor → set each bracket to zero → solve. That is the whole idea behind solving many quadratics.

BN: যদি দুটো জিনিসের গুণফল শূন্য হয়, তাহলে অন্তত একটা শূন্য। তাই (x−2)(x−3) = 0 হলে x = 2 বা x = 3। এই কারণেই ফ্যাক্টর করা দিয়ে দ্বিঘাত সমীকরণ সমাধান করা যায়। এটা আমরা সামনের সপ্তাহে ভালো করে করব।

### Acceleration: how fast velocity changes
Velocity can change because you speed up, slow down or turn. **Acceleration** measures how fast velocity changes. In a straight line:

EQ: a = (v − u) ÷ t     (u = starting velocity, v = final velocity, t = time taken)

The unit is (m/s) ÷ s = **m/s²**. An acceleration of 2 m/s² means "every second, the velocity grows by 2 m/s".

EX: A sprinter
1. A sprinter goes from rest (u = 0) to 10 m/s in 2.5 s.
2. a = (10 − 0) ÷ 2.5 = **4 m/s²**.
3. Meaning: each second her velocity increases by 4 m/s.
/EX

EX: A braking car
1. A car slows from 25 m/s to 5 m/s in 4 s.
2. a = (5 − 25) ÷ 4 = **−5 m/s²**.
3. The negative sign means the acceleration points opposite to the motion. Slowing down is a negative acceleration when forward is positive.
/EX

BN: ত্বরণ (acceleration) মানে প্রতি সেকেন্ডে বেগ কতটা বদলায়। a = (v − u) ÷ t। একক m/s²। ঋণাত্মক চিহ্ন মানে গাড়ি ধীর হচ্ছে (যখন সামনের দিক ধনাত্মক ধরেছ)। "ত্বরণ ঋণাত্মক" মানে "গাড়ি পিছনে যাচ্ছে" এমন নয়।

### Position-time graphs (x–t)
Put time on the horizontal axis and position on the vertical axis. The **slope** (rise ÷ run) of the line is the velocity. A flat line means standing still. A steeper line means faster. A line going down means moving backwards.

GRAPH: A walk: out, rest, back | time (s) | x (m) | 0,0 4,8 8,8 12,0 | The slope on the first segment is 8 ÷ 4 = +2 m/s. The flat part has slope 0, so velocity 0. On the last segment the slope is (0 − 8) ÷ 4 = −2 m/s: coming back.

### Velocity-time graphs (v–t): the most useful picture in mechanics
Now put velocity on the vertical axis. Two facts to remember forever:

KEY: On a v–t graph, the **slope** is the acceleration, and the **area under the line** is the displacement.

**Why the area is displacement.** For a constant velocity, distance = v × t, which is exactly the area of a rectangle with height v and width t. For a changing velocity, split the time into tiny strips: each strip is a tiny rectangle, and the total area is the total displacement. You will do this properly with integration in Week 8.

GRAPH: A car's journey | time (s) | v (m/s) | 0,0 5,10 10,10 14,0 | The car accelerates for 5 s, cruises for 5 s, then brakes for 4 s.

EX: Read the graph
1. Acceleration (0 to 5 s): slope = (10 − 0) ÷ 5 = **2 m/s²**.
2. Cruising (5 to 10 s): slope = 0, so **a = 0**.
3. Braking (10 to 14 s): slope = (0 − 10) ÷ 4 = **−2.5 m/s²**.
4. Distance = area. Triangle ½ × 5 × 10 = 25 m. Rectangle 5 × 10 = 50 m. Triangle ½ × 4 × 10 = 20 m. Total = **95 m**.
/EX

BN: v–t গ্রাফের দুটো নিয়ম মনে রাখো: ঢাল (slope) = ত্বরণ, আর রেখার নিচের ক্ষেত্রফল (area) = সরণ। রেক্ট্যাঙ্গলের ক্ষেত্রফল = উচ্চতা × প্রস্থ = বেগ × সময় = দূরত্ব। এই কারণেই ক্ষেত্রফল সরণ। ত্রিভুজ হলে ½ × ভূমি × উচ্চতা।

### Recap
- Expanding: multiply every term by every term. (a ± b)² = a² ± 2ab + b². (a + b)(a − b) = a² − b².
- Factoring order: common factor → difference of squares → x² + bx + c → grouping.
- Equations: do the same thing to both sides. Clear fractions with the LCD. Check your answer.
- Zero-product rule: product = 0 means a factor = 0.
- a = (v − u) ÷ t in m/s². On a v–t graph: slope = acceleration, area = displacement.
