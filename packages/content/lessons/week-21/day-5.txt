# Fourier series for heat and strings. Then: the electric motor
lead: The first use of Fourier series was not signals but heat. Fourier wanted to know how an iron bar cools, and found that any starting temperature profile is a sum of simple sine shapes, and each shape fades away at its own speed, the higher ones much faster. The same idea explains a plucked guitar string: a sum of standing waves. In physics, you put the magnetic force on a current loop to work: a torque, which spins the coil of a motor. Today's story is the electric motor, from the torque on one turn of wire to the speed and power of a real DC motor.

BN: ফুরিয়ে ধারা প্রথম ব্যবহার হয় সংকেতে নয়, তাপে। লোহার দণ্ড কীভাবে ঠান্ডা হয়? যেকোনো প্রাথমিক তাপমাত্রা প্রোফাইল সরল সাইন আকৃতির যোগফল, আর প্রতিটা আকৃতি নিজের গতিতে ক্ষীণ হয়, উঁচু গুলো অনেক দ্রুত। একই ধারণায় গিটারের টানা তার = দাঁড়ানো তরঙ্গের যোগফল। পদার্থবিজ্ঞানে কারেন্ট লুপে চৌম্বক বল থেকে টর্ক, যা মোটরের কয়েল ঘোরায়। আজকের গল্প বৈদ্যুতিক মোটর: এক পাকের টর্ক থেকে বাস্তব DC মোটরের গতি ও ক্ষমতা।

@section math | 2 · Math: heat in a rod and a plucked string | 50 min

### Heat flow
A thin rod of length π has its ends held at temperature 0 (the ambient level) and starts with the temperature profile u (x, 0) = f (x). The temperature u (x, t) obeys the **heat equation** ∂u/∂t = α ∂²u/∂x² (α is the thermal diffusivity). Try u = X (x) T (t): then T′ ÷ (α T) = X″ ÷ X, which is a constant −n². The solutions that vanish at both ends are X = sin nx and T = e^(−α n² t). Add them all up:

EQ: u (x, t) = Σ bₙ e^(−α n² t) sin nx, where bₙ are the sine-series coefficients of f

**Mode n decays n² times faster than mode 1.** So any bumpy start becomes smooth very quickly, and after a while only the lowest mode, a single half sine, remains.

EX: A rod heated evenly
f = 1 on (0, π) (a square wave). bₙ = 4 ÷ (nπ) for odd n. Let τ = α t.
1. **At the centre** (x = π ÷ 2): u = (4 ÷ π)(e^(−τ) − e^(−9τ) ÷ 3 + e^(−25τ) ÷ 5 − …).
2. **τ = 0.5:** (4 ÷ π)(0.6065 − 0.0037 + 0) = **0.768**.
3. **τ = 1:** (4 ÷ π)(0.3679 − 0.0000) = **0.468**. After this the whole rod is a half sine: the third mode is e^(−8τ) = 0.03% of the first.
4. **Time for the centre to fall to 0.5:** (4 ÷ π) e^(−τ) = 0.5 gives τ = ln (8 ÷ π) = **0.935**.
5. **In real units**, for a rod of length L the slowest decay time is τ₁ = L² ÷ (α π²). For a 0.5 m copper rod (α = 1.11 × 10⁻⁴ m²/s): **228 s**, which is 3.8 minutes. For iron (α = 2.3 × 10⁻⁵): **1101 s**, which is 18 minutes. Mode 3 lasts 9 times less: 25 s and 122 s.
6. **Why it matters:** no sharp temperature step survives. Heat smooths every detail quickly, and the smaller the detail (the higher n), the faster it goes (as n²).
/EX

### A plucked guitar string
A string of length L fixed at both ends obeys the wave equation y_tt = c² y_xx, with c = √(T ÷ μ). Its motion is a sum of standing waves:

EQ: y (x, t) = Σ bₙ sin (n π x ÷ L) cos (n π c t ÷ L), with fₙ = n c ÷ (2L)

If it is plucked at the fraction p of its length to a height h (a triangle), the amplitudes are **bₙ = 2 h sin (n π p) ÷ (n² π² p (1 − p))**. Plucking at the middle (p = ½) gives bₙ = 8 h ÷ (π² n²) for n = 1, 3, 5 with alternating sign, and all even modes **missing**. The harmonics fall as 1 ÷ n² (the shape has a corner, but no jump). A pluck at p = 1/5 kills the 5th harmonic, 10th, …

BN: তাপ সমীকরণ u_t = αu_xx; বিচ্ছিন্ন করে u = Σ bₙ e^(−αn²t) sin nx; n-তম মোড n² গুণ দ্রুত ক্ষীণ হয়। দৈর্ঘ্য L হলে τ₁ = L²/(απ²)। গিটার: y = Σ bₙ sin(nπx/L) cos(nπct/L), fₙ = nc/(2L); p অংশে টানলে bₙ = 2h sin(nπp)/(n²π²p(1−p)); মাঝখানে টানলে জোড় হারমোনিক নেই।

EX: The top string of a guitar
L = 0.65 m, μ = 0.4 g/m and T = 73.5 N, plucked 3 mm at the middle.
1. **Speed:** c = √(73.5 ÷ 0.0004) = **428.7 m/s**. The fundamental is f₁ = 428.7 ÷ 1.3 = **329.7 Hz** (the note E4). Harmonics at 659, 989, 1319 Hz.
2. **Amplitudes** (middle pluck): b₁ = 8 × 3 ÷ π² = **2.43 mm**, b₃ = **−0.270 mm**, b₅ = **+0.097 mm**, and b₂ = b₄ = 0.
3. **A pluck at one-fifth of the length** (near the bridge) gives b₁ = 2.23 mm, b₂ = 0.90 mm, b₃ = 0.40 mm, b₄ = 0.14 mm and b₅ = 0. More high harmonics, so the sound is sharper and brighter. This is why guitarists pluck near the bridge for a bright sound and over the soundhole (the middle) for a soft, round one.
4. **Energy of the first harmonic** = b₁² ÷ Σ bₙ²: for the middle pluck about **98.6%**, so it sounds nearly pure.
/EX

TRAP: In the heat equation, the exponent is **n² α t**, not n α t. Each mode keeps its own shape and only its size changes. The boundary condition decides the shape of the modes: fixed ends mean sines. A guitar string's amplitude formula has n² in the denominator, so the harmonics fall quickly, but a string plucked at p has the harmonics with n p a whole number **missing**.

@section physics | 3 · Physics: torque on a loop and the DC motor | 40 min

### Torque on a current loop
A rectangular loop with N turns, area A and current I sits in a field B. The two long sides feel opposite forces F = N I a B (a = the length of the side), which make a torque. The loop has a **magnetic moment** μ = N I A (pointing along its normal) and the torque is

EQ: τ = N I A B sin θ, **τ** = **μ** × **B**

where θ is the angle between the normal and B. The torque is **maximum (N I A B)** when the loop's plane is parallel to B (θ = 90°) and **zero** when it is perpendicular (θ = 0): the loop turns until its moment lines up with B (like a compass needle).

### The DC motor
A **commutator** (a split ring with brushes) reverses the current in the coil every half turn, so the torque keeps pushing in the same direction, and the coil keeps turning. For N turns of angular speed ω, the coil cuts field lines and generates a **back EMF** ε_b = k ω that **opposes** the supply (Lenz's law), where k (V s/rad) is the motor constant. The same k links torque and current: **τ = k I**. With a supply V and coil resistance R:

EQ: I = (V − k ω) ÷ R, τ = k I, P_out = τ ω = ε_b I, P_in = V I, efficiency η = ε_b ÷ V = ω ÷ ω₀

where ω₀ = V ÷ k is the no-load speed. As the motor speeds up, the back EMF grows, the current falls, and the torque falls: it settles where the torque equals the load.

BN: লুপে টর্ক τ = NIAB sin θ (চৌম্বক ভ্রামক μ = NIA)। কমিউটেটর প্রতি অর্ধ-পাকে কারেন্ট উল্টায়, তাই টর্ক একই দিকে। ঘূর্ণনে ব্যাক EMF ε_b = kω যা উৎসের বিরুদ্ধে; I = (V − kω)/R, τ = kI, P_out = ε_b I, দক্ষতা η = ω/ω₀, ω₀ = V/k।

EX: Torque on a coil
N = 100 turns, 4 cm × 5 cm (A = 2 × 10⁻³ m²), I = 2 A and B = 0.3 T.
1. **Magnetic moment:** μ = 100 × 2 × 2 × 10⁻³ = **0.4 A m²**.
2. **Maximum torque:** 0.4 × 0.3 = **0.12 N m**.
3. **Average over a half turn** (the commutator keeps the torque positive): (2 ÷ π) × 0.12 = **0.0764 N m**, since the average of |sin θ| is 2 ÷ π.
4. **Power at 3000 rpm:** ω = 314 rad/s, so P = τ ω = 0.0764 × 314 = **24 W** if the torque were constant. Real motors use many coils at different angles, so the torque is smooth.
/EX

EX: A small 12 V DC motor
V = 12 V, R = 1 Ω and k = 0.02 V s/rad.
1. **Stall** (ω = 0, no back EMF): I = 12 ÷ 1 = **12 A** and τ = k I = **0.24 N m**. The heat is I² R = **144 W**: a jammed motor burns out in seconds, which is why motors have fuses and why a motor takes a big current at the instant it starts.
2. **No-load speed:** ω₀ = 12 ÷ 0.02 = **600 rad/s = 5730 rpm**, with no current and no torque in theory.
3. **At ω = 400 rad/s:** ε_b = 8 V, **I = 4 A**, τ = **0.08 N m**, P_out = 8 × 4 = **32 W**, P_in = 48 W, efficiency **66.7%** = 400 ÷ 600.
4. **Maximum power at half the no-load speed** (ω = 300): ε_b = 6 V, I = 6 A, τ = 0.12 N m, **P_out = 36 W** = V² ÷ (4R). But the efficiency is only **50%**. Real motors run at a higher speed to be more efficient.
5. **Real numbers:** an electric car motor of 100 kW at 3000 rpm produces τ = 100,000 ÷ 314 = **318 N m**.
/EX

TRAP: The torque on a loop is zero when its plane is **perpendicular** to the field (θ = 0), maximum when parallel. A motor does not draw V ÷ R when running: the back EMF reduces the current, so V ÷ R is only the **starting** (stall) current. Maximum power is at half the no-load speed, but efficiency there is only 50%. The commutator does not change the magnitude of the average torque by itself: it keeps the sign.
