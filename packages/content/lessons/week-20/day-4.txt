# Numerical methods: Euler, improved Euler and Runge-Kutta. Then: alternating current
lead: Most real differential equations have no formula. A computer steps through time with small steps, and the question is how much accuracy each step buys. Euler's method is the idea, improved Euler is a cleverer step, and Runge-Kutta (RK4) is the workhorse of science and engineering. In physics, you meet alternating current: the mains in your wall is a sine wave, and resistors, inductors and capacitors respond to it with different strengths and different timing.

BN: বেশিরভাগ বাস্তব অবকল সমীকরণের সূত্র নেই। কম্পিউটার ছোট ছোট ধাপে এগোয়; প্রশ্ন হলো প্রতি ধাপে কতটা নির্ভুলতা মেলে। অয়লার হলো মূল ধারণা, উন্নত অয়লার চালাক ধাপ, আর রুঙ্গে-কুট্টা (RK4) বিজ্ঞান-প্রকৌশলের প্রধান ঘোড়া। পদার্থবিজ্ঞানে পরিবর্তী প্রবাহ (AC): দেয়ালের মেইন একটা সাইন তরঙ্গ, আর রোধ, ইনডাক্টর ও ক্যাপাসিটর তাতে ভিন্ন শক্তি ও ভিন্ন সময়ে সাড়া দেয়।

@section math | 2 · Math: three ways to step | 55 min

### The test problem
Take y′ = −2y with y (0) = 1. The exact answer at t = 1 is e⁻² = **0.135335**. A numerical method with step h = 1 ÷ N takes N steps, and each step multiplies y by a fixed factor.

- **Euler** follows the slope at the start of the step: factor **1 + z** with z = −2h. Cheap, error ∝ h (first order).
- **Improved Euler (Heun)** averages the slope at the start and the predicted slope at the end: factor **1 + z + z²/2**. Error ∝ h² (second order).
- **Runge-Kutta 4 (RK4)** uses four slopes per step: k₁ at the start, k₂ and k₃ at the midpoint, k₄ at the end, and y_(n+1) = y_n + (h ÷ 6)(k₁ + 2k₂ + 2k₃ + k₄). Its factor matches the Taylor series up to **z⁴ ÷ 24**, so the error ∝ h⁴ (fourth order).

BN: Euler: ধাপের শুরুর ঢাল; ফ্যাক্টর 1 + z (ত্রুটি ∝ h)। Heun: শুরু ও শেষের ঢালের গড়; ফ্যাক্টর 1 + z + z²/2 (ত্রুটি ∝ h²)। RK4: চারটা ঢাল k₁, k₂, k₃, k₄; y_(n+1) = y_n + (h/6)(k₁ + 2k₂ + 2k₃ + k₄), ত্রুটি ∝ h⁴।

EX: Error against the step size
| Steps N | Euler | Heun | RK4 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | 0.0625 (error 0.0728) | 0.1526 (error 0.0173) | 0.13555 (error 0.00021) |
| 8 | 0.1001 (error 0.0352) | 0.13878 (error 0.0034) | 0.135346 (error 0.000011) |
| 16 | 0.1181 (error 0.0173) | 0.13611 (error 0.00078) | 0.1353359 (error 0.0000006) |
1. **Halving h:** the Euler error halves (ratio about 2), the Heun error falls by about 4 to 5, and the RK4 error falls by about 16 to 20.
2. **Cost versus accuracy:** RK4 costs four slope evaluations per step, but with 4 steps it is already about 80 times more accurate than Euler with 16 steps. For a given accuracy, RK4 is much cheaper.
/EX

EX: A direct step by hand
Solve y′ = x + y with y (0) = 1 up to x = 0.2 (the exact answer is 2 eˣ − x − 1 = **1.242806**).
1. **Euler, h = 0.1:** y₁ = 1 + 0.1 × 1 = 1.1; y₂ = 1.1 + 0.1 × (0.1 + 1.1) = **1.22**. The error is 0.0228.
2. **Heun, h = 0.1:** step 1: k₁ = 1, the predictor is 1.1, k₂ = f (0.1, 1.1) = 1.2, y₁ = 1 + 0.05 × (1 + 1.2) = **1.11**. Step 2: k₁ = 1.21, the predictor is 1.231, k₂ = f (0.2, 1.231) = 1.431, y₂ = 1.11 + 0.05 × (1.21 + 1.431) = **1.24205**. The error is 0.0008.
3. **RK4, one step h = 0.2:** k₁ = 1, k₂ = f (0.1, 1.1) = 1.2, k₃ = f (0.1, 1.12) = 1.22, k₄ = f (0.2, 1.244) = 1.444. y = 1 + (0.2 ÷ 6)(1 + 2.4 + 2.44 + 1.444) = **1.2428**. The error is 6 × 10⁻⁶ with a single step.
/EX

EX: Why Euler fails for oscillators
Take x′ = v, v′ = −x (the exact motion is a circle with constant amplitude 1).
1. **Euler's step** multiplies the amplitude by √(1 + h²) each time: for h = 0.1 over one period (63 steps) the amplitude grows to (1.01)^(31.4) = **1.37**, and the energy grows by **87%**. The planet spirals outward.
2. **RK4's step** multiplies the amplitude by about (1 − h⁶ ÷ 72)^(1/2) per step, so over a period the amplitude is **0.9999996**: practically no drift.
3. **Lesson:** for long simulations of oscillation or orbits, never use plain Euler. This is why physics engines and space missions use RK4 or special energy-preserving methods.
/EX

TRAP: A smaller step does not mean an exact answer, only a smaller error, and the error of Euler is only first order. Too large a step can make the method unstable (for y′ = −2y, Euler with h > 1 oscillates and grows). Do not take the four slopes of RK4 from the start of the step: k₂ and k₃ use the midpoint and k₄ the end. Also Euler's error accumulates: halving the step doubles the work and halves the error, nothing more.

### The mains
A generator in a power station produces **v (t) = V_p sin ωt** with ω = 2π f. The mains frequency is **50 Hz** in Bangladesh and Europe, 60 Hz in America. The meaningful number is not the peak but the **RMS** (root mean square), the constant voltage that would heat a resistor equally: since the average of sin² is ½,

EQ: V_rms = V_p ÷ √2, I_rms = I_p ÷ √2, P_avg = V_rms² ÷ R

So the "230 V" of your wall is an RMS value: the peak is 230 √2 = **325 V**.

For a resistor, current and voltage are in phase. The inductor and capacitor respond with a **reactance** (opposition measured in ohms):
- **Inductor:** X_L = ω L. The current **lags** the voltage by 90° (the inductor opposes change; higher frequency means more opposition).
- **Capacitor:** X_C = 1 ÷ (ω C). The current **leads** the voltage by 90° (high frequency means an easy passage).
In complex notation (Week 12): Z_R = R, Z_L = i ω L, Z_C = 1 ÷ (i ω C), and I = V ÷ Z for each.

BN: v = V_p sin ωt, ω = 2πf; বাংলাদেশে 50 Hz। RMS: V_rms = V_p/√2, তাই 230 V এর পিক 325 V; P_avg = V_rms²/R। রোধে কারেন্ট ও ভোল্টেজ একই দশায়। ইনডাক্টর: X_L = ωL, কারেন্ট 90° পিছিয়ে; ক্যাপাসিটর: X_C = 1/(ωC), কারেন্ট 90° এগিয়ে। জটিল সংখ্যায় Z_L = iωL, Z_C = 1/(iωC)।

EX: The wall socket
1. **Peak voltage:** 230 × √2 = **325 V**. The period is 1 ÷ 50 = **20 ms**.
2. **A resistive heater of 50 Ω:** I_rms = 230 ÷ 50 = **4.6 A**, I_peak = **6.5 A**, P = 230² ÷ 50 = **1058 W**.
3. **A coil of 0.1 H at 50 Hz:** X_L = 2π × 50 × 0.1 = **31.4 Ω**. At 1 kHz: 628 Ω. A choke passes slow signals and blocks fast ones.
4. **A capacitor of 100 μF at 50 Hz:** X_C = 1 ÷ (314.16 × 10⁻⁴) = **31.8 Ω**. At 1 kHz: 1.6 Ω.
5. **Resistor and inductor in series:** R = 30 Ω and X_L = 40 Ω. The total opposition is the impedance |Z| = √(30² + 40²) = **50 Ω**, the current is 230 ÷ 50 = **4.6 A**, and the current lags the voltage by atan (40 ÷ 30) = **53.1°**.
6. **R = 100 Ω with L = 0.2 H at 50 Hz:** X_L = 62.8 Ω, |Z| = 118.1 Ω, I = **1.95 A** and the lag is **32.1°**.
/EX

TRAP: Mains "230 V" is an RMS value, so the peak is 325 V. The average of a sine is zero: the power uses the RMS, never the plain average. Impedances do **not** add arithmetically: R = 30 and X_L = 40 give 50, not 70, because the phases differ by 90°. "ELI the ICE man": voltage E leads current I in an inductor L (ELI), and current leads voltage in a capacitor C (ICE).

NOTES:

### Recap
- Euler: first order, factor 1 + z. Heun: second order, 1 + z + z²/2. RK4: fourth order, (h ÷ 6)(k₁ + 2k₂ + 2k₃ + k₄). Halving h: ratio 2, 4, 16. Euler gains energy in oscillators; use RK4.
- AC: v = V_p sin ωt, V_rms = V_p ÷ √2, P = V_rms² ÷ R. X_L = ωL (current lags 90°), X_C = 1 ÷ ωC (current leads 90°). R and X combine as √(R² + X²) because of the 90° phase.

BN: আজকের সারকথা: Euler প্রথম ক্রম, Heun দ্বিতীয়, RK4 চতুর্থ; h অর্ধেক করলে ত্রুটি 2, 4, 16 গুণ কমে। দোলকে Euler শক্তি বাড়ায়। AC: V_rms = V_p/√2; X_L = ωL, X_C = 1/ωC; R ও X মিলে √(R² + X²)।
