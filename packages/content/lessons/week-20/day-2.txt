# Solving differential equations with Laplace, partial fractions and switches. Then: the RL circuit
lead: Yesterday you built the table. Today you use it as a machine: any linear equation with constant coefficients becomes algebra, partial fractions split the answer into table entries, and the switch-on of a source (a step) is handled by a shift rule. In physics, you combine an inductor with a resistor and meet the RL circuit, the mirror of last week's RC circuit, where the current cannot jump.

BN: গতকাল তুমি সারণি বানিয়েছ। আজ সেটা যন্ত্রের মতো ব্যবহার করবে: রৈখিক সমীকরণ → বীজগণিত, আংশিক ভগ্নাংশ → সারণির পদ, আর সুইচ অন (ধাপ ফাংশন) → স্থানান্তর নিয়ম। পদার্থবিজ্ঞানে ইনডাক্টর + রোধ = RL বর্তনী, গত সপ্তাহের RC বর্তনীর আয়না, যেখানে কারেন্ট লাফ দিতে পারে না।

@section math | 2 · Math: Laplace for second-order equations and switches | 55 min

### The recipe
1. Transform every term (use L{y′} = sY − y (0), L{y″} = s²Y − s y (0) − y′ (0)).
2. Solve the algebra for Y (s).
3. Split Y into **partial fractions** that match the table.
4. Invert term by term.

BN: ধাপ: ১) প্রতিটা পদ ট্রান্সফর্ম; ২) Y এর জন্য বীজগণিত; ৩) আংশিক ভগ্নাংশে ভাঙো; ৪) সারণি থেকে উল্টো রূপান্তর।

EX: Real roots with a constant push
Solve y″ + 3y′ + 2y = 6 with y (0) = 0 and y′ (0) = 0.
1. **Transform:** s² Y + 3 s Y + 2 Y = 6 ÷ s, so Y = 6 ÷ (s (s + 1)(s + 2)).
2. **Partial fractions:** Y = A ÷ s + B ÷ (s + 1) + C ÷ (s + 2).
   - Cover-up at s = 0: A = 6 ÷ (1 × 2) = **3**.
   - At s = −1: B = 6 ÷ ((−1)(1)) = **−6**.
   - At s = −2: C = 6 ÷ ((−2)(−1)) = **3**.
3. **Invert:** y = **3 − 6 e^(−t) + 3 e^(−2t)**.
4. **Check:** y (0) = 3 − 6 + 3 = 0 ✓. y′ = 6 e^(−t) − 6 e^(−2t), and y′ (0) = 0 ✓. The e^(−t) terms in y″ + 3y′ + 2y: −6 + 18 − 12 = 0 ✓. The e^(−2t) terms: 12 − 18 + 6 = 0 ✓. The constant: 2 × 3 = 6 ✓.
5. **Read it:** y (1) = 3 − 6 e⁻¹ + 3 e⁻² = **1.199** and the long-run value is 3 = 6 ÷ 2.
/EX

EX: Complex roots and a repeated root
1. **y″ + 2y′ + 5y = 0, y (0) = 1, y′ (0) = 0.** Transform: s² Y − s + 2 (s Y − 1) + 5 Y = 0, so Y = (s + 2) ÷ (s² + 2s + 5). Complete the square: s² + 2s + 5 = (s + 1)² + 4. Then Y = ((s + 1) + 1) ÷ ((s + 1)² + 4) = (s + 1) ÷ ((s + 1)² + 4) + (1 ÷ 2) × 2 ÷ ((s + 1)² + 4). Invert (shift s → s + 1): **y = e^(−t) (cos 2t + ½ sin 2t)**. This is the same as Week 19, found without guessing. At t = 1: y = 0.0142.
2. **Repeated root:** Y = 1 ÷ (s + 2)² gives **t e^(−2t)**: the t factor comes from the double pole.
3. **A rule for poles:** the poles of Y (the roots of the denominator) decide the behaviour. A pole at −a gives e^(−at) (decay), a pole at −a ± ib gives a decaying oscillation, a pole at +a gives growth. All poles in the left half-plane means the system settles.
/EX

### Switching on at a time: the step function
The **unit step** u (t − a) is 0 before t = a and 1 after. Switching a source on at time a is a step. **Time shift rule:** L{u (t − a) f (t − a)} = **e^(−a s) F (s)**: delaying by a multiplies by e^(−as).

EX: Switch on at t = 2
Solve y′ + y = u (t − 2) with y (0) = 0.
1. **Transform:** (s + 1) Y = e^(−2s) ÷ s, so Y = e^(−2s) ÷ (s (s + 1)) = e^(−2s) (1 ÷ s − 1 ÷ (s + 1)).
2. **Invert** the bracket to get 1 − e^(−t) and shift it by 2: **y = u (t − 2) (1 − e^(−(t − 2)))**.
3. **Meaning:** nothing happens until t = 2, then y rises towards 1 with time constant 1. At t = 3: y = 1 − e⁻¹ = **0.632**.
/EX

BN: ধাপ ফাংশন u(t − a): a সময়ের আগে 0, পরে 1। সময় স্থানান্তর: L{u(t − a)f(t − a)} = e^(−as)F(s)। মেরু (pole) বাম অর্ধতলে হলে সিস্টেম স্থির হয়; জটিল মেরু = দোলনসহ ক্ষয়; দ্বিগুণ মেরু = t গুণক।

LAB: Two decaying exponentials | The inverse transform of 1 over ((s + a)(s + b)) is (e to the minus at minus e to the minus bt) over (b minus a). It rises, peaks and decays. Choose the two poles. The numbers give the time and the height of the peak. | a:First pole a:1:4:0.5:2 ;; b:Second pole b:5:9:0.5:6 | log(b/a)/(b-a):Time of the peak:3 ;; (exp(-a*log(b/a)/(b-a))-exp(-b*log(b/a)/(b-a)))/(b-a):Height of the peak:3 | (exp(-a*x)-exp(-b*x))/(b-a) | 0,3 | 0,0.4

TRAP: Do not forget to move the starting values to the right-hand side: L{y″} has the extra terms −s y (0) − y′ (0). In partial fractions the numerator must be one degree lower than the denominator. And completing the square: s² + 2s + 5 is (s + 1)² + 4, so the shift is s → s + 1 and the frequency is 2. Do not invert (s + 2) ÷ (s² + 2s + 5) by looking for a single table entry.

@section physics | 3 · Physics: the RL circuit | 35 min

### The current cannot jump
Close a switch connecting a battery ε, a resistor R and an inductor L in series. Kirchhoff's loop rule gives ε − I R − L dI/dt = 0. With the Laplace transform and I (0) = 0: (L s + R) I = ε ÷ s, so

EQ: I (s) = ε ÷ (s (L s + R)) = (ε ÷ R) (1 ÷ s − 1 ÷ (s + R ÷ L))

and therefore **I (t) = (ε ÷ R) (1 − e^(−t ÷ τ)), τ = L ÷ R**. The current rises from 0 to ε ÷ R, with time constant **τ = L ÷ R** (compare τ = R C). The voltage across the inductor is V_L = ε e^(−t ÷ τ): it is the full ε at the start (the inductor blocks the current completely at t = 0) and decays to zero (a steady current needs no voltage).

When the source is removed and the coil is shorted through R: **I = I₀ e^(−t ÷ τ)**.

BN: সুইচ বন্ধ করলে ε − IR − L dI/dt = 0। সমাধান I(t) = (ε/R)(1 − e^(−t/τ)), τ = L/R। শুরুতে ইনডাক্টরে পুরো ε (কারেন্ট শূন্য), পরে শূন্য। সুইচ খুলে শর্ট করলে I = I₀e^(−t/τ)। তুলনা: RC তে V লাফ দেয় না, RL এ I লাফ দেয় না।

EX: An RL circuit
ε = 12 V, R = 4 Ω and L = 0.2 H.
1. **Time constant:** τ = 0.2 ÷ 4 = **0.05 s**. **Final current:** 12 ÷ 4 = **3 A**.
2. **At t = τ:** I = 3 (1 − e⁻¹) = **1.90 A** (63% of the final value). V_L = 12 e⁻¹ = **4.41 V**.
3. **Time to reach 90%:** t = τ ln 10 = **0.115 s**.
4. **Half the final current:** t = τ ln 2 = **0.0347 s**.
5. **After 5τ** (0.25 s): I = 3 (1 − e⁻⁵) = **2.98 A**, so the circuit is effectively steady.
6. **Energy in the coil** at the end: ½ × 0.2 × 3² = **0.9 J**. The resistor then dissipates 3² × 4 = **36 W**, equal to the battery power 12 × 3 = 36 W.
7. **A voltage spike:** the coil carries 3 A and the switch is opened so that the current must flow through a 100 Ω resistor placed across the coil. At that moment the current is still 3 A, so the voltage is 3 × 100 = **300 V**, from a 12 V supply. It decays with τ = 0.2 ÷ 104 = **1.9 ms**.
/EX

TRAP: The inductor current is continuous, but the voltage across the inductor can jump (at switch-on it jumps to ε). In the RC circuit it was the opposite: the capacitor voltage is continuous and the current jumps. τ = L ÷ R, so a bigger inductance makes the current slower, and a bigger resistance makes it faster. Opening a switch through an inductor is dangerous because the voltage can reach L dI/dt.

NOTES:

### Recap
- Laplace method: transform, solve, partial fractions, invert. Poles decide the behaviour: left half-plane means decay, complex poles mean oscillation, double pole means t e^(−at). Step on at a: multiply by e^(−as).
- RL circuit: I = (ε ÷ R)(1 − e^(−t ÷ τ)), τ = L ÷ R. The current is continuous, V_L jumps. Switch-off through a resistor R′ gives a spike I₀ R′.
