# Eigenvalues and eigenvectors. Then: the second law and heat engines
lead: Most vectors are turned by a matrix, but a few special directions are only stretched or flipped. Those directions and their stretch factors, the eigenvectors and eigenvalues, reveal the true behaviour of the matrix: vibration frequencies, growth rates, stability. In physics, you meet the second law of thermodynamics. It says why a hot coffee never heats itself up, and why every engine must throw away heat.

BN: বেশিরভাগ ভেক্টরকে ম্যাট্রিক্স ঘুরিয়ে দেয়, কিন্তু কিছু বিশেষ দিক শুধু টানা বা উল্টে যায়। এই দিকগুলো ও তাদের গুণক (eigenvector ও eigenvalue) ম্যাট্রিক্সের আসল আচরণ দেখায়: কম্পনের কম্পাঙ্ক, বৃদ্ধির হার, স্থিতিশীলতা। পদার্থবিজ্ঞানে তাপগতিবিদ্যার দ্বিতীয় সূত্র পড়বে। এটা বলে কেন গরম কফি নিজে নিজে আরও গরম হয় না, আর কেন প্রতিটি ইঞ্জিনকে তাপ ফেলে দিতে হয়।

@section math | 2 · Math: eigenvalues and eigenvectors | 55 min

### The definition
A non-zero vector v is an **eigenvector** of the square matrix A, with **eigenvalue** λ, if

EQ: A v = λ v

The matrix only **stretches** v by the factor λ (and flips it if λ is negative): the direction of v is kept.

To find λ, rewrite as (A − λ I) v = 0. A non-zero v exists only when A − λ I is singular:

EQ: det (A − λ I) = 0     (the **characteristic equation**)     for a 2 × 2 matrix:     λ² − (trace A) λ + det A = 0

BN: A v = λ v হলে v হলো A এর eigenvector আর λ eigenvalue: ম্যাট্রিক্স v এর দিক বদলায় না, শুধু λ গুণ টানে। λ বের করতে det(A − λI) = 0 সমাধান করো। 2 × 2 ম্যাট্রিক্সে: λ² − (trace)λ + det = 0।

EX: A first example
1. A = [[2, 1], [1, 2]]. trace = 4, det = 3. So λ² − 4λ + 3 = 0, **λ = 1 and λ = 3**.
2. For λ = 3: A − 3I = [[−1, 1], [1, −1]], so −x + y = 0 and the eigenvector is **(1, 1)**. Check: A (1, 1) = (3, 3) = 3 (1, 1) ✓.
3. For λ = 1: A − I = [[1, 1], [1, 1]], so x + y = 0 and the eigenvector is **(1, −1)**. Check: A (1, −1) = (1, −1) ✓.
4. The matrix stretches the diagonal direction by 3 and leaves the other diagonal unchanged.
/EX

EX: A second example
1. A = [[4, 1], [2, 3]]. trace = 7, det = 10. λ² − 7λ + 10 = 0, **λ = 2 and 5**.
2. λ = 5: A − 5I = [[−1, 1], [2, −2]], eigenvector **(1, 1)**. Check: A (1, 1) = (5, 5) ✓.
3. λ = 2: A − 2I = [[2, 1], [2, 1]], so 2x + y = 0 and the eigenvector is **(1, −2)**. Check: A (1, −2) = (4 − 2, 2 − 6) = (2, −4) = 2 (1, −2) ✓.
/EX

PREDICT: A rotation by 90° turns every vector. Does it have any eigenvector? What do you think its eigenvalues are? => No real vector keeps its direction. The characteristic equation of [[0, −1], [1, 0]] is λ² + 1 = 0, so the eigenvalues are **λ = ±i**: complex numbers! Complex eigenvalues are how matrices describe rotation and oscillation.

### Useful facts
1. **Sum of eigenvalues = trace; product = determinant.** (Example 1: 1 + 3 = 4 ✓, 1 × 3 = 3 ✓.)
2. For a **triangular** matrix the eigenvalues are the diagonal entries: [[2, 5], [0, 3]] has λ = 2 and 3.
3. λ = 0 is an eigenvalue exactly when det A = 0 (A is singular).
4. If A has eigenvalue λ, then A² has λ², A⁻¹ has 1 ÷ λ, and A + c I has λ + c. For A = [[2, 1], [1, 2]]: A² = [[5, 4], [4, 5]] has trace 10 and det 9, so λ² − 10λ + 9 = 0 gives **1 and 9** ✓.
5. A shear [[1, 1], [0, 1]] has only the eigenvalue 1 with a single direction (1, 0): it cannot be fully described by eigenvectors.

EX: Repeated multiplication (power iteration)
1. Start with v₀ = (1, 0) and apply A = [[2, 1], [1, 2]] again and again: (2, 1), (5, 4), (14, 13), (41, 40). In general **Aⁿ (1, 0) = ((3ⁿ + 1) ÷ 2, (3ⁿ − 1) ÷ 2)**.
2. The vector swings round to the eigenvector direction (1, 1), because the λ = 3 component grows fastest, and each step multiplies the length by nearly 3.
3. This is how Google's PageRank finds the dominant eigenvector of the web's link matrix.
/EX

BN: eigenvalue এর যোগফল = trace, গুণফল = det। ত্রিকোণাকার ম্যাট্রিক্সে কর্ণের সংখ্যাগুলোই eigenvalue। A² এর eigenvalue λ², A⁻¹ এর 1/λ। বারবার A দিয়ে গুণ করলে ভেক্টর সবচেয়ে বড় eigenvalue এর eigenvector এর দিকে ঘুরে যায় (power iteration), Google PageRank এভাবেই কাজ করে।

TRAP: An eigenvector must be **non-zero** (the zero vector satisfies A v = λ v for every λ, and tells you nothing). Eigenvectors are only defined up to a multiple: (1, 1) and (5, 5) are the same direction. And do not forget to **check** A v = λ v with the numbers: it takes ten seconds.

@section physics | 3 · Physics: the second law and heat engines | 35 min

### What the first law does not say
Some things never happen, though energy conservation allows them: a cup of coffee never heats itself by cooling the room; a ball on the floor never gathers heat and bounces up; a gas that has expanded into a vacuum never rushes back by itself. All of them conserve energy. They are forbidden by the **second law of thermodynamics**, which gives the arrow of time. It has many equivalent statements:

- **Clausius:** heat never flows by itself from a colder to a hotter body. (Moving it requires work: a refrigerator.)
- **Kelvin–Planck:** no cyclic engine can turn heat from a single reservoir entirely into work. Some heat must be rejected.

BN: প্রথম সূত্র শক্তি সংরক্ষণ বলে, কিন্তু সব ঘটনা ঘটতে দেয় না: গরম কফি নিজে নিজে ঘর ঠান্ডা করে আরও গরম হয় না। এগুলো দ্বিতীয় সূত্রে নিষিদ্ধ। ক্লসিয়াস: তাপ নিজে নিজে ঠান্ডা থেকে গরমে যায় না। কেলভিন-প্লাঙ্ক: কোনো চক্রীয় ইঞ্জিন একটা উৎস থেকে নেওয়া তাপ পুরোটা কাজে বদলাতে পারে না।

### Heat engines
A **heat engine** takes heat Q_H from a hot reservoir, does work W, and rejects heat Q_C to a cold reservoir. Over a full cycle ΔU = 0, so by the first law W = Q_H − Q_C. The **thermal efficiency** is

EQ: e = W ÷ Q_H = 1 − Q_C ÷ Q_H

| Engine | Typical efficiency |
| Human muscle | 20 to 25% |
| Petrol (gasoline) car engine | 25 to 30% |
| Diesel engine | 35 to 45% |
| Steam power plant | 35 to 45% |
| Combined-cycle gas plant | about 60% |
| Electric motor (not a heat engine) | 90% or more |

EX: Engines
1. **Car engine:** Q_H = 3000 J of fuel heat per cycle and W = 750 J: e = **25%**, Q_C = 2250 J rejected through the exhaust and radiator.
2. **Power plant:** 500 MW electrical output at 38% efficiency: Q_H = 500 ÷ 0.38 = **1316 MW** of fuel heat and **816 MW** of waste heat. If the cooling river water warms by 10 K, the flow needed is 816 × 10⁶ ÷ (4186 × 10) = **19,500 kg/s ≈ 19.5 m³/s**.
3. **Car:** to deliver 50 kW to the wheels at 25%, the fuel supplies 200 kW. Petrol has 46 MJ/kg: 200,000 ÷ 46 × 10⁶ = 4.35 g/s, about **15.6 kg per hour (21 L/h)**.
4. **A cyclist** delivers 100 W of mechanical power at 25%: the body must burn 400 W, of which **300 W** becomes heat (which is why you sweat).
/EX

### Refrigerators and heat pumps
Run an engine backwards: with work W put in, a **refrigerator** removes Q_C from the cold space and dumps Q_H = Q_C + W into the hot space. The **coefficient of performance** is

EQ: COP (refrigerator) = Q_C ÷ W     COP (heat pump) = Q_H ÷ W = COP (refrigerator) + 1

EX: Fridges and heat pumps
1. A fridge with COP 3 removes 300 W of heat from inside: it needs W = 100 W of electricity and rejects **400 W** into the kitchen (so an open fridge door heats the room).
2. A heat pump with a heating COP of 4 delivers 4 kW of heat for each 1 kW of electricity: 3 kW comes from the cold outside air. Direct electric heating has a COP of only 1.
3. A device claiming Q_H = 1000 J and W = 1200 J would be **impossible** by the first law alone (W cannot exceed Q_H in a cycle).
/EX

TRAP: Efficiency is **work out divided by heat in**, not heat out. Do not confuse the efficiency of an engine (always below 1) with the COP of a refrigerator (which can be above 1, because it **moves** heat rather than creating it). And never let Q_C be zero: the second law forbids it.

LVL: Challenge | Clausius and Kelvin–Planck are equivalent

The cold reservoir loses Q to the device and gains Q from the engine: **no net change**. The hot reservoir gives Q_H to the engine and receives Q from the device: net loss **Q_H − Q = W**. The combined machine takes heat only from the hot reservoir and converts it **completely into work**: a violation of Kelvin–Planck. So the two statements stand or fall together.

NOTES:

### Recap
- Eigenvectors A v = λ v: directions that are only stretched. Solve det (A − λ I) = 0, i.e. λ² − (trace) λ + det = 0 for 2 × 2. Sum of eigenvalues = trace, product = det, λ = 0 means singular. A² has λ², A⁻¹ has 1/λ. Rotation has complex eigenvalues ±i. Power iteration finds the dominant eigenvector.
- Second law: Clausius, Kelvin–Planck. Heat engine W = Q_H − Q_C, e = W ÷ Q_H. Refrigerator COP = Q_C ÷ W, heat pump COP = Q_H ÷ W. Real plants waste 55 to 65% of the fuel heat.

BN: আজকের সারকথা: Av = λv; det(A − λI) = 0; eigenvalue এর যোগফল = trace, গুণফল = det; ঘূর্ণনের eigenvalue ±i। দ্বিতীয় সূত্র (ক্লসিয়াস, কেলভিন-প্লাঙ্ক): ইঞ্জিনে W = Q_H − Q_C, e = W/Q_H; ফ্রিজের COP = Q_C/W, হিট পাম্পের COP = Q_H/W।
